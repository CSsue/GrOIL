General Provisions 
Entire Contract  
This Policy, together with the attached application, any supplemental applications, riders, endorsements, and 
amendments issued by the Company, constitutes the entire contract. All statements made by the Insured or the 
Policy Owner shall, in the absence of fraud, be deemed representations and not warranties. No statement shall be 
used in defense of a claim under this Policy unless it is contained in the written application and a copy of such 
application is attached to the Policy at issue.  
Modifications  
No change or modification to this Policy shall be valid unless approved in writing by an executive officer of the 
Company. No agent, producer, or field representative has authority to alter or waive any provision of this Policy or 
to extend the time for payment of premiums. Any statutory or regulatory change mandated by governing law shall 
be incorporated into this Policy automatically as required, with or without notice to the Policy Owner.  
Misstatement of Age or Sex  
If the age or sex of the Insured has been misstated, the amount of the death benefit payable under this Policy 
shall be the amount the premium paid would have purchased at the correct age and sex. If the correction results in 
an overpayment of premiums, the Company shall refund the excess to the Policy Owner without interest. If the 
correction results in an underpayment of premiums, the death benefit shall be reduced accordingly. If the Insured 
is found to be outside of an insurable age range at issue, the Policy shall be void ab initio and all premiums shall 
be refunded.  
Assignment  
The Policy Owner may assign this Policy or any rights under it by written notice satisfactory to the Company. The 
assignment shall take effect upon the Company’s receipt and recording of such notice. The Company assumes no 
responsibility for the validity, sufficiency, or tax consequences of any assignment. Unless otherwise agreed in 
writing by an irrevocable beneficiary, the rights of the assignee are subordinate to those of any such beneficiary. 
An assignment may limit or preclude the exercise of certain rights under the Policy.  
Policy Owner Rights  
While the Insured is living, and unless otherwise restricted by an assignment or irrevocable beneficiary 
designation, the Policy Owner may exercise all contractual rights without the consent of any other person. 
These rights include, but are not limited to: changing the Beneficiary, assigning the Policy, electing optional 
forms of benefits, and requesting policy information. Actions of the Policy Owner shall be binding on all parties 
with an interest in the Policy, subject to applicable law and the terms herein. 
Nonparticipating  
This Policy is nonparticipating and shall not share in any surplus or earnings of the Company. No dividends are 
payable to the Policy Owner now or in the future. Premium rates are based on the underwriting class of the 
Insured and the Company’s pricing standards at the time of issue.

Premiums and Grace Period  
Premium Payments  
Premiums are payable in advance on or before the due date shown in the Policy 
Specifications. The Policy Owner may change the payment mode with the 
Company’s approval. Premiums must be paid to the Company at its Administrative 
Office or to an authorized agent in exchange for an official receipt.  
Grace Period  
A grace period of 31 days will be allowed for the payment of each premium after the first. 
During this period, the Policy shall remain in force. If the Insured dies during the grace period, 
any unpaid premium due will be deducted from the death benefit.  
Lapse for Nonpayment  
If any premium is not paid before the end of the grace period, the Policy will lapse as of 
the due date of the unpaid premium, and coverage will terminate. No benefits shall be 
payable under a lapsed Policy, except as provided in the Reinstatement provision.  
Reinstatement Rights  
A lapsed Policy may be reinstated within five years from the date of lapse if the following 
conditions are met: 1) written request for reinstatement is received, 2) evidence of insurability 
is submitted and accepted, and 3) all overdue premiums are paid with interest at the rate 
specified by the Company at the time of reinstatement. Upon reinstatement, coverage will 
resume as though the Policy had remained continuously in force, subject to the 
Incontestability and Suicide Exclusion provisions starting anew from the date of 
reinstatement.

Death Benefit  
Benefit Payable Upon Death  
If the Insured dies while this Policy is in force and before the Expiry Date, the Company will 
pay the death benefit to the designated Beneficiary, subject to all terms and conditions herein. 
The death benefit will be equal to the Face Amount shown in the Policy Specifications, less 
any unpaid premiums or indebtedness.  
Proof of Death  
Before any death benefit is payable, the Company must receive due proof of the Insured’s 
death, including a certified death certificate and any additional documentation the 
Company reasonably requires. The Company reserves the right to investigate the cause 
and circumstances of death and to contest the claim if within the contestability period.  
Payment of Proceeds  
Death benefit proceeds will be paid in a lump sum unless an alternative payment option is 
elected by the Policy Owner during the Insured's lifetime or by the Beneficiary after the 
Insured’s death. Payment shall be made promptly upon receipt and approval of all required 
claim documentation.  
Interest on Death Benefit  
Interest shall accrue on the death benefit from the date of death to the date of payment, at 
a rate not less than required by applicable state law.  
Contingent Beneficiaries and Simultaneous Death  
If no designated Beneficiary survives the Insured, proceeds will be paid to the 
Contingent Beneficiary, if any. If no Contingent Beneficiary survives, proceeds will be 
payable to the Policy Owner’s estate. In the event the Insured and the Beneficiary die 
simultaneously or  
under circumstances rendering the order of death uncertain, the Company shall assume 
the Beneficiary predeceased the Insured unless otherwise governed by state law.

Beneficiary Provisions  
Designation of Beneficiary  
The Beneficiary is the person or persons designated by the Policy Owner to receive the death 
benefit upon the death of the Insured. The designation must be made in writing and filed with 
the Company. If multiple Beneficiaries are named, and no distribution percentages are 
specified, proceeds will be paid in equal shares.  
Change of Beneficiary  
Unless an irrevocable Beneficiary has been designated, the Policy Owner may change the 
Beneficiary at any time during the lifetime of the Insured by written request satisfactory to the 
Company. A change will take effect as of the date the request is signed, regardless of 
whether the Insured is alive at the time the change is recorded by the Company.  
Irrevocable Beneficiary  
If the Policy Owner names an irrevocable Beneficiary, the Policy Owner may not exercise 
certain rights under the Policy—including changing the Beneficiary or assigning the 
Policy—without the written consent of the irrevocable Beneficiary.  
Simultaneous Death and Disclaimer  
If the Insured and the Beneficiary die under circumstances that make the order of death 
uncertain, the Beneficiary shall be presumed to have predeceased the Insured. A 
Beneficiary who disclaims proceeds shall be treated as having predeceased the Insured.  
Facility of Payment  
If no Beneficiary survives the Insured, or if a Beneficiary cannot be located within 90 days 
of the Insured’s death, the Company may pay the proceeds to the Insured’s estate or to any 
person deemed by the Company to be equitably entitled, including one who incurred 
expenses related to the Insured’s last illness or burial. Such payment shall discharge the 
Company’s liability to the extent of the amount paid.

Contestability and Suicide Clause  
Incontestability  
This Policy shall be incontestable after it has been in force during the lifetime of the Insured 
for a period of two (2) years from the Issue Date, except for nonpayment of premiums. During 
the two-year contestability period, the Company may rescind the Policy or deny a claim if 
there was a material misrepresentation in the application. After the contestability period 
expires, the Company may not contest the validity of the Policy, even in the event of 
misstatement, unless such misstatement constitutes fraud as defined by applicable state law.  
Contestability After Reinstatement  
If the Policy is reinstated, a new two-year contestability period shall apply from the date 
of reinstatement, but only with respect to statements made in the reinstatement 
application.  
Suicide Exclusion  
If the Insured dies by suicide, while sane or insane, within two (2) years from the Issue Date 
(or from the date of reinstatement, if applicable), the Company’s liability shall be limited to 
an amount equal to the total premiums paid, without interest. After two years, the full death 
benefit is payable regardless of cause of death, subject to all other terms of this Policy.

Reinstatement and Continuation  
Right to Reinstate  
If this Policy has lapsed due to nonpayment of premiums, it may be reinstated within five (5) 
years from the date of lapse, provided the following conditions are met: 1) The Policy Owner 
submits a written application for reinstatement; 2) Satisfactory evidence of the Insured’s 
insurability is submitted; and 3) All overdue premiums are paid with interest at a rate 
determined by the Company, compounded annually.  
Effect of Reinstatement  
Upon reinstatement, the Policy shall be deemed to be in full force as if it had never lapsed, 
subject to the following: 1) A new two-year contestability period shall begin as of the date of 
reinstatement; 2) The suicide exclusion shall apply anew from the date of reinstatement; 3)  
The Company shall not be liable for any claim arising from an event occurring during 
the period in which the Policy was lapsed.  
Extension or Conversion at Expiry  
This Policy does not provide automatic renewal beyond the term period. However, prior to the 
Expiry Date, the Policy Owner may be eligible to convert this Policy to a permanent life 
insurance policy offered by the Company without evidence of insurability, provided: 1) The 
request is made during the conversion period specified in the Policy Specifications; 2) The 
Insured is not older than the maximum conversion age established by the Company at the 
time of request; and 3) Premiums for the new policy are paid in accordance with the terms of 
the selected product.
