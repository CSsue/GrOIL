2. ACCIDENTAL DEATH BENEFIT RIDER (ADB-22)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2.1  If the Insured's death is caused directly and exclusively by 
     accidental bodily injury, the Rider pays an ADDITIONAL $20,000 
     on top of the Base Face Amount, for a total of $40,000.

2.2  ACCIDENTAL DEATH DEFINED: Death must (a) result from a 
     sudden, unforeseen, external event; (b) occur within 120 days 
     of the injury; and (c) not be contributed to by sickness, 
     disease, or any infection (except infection of an accidental 
     wound).

2.3  RIDER EXCLUSIONS — The ADB rider does NOT pay the additional 
     benefit if death results from:
       (a) Self-inflicted injury;
       (b) Driving under the influence of alcohol (BAC ≥ 0.08%);
       (c) Commission of a felony;
       (d) Participation in a riot;
       (e) Aviation (except as a fare-paying passenger on a 
           scheduled commercial airline).

2.4  The ADB rider terminates on the Policy Anniversary following 
     the Insured's 85th birthday. Base policy coverage continues.

2.5  The ADB rider may be removed by the Owner at any time, 
     reducing the monthly premium accordingly.
