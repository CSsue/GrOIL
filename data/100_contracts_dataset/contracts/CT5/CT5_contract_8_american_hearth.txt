1. FRATERNAL BENEFIT SOCIETY — YOUR MEMBERSHIP
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1  The Order of the American Hearth is a fraternal benefit 
     society organized under Chapter 632 of the Ohio Revised 
     Code and recognized in all states where it operates.

1.2  As a member, you have the right and responsibility to 
     participate in the Order's activities, including:
       (a) Voting for Lodge #47 officers annually;
       (b) Participation in Lodge charitable and social programs;
       (c) Access to member-only benefits described in Section 7.

1.3  MEMBER GOOD STANDING REQUIREMENT: This certificate remains 
     in force only while the Member is in good standing with 
     Lodge #47. Good standing requires: (a) payment of all 
     fees; and (b) compliance with the Laws of the Order. 
     Suspension or expulsion from the Order terminates this 
     certificate (see Section 8).

1.4  Non-forfeiture benefits (Section 6) remain available even 
     if the Member's lodge standing lapses after Year 3, so 
     long as fees are current.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

     health questions were required for membership.

2.2  GRADED DEATH BENEFIT:

     YEAR 1 (before July 1, 2025):
       Non-accidental death: All fees paid returned + 5% interest
       Accidental death:     $10,000 (full benefit)

     circumstances such as pandemic or catastrophic loss events, 
     invoke the ASSESSMENT PROVISION (see Section 3) in lieu of 
     the standard benefit. This provision has not been invoked 
     in 47 years and is reserved for extreme circumstances.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. ASSESSMENT PROVISION (FRATERNAL RESERVE MECHANISM)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1  The Order of the American Hearth maintains actuarially 
     adequate reserves to pay all benefits in full. However, 
     as required by fraternal benefit society law, this 
     certificate discloses that:

     If the Order's reserves fall below 90% of minimum statutory 
     requirements, the Supreme Lodge Board may, with regulatory 
     approval, impose a special assessment on all members, 
     not to exceed 3 additional monthly fee payments per year, 
     to restore reserve adequacy.

3.2  In the alternative to assessment, the Supreme Lodge may 
     proportionally reduce benefits pending reserve restoration.

3.3  HISTORICAL NOTE: The Order has maintained reserves above 
     120% of statutory minimums continuously since 1952. This 
     provision is disclosed as required by law, not as an 
     expected event.

7. MEMBER BENEFITS (NON-INSURANCE)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
As a member of the Order of the American Hearth, you also receive:

7.1  LODGE SOCIAL PROGRAMS: Access to Lodge #47 weekly social 
     gatherings, holiday events, and community service activities.

7.2  MEMBER ASSISTANCE FUND: Emergency financial assistance 
     of up to $500 per calendar year, subject to Lodge #47 
     Board approval, for members experiencing hardship.

7.3  SCHOLARSHIP ACCESS: Eligible grandchildren of members 
     may apply to the Order's annual scholarship program 
     ($1,500 awards, competitive).

7.4  CHAPLAINCY SERVICES: Access to the Order's chaplaincy 
     program for hospital visits and pastoral care.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. FORFEITURE OF MEMBERSHIP
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1  A Member who is expelled from the Order for cause 
     (conduct unbecoming, as defined in the Laws of the Order) 
     forfeits all insurance and non-insurance benefits.

8.2  EXCEPTION: If a Member has paid fees for 3 or more years 
     and is subsequently expelled, the Member retains the 
     non-insurance right to receive the net reserve value in 
     cash (surrender benefit). The death benefit certificate 
     terminates upon expulsion regardless of duration.

8.3  Appeal rights are governed by the Laws of the Order, 
     including a right to petition the Supreme Lodge.
