1. MUTUAL COMPANY MEMBERSHIP
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
As a policyholder of Piedmont Mutual Life Insurance Society, the 
Owner is a member of the Society. Membership confers:

  (a) The right to vote for Board of Trustees at annual meetings 
      (one vote per policy, regardless of face amount);
  (b) Eligibility to receive dividends, if declared;
  (c) Access to member financial statements upon written request.

Membership rights cannot be transferred separately from this policy.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. DEATH BENEFIT — PARTICIPATING STRUCTURE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1  IMMEDIATE DEATH BENEFIT: The full Face Amount of $12,000 is 
     payable upon the Insured's death from any cause (subject to 
     Section 6 exclusions) at any time from the date of issue.
     
     This policy does NOT have a graded benefit period. Issuance 
     is based on satisfactory answers to health screening questions.

2.2  DIVIDEND ADDITIONS: If dividends are applied to purchase 
     Paid-Up Additions (see Section 4), the total death benefit 
     will exceed the Base Face Amount over time.

2.3  BENEFIT PAYMENT OPTIONS: The beneficiary may elect to receive 
     the death benefit as:
       (a) Lump sum (default);
       (b) 12 equal monthly installments; or
       (c) Held at interest (minimum 3.0% annually) for up to 
           5 years, with beneficiary retaining withdrawal rights.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. DIVIDEND PROVISIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1  This policy is eligible to participate in the Society's 
     divisible surplus. Dividends are NOT guaranteed. The Board 
     of Trustees declares dividends annually based on the Society's 
     actual mortality, investment, and expense experience.

3.2  Current illustrated (not guaranteed) annual dividend at Year 5: 
     approximately $180. Actual results may be higher or lower.

3.3  DIVIDEND OPTIONS — Owner elects one of the following:

     Option 1 — PAID-UP ADDITIONS (default):
       Dividends purchase additional paid-up whole life insurance, 
       increasing both death benefit and cash value over time.

     Option 2 — PREMIUM REDUCTION:
       Dividends are applied to reduce the next premium due.

     Option 3 — CASH PAYMENT:
       Dividends are paid in cash to the Owner annually.

     Option 4 — ACCUMULATE AT INTEREST:
       Dividends are held by the Society at a rate not less than 
       3.5% per annum, withdrawable at any time.

     The Owner may change the dividend option once per year.

4.2  POLICY LOANS: Available at 5.5% annual interest rate,

     loans reduce the death benefit dollar-for-dollar.

     of grace period, and sufficient cash value exists, the Company 
     will automatically make a policy loan to pay the premium. The 
     Owner may decline APL in writing.

6. EXCLUSIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1  SUICIDE: If the Insured dies by suicide within 2 years of the 
     Policy Date, the death benefit is limited to a return of 
     premiums paid to the Society, without interest.

6.2  CONTESTABILITY: The Society may contest this policy on grounds 
     of material misrepresentation within 2 years of the Policy 
     Date. This policy is incontestable thereafter.

6.3  There are no exclusions for war, aviation, hazardous activity, 
     or travel outside the United States.

7. NONFORFEITURE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
After cash value exists, if premiums lapse beyond the grace period:

  Option A — EXTENDED TERM INSURANCE:
    Cash value purchases term insurance at the full Face Amount

8. REINSTATEMENT
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  (a) Providing evidence of insurability satisfactory to the Society;
  (b) Paying all overdue premiums plus 6% annual interest; and
  (c) Repaying any outstanding policy loan balance.
