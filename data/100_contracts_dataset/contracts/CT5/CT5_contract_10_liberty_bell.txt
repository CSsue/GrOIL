1. LEVEL-THEN-INCREASING BENEFIT DESIGN
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy features a unique INCREASING BENEFIT design after 
the modified benefit period ends:

  PHASE 1 — MODIFIED BENEFIT (Years 1–2):
    Non-accidental death: Return of premiums paid (no interest)
    Accidental death:     $7,500 (full benefit)

  PHASE 2 — FULL LEVEL BENEFIT (Years 3–5):
    All deaths:           $7,500

  PHASE 3 — INCREASING BENEFIT (Year 6 and beyond):
    Beginning on November 1, 2029 (6th Policy Anniversary), 
    the face amount INCREASES by $150 per year on each 
    Policy Anniversary, up to a maximum of $10,000.

    Example increasing schedule (not guaranteed — illustrates 
    the $150/year increase feature):
      Year 6:  $7,650
      Year 7:  $7,800
      Year 10: $8,250
      Year 16: $9,150
      Year 17: $9,300 (approximate maximum reached)

    IMPORTANT: The premium remains at $79.00 per month 
    throughout, even as the benefit increases. The increasing 
    benefit is funded through the policy's reserve growth.

    The increasing benefit cannot exceed $10,000 under any 
    circumstances. Once $10,000 is reached, the benefit is 
    level at $10,000 thereafter.

1.1  THE INCREASING BENEFIT IS GUARANTEED once Phase 3 begins — 
     it is not dividend-dependent and not subject to the 
     Company's discretion. It is a contractual guarantee.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. EXCLUSIONS — ENUMERATED EXCLUSION REGIME
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy has a broader exclusion set than most final expense 
products, reflecting the guaranteed-issue underwriting basis.

3.1  SUICIDE: If the Insured dies by suicide within 24 months 
     of the Policy Date — whether sane or insane — the benefit 
     is limited to return of premiums paid, without interest. 
     After 24 months, suicide is covered.

3.2  INTOXICATION: If the Insured's death is caused by, or 
     the Insured contributed to their own death through, 
     voluntary intoxication (blood alcohol concentration 
     ≥ 0.10%), the benefit during Years 1–2 is limited to 
     return of premiums. In Year 3+, intoxication alone 
     does NOT reduce the benefit — only if intoxication 
     is the DIRECT cause of death.

3.3  FELONY EXCLUSION: If the Insured's death occurs while 
     the Insured is committing or attempting to commit a 
     felony, the benefit during Years 1–2 is limited to 
     return of premiums. In Year 3+, this exclusion expires 
     and the full benefit applies regardless.

3.4  WAR EXCLUSION: If the Insured's death results from 
     an act of war (declared or undeclared), armed conflict, 
     insurrection, or civil war in which the Insured is an 
     active participant (as a combatant, not as a civilian 
     bystander), the benefit is limited to return of premiums 
     paid. This exclusion applies in ALL policy years and 
     does NOT expire.

     NOTE: This war exclusion does NOT apply to civilian 
     bystanders or victims of war-related events. It applies 
     only if the Insured is an active combatant.

3.5  CONTESTABILITY: This Guaranteed Issue policy has no 
     health representations, so the standard contestability 
     provision is modified: the Company may only contest 
     this policy for FRAUD (identity fraud or age fraud) 
     within 24 months. Age misstatement is corrected by 
     adjusting the face amount (not voiding the policy).
