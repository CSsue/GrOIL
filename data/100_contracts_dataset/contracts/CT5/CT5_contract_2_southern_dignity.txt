════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. POLICY PURPOSE AND PRENEED COMPATIBILITY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This policy is designed to fund prearranged funeral and burial 
services. The Owner (Magnolia Funeral Services) and the Insured 
have entered into a separately executed Preneed Funeral Contract, 
which specifies the funeral goods and services to be provided 
upon the Insured's death.

This life insurance policy serves as the funding instrument for 
that Preneed Funeral Contract. The assignment of death benefit 
to the funeral home (Section 3) is integral to this arrangement.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. DEATH BENEFIT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1  IMMEDIATE FULL BENEFIT: This policy is issued on a simplified 
     issue basis with confirmed health status. The FULL Face Amount 
     of $8,500 is payable from the date of issue — there is NO 
     graded benefit period.

     Health questions were answered at the time of application.
     Issuance of this policy constitutes acceptance of the health 
     representations made therein.

2.2  The death benefit shall not decrease during the life of this 
     policy as long as premiums are paid when due.

2.3  INFLATION BENEFIT OPTION: If the Insured's preneed funeral 
     contract is updated to reflect increased funeral costs, the 
     Owner may apply to increase the Face Amount in $500 increments 
     (up to $12,000 total) by submitting evidence of continued 
     insurability. Increased amount requires underwriting approval.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. FUNERAL SERVICE ASSIGNMENT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1  The Insured and Owner have irrevocably assigned the death 
     benefit of this policy to Magnolia Funeral Services, LLC, 
     to the extent of the actual cost of contracted funeral goods 
     and services at the time of death.

3.2  Any death benefit proceeds EXCEEDING the cost of funeral 
     services shall be paid to the Contingent Beneficiary 
     (Gloria Ann Beaumont) within 5 business days of the funeral 
     home's final accounting.

3.3  If the contracted funeral home ceases operations, the 
     assignment becomes void and the Contingent Beneficiary 
     becomes the sole beneficiary.

3.4  The Insured retains the right to transfer the preneed 
     contract (and assignment) to a different licensed funeral 
     home upon 30 days written notice to the Company.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. DEBIT PREMIUM COLLECTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1  Premiums are collected weekly by a licensed Debit Agent 
     assigned to the Insured's service area.

4.2  If the Insured is unavailable on the scheduled collection 
     date, the Insured may pay up to 4 weeks in advance. A 
     premium receipt book is provided for all payments.

4.3  GRACE PERIOD: 28 days from the due date of any missed weekly 
     premium. The policy remains in force during the grace period.

4.4  If collection cannot be made after 2 consecutive weekly 
     attempts, the Debit Agent will leave a written notice. The 
     Insured may also pay at the Company's local service office.

4.5  The Owner may elect to convert to monthly EFT collection 
     at any time without fee.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. CASH VALUE AND LOAN PROVISIONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1  Cash values accrue beginning in Policy Year 2.

5.2  ILLUSTRATIVE GUARANTEED VALUES:
       End of Year 2:   $380
       End of Year 5:   $1,450
       End of Year 10:  $3,600

5.3  Because this policy is assigned to a funeral home, policy 
     loans require written consent of the assignee (Magnolia 
     Funeral Services) during the term of the assignment.

5.4  SURRENDER: If the preneed contract and assignment are 
     cancelled by mutual written agreement of all parties, the 
     Owner may surrender this policy for its net cash value.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. NONFORFEITURE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1  After Policy Year 2, if premiums lapse:

     AUTOMATIC OPTION: Reduced Paid-Up whole life insurance for 
     a face amount proportional to cash value — the assignment 
     to the funeral home remains in effect for the reduced amount.

6.2  If the Insured wishes to reinstate: all overdue premiums plus 
     interest at 5% per annum must be paid within 3 years of lapse.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. EXCLUSIONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1  SUICIDE: Death by suicide within 24 months of issue — only 
     premiums paid are returned (no interest).

7.2  CONTESTABILITY: Material misrepresentation in the health 
     questions allows the Company to contest within 24 months.
     After 24 months: incontestable except for non-payment.

7.3  This policy contains NO exclusion for war, military service, 
     aviation as a passenger, or foreign travel.
