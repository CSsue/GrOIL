CRITICAL TAX DISCLOSURE — MODIFIED ENDOWMENT CONTRACT (MEC)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
THIS POLICY IS A MODIFIED ENDOWMENT CONTRACT (MEC) under 
Internal Revenue Code Section 7702A.

Because this is a single-premium policy, it fails the "7-pay 
test" under the IRC and is therefore classified as a MEC.

MEC TAX CONSEQUENCES — Please consult a tax advisor:

  (1) LOANS AND WITHDRAWALS from a MEC are taxable as ordinary 
      income to the extent of gain in the policy, BEFORE 
      recovery of basis (unlike non-MEC policies).

  (2) A 10% PENALTY TAX may apply to taxable distributions 
      from a MEC taken before the Owner reaches age 59½.

  (3) DEATH BENEFIT: The death benefit paid to beneficiaries 
      is generally INCOME TAX-FREE under IRC §101(a), 
      regardless of MEC status. The MEC classification does 
      NOT affect the tax-free treatment of death proceeds.

  (4) This MEC status is PERMANENT and cannot be changed.

The Company recommends the Owner consult a qualified tax 
advisor before taking any loan or withdrawal from this policy.
