2. DEATH BENEFIT STRUCTURE — THREE-TIER SCHEDULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Because this policy was issued on a simplified-issue basis, the 
following tiered death benefit applies:

  TIER 1 — Policy Months 1 through 12:
    Benefit = 50% of Face Amount ($7,500)
    Plus: Total premiums paid returned to beneficiary

  TIER 2 — Policy Months 13 through 24:
    Benefit = 75% of Face Amount ($11,250)
    Plus: Total premiums paid returned to beneficiary

  TIER 3 — Policy Month 25 and thereafter:
    Benefit = 100% of Face Amount ($15,000)
    No additional premium return — full face amount paid

  ACCIDENTAL DEATH OVERRIDE:
    If the Insured's death results directly and independently of all 
    other causes from an accidental bodily injury (as defined in 
    Section 9), the Tier 3 benefit of $15,000 is payable from the 
    Policy Date, regardless of which Tier would otherwise apply.

    The accidental death must occur within 180 days of the injury.

3. RETURN OF PREMIUM RIDER (ROP-7)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Rider Form: HL-ROP-7-2022

3.1  If the Insured survives to the 7th Policy Anniversary (March 15, 
     2031), the Company will pay the Owner a one-time Return of 
     Premium Benefit equal to 50% of all base policy premiums paid 
     to that date.

3.2  This rider benefit does not reduce the Face Amount or cash value.

3.3  Rider benefit is paid only once. If the policy lapses before the 
     7th Anniversary, no ROP benefit is paid.

3.4  Monthly rider premium included in the $143.20 modal premium 
     shown above (rider cost: $9.40/month).

After the policy has a cash value (Year 3+), if the Owner stops 
paying premiums, the Owner may elect:

  Option A — EXTENDED TERM: Applies the net cash value as a single 
  premium to purchase term insurance for the full Face Amount for 
  the longest period possible.

  Option B — REDUCED PAID-UP: Converts to a fully paid-up whole 
  life policy for a reduced face amount, with no further premiums.

  DEFAULT: If no election is made, Option B (Reduced Paid-Up) 
  applies automatically.
