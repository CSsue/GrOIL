══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. HOW JOINT FIRST-TO-DIE WORKS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1  This is a SINGLE POLICY covering TWO lives. The $12,000 
     death benefit is paid ONCE — upon the FIRST death of either 
     Insured A or Insured B, whichever occurs first.

1.2  After the first death claim is paid, this policy terminates. 
     The surviving spouse receives the $12,000 benefit and the 
     policy ends. No further premiums are collected after the 
     first death.

1.3  SURVIVORSHIP PROVISION: If both Insureds die within 30 days 
     of each other (e.g., a shared accident), the Company will 
     treat the elder Insured (Thaddeus) as having died first 
     and pay $12,000 to the Contingent Beneficiary.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. DEATH BENEFIT — GRADED SCHEDULE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Because this policy is issued on a simplified-issue basis, the 
following graded benefit applies based on the cause of death:

  POLICY YEAR 1 (before February 14, 2025):
    Death from Illness/Disease:    $4,800 (40%) + premiums paid
    Death from Accident:           $12,000 (full benefit)

  POLICY YEAR 2 (February 14, 2025 – February 13, 2026):
    Death from Illness/Disease:    $8,400 (70%) + premiums paid
    Death from Accident:           $12,000 (full benefit)

  POLICY YEAR 3+ (February 14, 2026 and thereafter):
    Death from any cause:          $12,000 (full benefit)

2.1  "Accident" means a sudden, unforeseen, external event 
     causing bodily injury that leads to death within 90 days, 
     independent of illness or disease.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. SURVIVOR CONVERSION OPTION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1  If the first Insured to die passes away in Policy Year 3 or 
     later (after the graded period), the surviving Insured has 
     a ONE-TIME OPTION to purchase a new individual final expense 
     policy within 90 days of the first death claim payment.

3.2  The new individual policy:
       (a) May have a face amount up to $12,000;
       (b) Requires NO new evidence of insurability or health 
           questions (guaranteed conversion right);
       (c) Is priced at the surviving Insured's attained age at 
           the time of conversion;
       (d) Will be a standard guaranteed-issue final expense 
           policy subject to a new 2-year graded benefit period.

3.3  The surviving Insured must be age 85 or younger at the time 
     of conversion for the conversion right to apply.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. PREMIUM PROVISIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1  The joint monthly premium of $198.40 reflects both lives 
     and will never increase as long as the policy is in force.

4.2  Premiums are the joint obligation of both Owners (both 
     Insureds). Either Owner may make premium payments.

4.3  GRACE PERIOD: 31 days from due date. Coverage on both 
     lives continues during the grace period.

4.4  DIVORCE PROVISION: If the Insureds legally divorce, either 
     Owner may request a policy split. The Company will, within 
     60 days of written notice and divorce decree, offer each 
     former spouse an individual policy at their respective 
     attained ages with no new health questions, and face 
     amounts totaling no more than $12,000 combined. The 
     original joint policy then terminates.

4.5  ANNUAL PAYMENT: $2,341.20 (pay 11 months, get 1 free).

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. CASH VALUE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1  This joint policy accumulates a single cash value (not 
     separate values per life). Guaranteed cash values:

       End of Year 2:   $1,200
       End of Year 5:   $3,800
       End of Year 10:  $7,500

5.2  LOANS: Either Owner may request a policy loan of up to 
     85% of net cash value at 6.0% annual interest.

5.3  SURRENDER: Either Owner may surrender the policy with the 
     other Owner's written consent. The net cash value is paid 
     equally to both Owners unless they direct otherwise.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. NONFORFEITURE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
If premiums lapse after the policy has cash value:
  Default: REDUCED PAID-UP. Both lives remain insured at a 
  reduced joint face amount with no further premiums.

  Alternative: EXTENDED TERM. Both lives covered for full 
  $12,000 for a limited additional period.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. EXCLUSIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1  SUICIDE: If either Insured dies by suicide within 24 months 
     of the Policy Date, only premiums paid are returned. If the 
     suicide exclusion applies, the policy continues in force on 
     the surviving Insured's life until that exclusion period 
     has also passed.

7.2  SIMULTANEOUS DEATH: Handled per Section 1.3.

7.3  CONTESTABILITY: 24 months from Policy Date for either life. 
     After 24 months, the policy is incontestable except for 
     non-payment of premium.

7.4  No exclusions for war, aviation, or travel.
