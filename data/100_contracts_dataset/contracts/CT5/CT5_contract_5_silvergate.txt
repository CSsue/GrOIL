     maintenance. The policy cannot be cancelled by the Company 
     due to health deterioration.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. DEATH BENEFIT — MODIFIED SCHEDULE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1  YEAR 1 (before October 1, 2025):
       Natural death: Return of premiums paid + 10% interest
       Accidental death: $25,000 (full benefit, immediate)

2.2  YEAR 2 (October 1, 2025 – September 30, 2026):
       Natural death: Return of premiums paid + 10% interest
       Accidental death: $25,000 (full benefit)

     NOTE: Years 1 and 2 for natural death pay a "premium plus 
     interest" benefit, not a percentage of face amount. This 
     means the beneficiary recovers more than premiums paid but 
     less than the face amount if death is from natural causes.

2.3  YEAR 3+ (October 1, 2026 and thereafter):
       All causes of death: Full $25,000 payable

2.4  ACCIDENTAL DEATH DEFINITION: Sudden, external, violent 
     event causing death within 90 days, excluding self-inflicted 
     injury, war, or illness.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT RIDER (ABR)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Rider Form: SG-ABR-2021 | No additional premium charged.

4.1  TERMINAL ILLNESS BENEFIT: If the Insured is diagnosed with 
     a terminal illness — defined as a condition certified by a 
     licensed physician to result in death within 12 months — the 
     Trustee (Owner) may request an acceleration of up to 80% of 
     the then-current death benefit, not to exceed $20,000.

4.2  CHRONIC ILLNESS BENEFIT: If the Insured is certified by a 
     licensed healthcare practitioner as chronically ill (unable 
     to perform at least 2 of 6 Activities of Daily Living without 
     substantial assistance, for a period expected to last at 
     least 90 days), the Trustee may request acceleration of up 
     to 50% of the then-current death benefit, not to exceed 
     $12,500. This benefit is available only once per policy year.

4.3  Accelerated amounts reduce the death benefit payable at 
     death on a dollar-for-dollar basis.

4.4  A $250 administrative fee is charged per acceleration 
     request, deducted from the accelerated amount.

4.5  The ABR rider is available only while the base policy is 
     in force and after the modified benefit period (Year 3+).

4.6  TAX NOTE: Amounts received under this rider may be taxable. 
     The Company recommends consulting a tax advisor.
