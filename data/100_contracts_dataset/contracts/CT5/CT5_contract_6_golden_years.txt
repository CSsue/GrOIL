──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
NOTE ON PURPOSE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy is a whole life policy placed on a juvenile life. It 
is categorized in the Company's Final Expense division because:
(a) the low face amount and simple structure mirror final expense 
    products; and
(b) the Owner intends the policy as a legacy gift to the Insured 
    that will be fully paid up before the Insured's adult years.

This product is NOT structured to fund the Insured's funeral 
expenses — it is a gift of lifelong insurance coverage.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCHEDULE OF INSURANCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Face Amount:                  $10,000
Monthly Premium:              $14.20 (due on 5th of each month)
Payment Mode:                 Monthly EFT from Owner's account
Premium Payment Period:       Limited Pay — premiums cease when 
                              Insured reaches age 25
                              (approximately 13 years of premiums)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. LIMITED PREMIUM PAYMENT DESIGN
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1  Premiums are payable for the period from the Policy Date 
     until the Policy Anniversary on or after the Insured's 25th 
     birthday (approximately April 5, 2037).

1.2  After the premium payment period ends, the policy becomes 
     FULLY PAID-UP. No further premiums are required, and the 
     full $10,000 Face Amount remains in force for the Insured's 
     entire life.

1.3  The Owner (grandmother) may continue paying after the 
     premium payment period ends to purchase additional paid-up 
     insurance, but is not required to do so.

1.4  If the Owner dies before the premium payment period ends, 
     the Contingent Owner (named below) assumes ownership and 
     premium-paying obligation.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. CONTINGENT OWNER PROVISION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Contingent Owner: Scott Alan Harrington (Father of Insured)

2.1  If the Owner (Dolores Eugenia Harrington) dies before the 
     Insured reaches age 25, ownership automatically passes to 
     the Contingent Owner (Scott Alan Harrington).

2.2  The Contingent Owner assumes full ownership rights, including 
     the obligation to continue premium payments to maintain the 
     policy in force.

2.3  If the Contingent Owner does not wish to continue premiums, 
     they may:
       (a) elect a nonforfeiture option (Section 6); or
       (b) transfer ownership to the Insured (if age 18+).

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. OWNERSHIP TRANSFER TO INSURED
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1  On the Policy Anniversary on or after the Insured's 25th 
     birthday (when premiums cease), ownership is automatically 
     transferred to the Insured (Jackson Cole Harrington) unless 
     the Owner files a written election to retain ownership.

3.2  Upon transfer, the Insured becomes the sole Owner and may 
     designate a new beneficiary.

3.3  The Owner may also voluntarily transfer ownership to the 
     Insured at any time after the Insured reaches age 18.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. DEATH BENEFIT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1  The full Face Amount of $10,000 is payable upon the Insured's 
     death from any cause at any time this policy is in force.

4.2  There is NO graded benefit period. The juvenile underwriting 
     basis (no health history concerns at age 12) supports 
     immediate full benefit from the Policy Date.

4.3  If the OWNER (grandmother) dies before the Insured, the 
     death benefit is NOT payable — the Owner's death triggers 
     the ownership transfer provisions of Section 2 and 3, not 
     a claim.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. CASH VALUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1  Because this is a limited-pay design, cash value accumulates 
     more rapidly than a continuous-pay policy. Guaranteed values:

       End of Year 3:    $310
       End of Year 5:    $640
       End of Year 10:   $1,800
       At Paid-Up (approx. 13 years): $2,900
       Insured age 40:   $4,700
       Insured age 65:   $8,400

5.2  LOANS (after Year 2): Owner may borrow up to 90% of cash 
     value at 5.0% annual interest.

5.3  SURRENDER: Policy may be surrendered for net cash value at 
     any time. If surrendered before paid-up date, all future 
     premium obligations cease.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. NONFORFEITURE OPTIONS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If premiums lapse before the paid-up date (and cash value exists):
  (a) EXTENDED TERM: Cash value buys term at full $10,000.
  (b) REDUCED PAID-UP: Smaller face amount, fully paid up.
  DEFAULT: Reduced Paid-Up.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. EXCLUSIONS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1  SUICIDE: If the Insured (once of legal age) dies by suicide 
     within 24 months of the Policy Date, premiums paid are 
     returned without interest.

7.2  CONTESTABILITY: 24 months from issue date. Policy is 
     incontestable thereafter (except non-payment).

7.3  No exclusions for war, aviation, sports, or hazardous activity.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. OWNER RIGHTS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1  FREE LOOK: 30 days from delivery — full refund of premiums.
8.2  The Owner may change the beneficiary designation while 
     the Insured is under age 18.
8.3  Once the Insured reaches age 18, the Insured must consent 
     to any change of beneficiary.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. CLAIMS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Claims filed by then-current Owner/Beneficiary. Required: 
certified death certificate and completed claim form. Claims 
paid within 7 business days.

Claims: 1-800-GY-CLAIM | P.O. Box 500, Savannah, GA 31402
