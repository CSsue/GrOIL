ARTICLE I - COVERAGE DESCRIPTION
1.1 This policy is a second-to-die whole life policy designed to provide guaranteed protection when required premiums are paid.
1.2 Death proceeds are payable only after both insureds have died.
1.3 The death of the first insured does not create a death claim.

ARTICLE II - NO-LAPSE GUARANTEE
2.1 The policy will remain in force during the no-lapse guarantee period if cumulative premiums paid equal or exceed the required guarantee premium amount shown in the schedule.
2.2 The no-lapse guarantee operates independently of actual cash value, so long as all other conditions are satisfied.
2.3 Loans and unpaid loan interest do not terminate the guarantee unless policy debt exceeds the maximum amount permitted.
2.4 After the guarantee period, coverage continues only if sufficient net cash value remains.

ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT PROVISIONS
3.1 We will pay the specified amount less policy debt and unpaid charges authorized by this policy.
3.2 Proof of both deaths and completed claim documents are required.
3.3 If the beneficiary elects an installment settlement, interest will be credited at a rate determined by us, but not below any legal minimum.

ARTICLE IV - PREMIUM REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Premiums are due annually on the policy anniversary.
4.2 The annual premium shown above is the minimum premium required to maintain the guarantee on an annual-pay basis.
4.3 If the first insured dies, guarantee premiums must continue to be paid to preserve the no-lapse guarantee.
4.4 Grace period is 60 days.

ARTICLE V - CASH VALUE AND POLICY LOANS
5.1 This policy develops limited guaranteed cash value during the no-lapse guarantee period.
5.2 Loans are available beginning in year 3.
5.3 Maximum loan amount is 85% of net cash surrender value.
5.4 Fixed loan interest rate: 6.0% per annum.
5.5 Partial withdrawals are not permitted.

ARTICLE VI - SURVIVORSHIP LAPSE PROTECTION RIDER
6.1 If this rider is shown in the schedule and one insured dies during the guarantee period, we will grant an additional 12-month extension for any unpaid premium due immediately after that first death.
6.2 The rider does not waive premium permanently and expires after the first death.

ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
7.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, our liability is limited to premiums paid less debt.
7.2 We may contest this policy within 2 years after issue for material misrepresentation.
7.3 This policy has no war or terrorism exclusion.

ARTICLE VIII - TRUST PROVISIONS
8.1 All owner rights are exercisable only by the acting trustee.
8.2 We may rely on any trust certification furnished to us.
8.3 We are not responsible for determining whether the trust is irrevocable or tax-effective.

ARTICLE IX - COMMON DISASTER
9.1 If both insureds die from the same disaster and evidence does not establish the order of death, we will treat the policy as matured by second death.

ARTICLE X - FREE LOOK
10.1 The owner may return this policy within 30 days after receipt for cancellation and refund.
