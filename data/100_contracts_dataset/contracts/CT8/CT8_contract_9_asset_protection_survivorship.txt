ARTICLE I - COVERAGE DESCRIPTION
1.1 This is a survivorship whole life policy covering two insureds.
1.2 The death benefit is payable only when the second insured dies.
1.3 If one insured dies first, the policy continues on the life of the surviving insured.
1.4 This policy is marketed for asset protection and legacy transfer strategies.

ARTICLE II - PREMIUM GUARANTEE AND FLEX PREMIUM PRIVILEGE
2.1 The annual premium shown above is guaranteed for the first 12 policy years.
2.2 Beginning in policy year 13, the owner may continue premiums at the current rate or any higher rate up to the maximum premium stated in the schedule.
2.3 Subject to our rules, the owner may prepay up to 3 annual premiums in advance, provided such payment does not violate applicable tax qualification limits.
2.4 If the first insured dies, the premium schedule continues.

ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT
3.1 We will pay the face amount less policy debt and unpaid loan interest at second death.
3.2 We may require trust authority documents and taxpayer identification information before payment.
3.3 Settlement options include lump sum, fixed installments, and retained-asset account where permitted.

ARTICLE IV - CASH VALUE ACCESS
4.1 Loans are available after 24 months in force.
4.2 Maximum loan amount is 92% of net surrender value.
4.3 Variable loan rate: currently 5.65%.
4.4 The owner may elect reduced paid-up insurance or cash surrender.
4.5 Partial withdrawals are not permitted.

ARTICLE V - CREDITOR PROTECTIVE ADMINISTRATION
5.1 If the owner is a trust formed for asset protection planning, we may rely on any certification of trustee authority and need not investigate the validity of the trust.
5.2 We do not guarantee creditor protection, spendthrift effect, or bankruptcy treatment of any policy interest.
5.3 The owner is responsible for obtaining legal advice.

ARTICLE VI - REPLACEMENT PRESERVATION ENDORSEMENT
6.1 If this policy replaced another survivorship policy, the owner may request, within 60 days after issue, an administrative review of any discrepancy in applied premium credits disclosed during replacement.
6.2 This endorsement does not increase the death benefit.
6.3 Any premium credit granted will be added to policy value as determined by us.

ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
7.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, liability is limited to premiums paid less debt.
7.2 Contestability period is 2 years after issue.
7.3 No occupational, foreign travel, or terrorism exclusion applies.

ARTICLE VIII - COMMON DISASTER
8.1 If both insureds die in the same occurrence and the order of death cannot be determined, we will process the claim as a second-death benefit claim.

ARTICLE IX - ASSIGNMENT AND OWNERSHIP
9.1 The owner may assign or collaterally assign the policy by a written instrument recorded by us.
9.2 We are not bound by any assignment until recorded.

ARTICLE X - FREE LOOK
10.1 The owner may cancel this policy within 30 days after delivery for a refund of premium paid.
