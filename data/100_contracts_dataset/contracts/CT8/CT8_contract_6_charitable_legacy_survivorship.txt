ARTICLE I - COVERAGE
1.1 This policy insures two lives and pays at second death only.
1.2 If one insured dies, no death claim is payable, and the policy continues on the surviving life.
1.3 This policy is intended to support charitable legacy planning.

ARTICLE II - CHARITABLE ASSIGNMENT OPTION
2.1 After the 10th policy year, the owner may irrevocably assign all or a portion of the policy proceeds to one or more qualified charitable organizations by filing an endorsement acceptable to us.
2.2 We are not required to determine whether an organization is a qualified charity under tax law.
2.3 If a named charity cannot accept the proceeds, payment will be made to the secondary beneficiary unless the owner has filed a replacement designation.

ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT SETTLEMENT
3.1 We will pay the death benefit less policy debt at second death.
3.2 The beneficiary may elect a lump sum or a fixed-period settlement.
3.3 For charitable beneficiaries, we may require evidence of legal existence and authority to receive the proceeds.

ARTICLE IV - PREMIUMS AND DIVIDENDS
4.1 Premiums are payable annually for life unless otherwise terminated by nonforfeiture election or surrender.
4.2 This is a participating policy and dividends are not guaranteed.
4.3 Dividend options are cash, premium reduction, accumulation at interest, or paid-up additions.
4.4 If the first insured dies, premium obligations continue.
4.5 Grace period is 61 days.

ARTICLE V - CASH VALUES AND LOANS
5.1 Guaranteed cash values accumulate under the policy.
5.2 Loans may be taken after one full policy year.
5.3 Fixed loan interest rate: 5.0%.
5.4 Maximum loan value is 90% of loan value then available.
5.5 Policy loans reduce the death benefit and cash surrender value.

ARTICLE VI - MEMORIAL BENEFIT ENDORSEMENT
6.1 If elected and shown in the schedule, a memorial benefit of $25,000 may be paid at first death to the owner for charitable event, donor recognition, or memorial expenses.
6.2 Any memorial benefit paid under this endorsement reduces the amount payable at second death.
6.3 The endorsement terminates at first death.

ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
7.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, liability is limited to premiums paid less indebtedness.
7.2 The policy is contestable for 2 years after issue for material misrepresentation.
7.3 No travel or aviation exclusion applies.

ARTICLE VIII - COMMON DISASTER
8.1 If both insureds die in a common event and the order of death cannot be established, we will pay the policy as a matured second-death claim.

ARTICLE IX - OWNER RIGHTS
9.1 Owner rights are exercisable only by the trustee.
9.2 The owner may change beneficiaries, assign the policy, borrow, or surrender the policy, subject to law.

ARTICLE X - FREE LOOK
10.1 The policy may be returned within 30 days after delivery for a refund of premium paid.
