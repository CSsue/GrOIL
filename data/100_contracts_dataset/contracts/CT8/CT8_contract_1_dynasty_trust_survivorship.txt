───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE I - NATURE OF COVERAGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This policy is a survivorship whole life policy insuring two
    persons under one contract.

1.2 DEATH BENEFIT TRIGGER: We pay the death benefit only upon the
    death of the second insured to die.

1.3 No death benefit is payable at the first death.

1.4 After the first death, the policy remains in force on the life
    of the surviving insured, subject to all premium, loan, and
    policy provisions.

1.5 This form is intended for estate equalization, liquidity for
    transfer taxes, and intergenerational wealth transfer planning.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE II - DEATH BENEFIT AND DIVIDENDS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 Upon the second death, we will pay the then-current face amount
    plus any paid-up additions, less policy debt and accrued
    interest.

2.2 This is a participating policy. Dividends, if declared, are not
    guaranteed.

2.3 Dividend options are:
    • Paid in cash
    • Reduction of premium
    • Left to accumulate at interest
    • Purchase of paid-up additions

2.4 Paid-up additions increase both cash value and death benefit.

2.5 Claim requirements include proof of the deaths of both insureds,
    completed claim forms, and any additional documentation
    reasonably required to establish beneficiary entitlement.

2.6 We will normally settle the claim within 20 business days after
    receipt of complete and satisfactory proof.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE III - PREMIUM STRUCTURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 Premiums are due on each policy anniversary.

3.2 This policy is designed on a continuous-pay whole life basis.

3.3 Premiums may be offset by dividends only to the extent permitted
    by our administrative rules then in effect.

3.4 If the first insured dies, premium obligations continue unless
    the policy has been converted to paid-up status.

3.5 GRACE PERIOD: A premium not paid by its due date may be paid
    within 61 days after the due date.

3.6 If the premium remains unpaid after the grace period, this
    policy will lapse subject to any nonforfeiture value available.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE IV - CASH VALUES, LOANS, AND NONFORFEITURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 This policy develops guaranteed cash value according to the
    policy schedule.

4.2 POLICY LOANS:
    • Available after the first policy year
    • Maximum loan amount is 92% of available loan value
    • Loan interest rate is fixed at 5.0% per annum
    • Unpaid loan interest will be added to the loan principal
    • Policy debt reduces the amount payable at surrender or death

4.3 NONFORFEITURE OPTIONS:
    • Cash surrender
    • Reduced paid-up insurance
    • Extended term option is not available on this policy form

4.4 Partial withdrawals are not allowed.

4.5 The owner may elect reduced paid-up insurance after the policy
    has acquired nonforfeiture values.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE V - FIRST DEATH ADMINISTRATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 At first death, the owner must notify us within 90 days or as
    soon thereafter as reasonably possible.

5.2 After the first death:
    (a) No death proceeds are payable
    (b) The face amount remains unchanged unless altered by policy
        debt, dividend election, or approved rider activity
    (c) The surviving insured does not become the owner unless the
        ownership arrangement separately provides for that result

5.3 We may require updated tax certifications or trustee authority
    if the policy is owned by a trust.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE VI - POLICY CHANGES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 FACE AMOUNT INCREASE OPTION: During the first 5 policy years,
    the owner may apply for an increase in coverage, subject to our
    underwriting and minimum issue rules.

6.2 RIDER OPTIONS, if elected and shown in the schedule, may
    include:
    • Guaranteed purchase option on the surviving life
    • Additional paid-up insurance rider
    • Waiver of monthly deductions rider is not available because
      this is not a universal life form

6.3 Any increase approved after issue will be separately
    contestable and subject to a separate suicide limitation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 SUICIDE: If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after
    issue, our liability is limited to total premiums paid minus any
    policy debt.

7.2 If there is an increase in face amount, the suicide provision
    applies separately to the increased amount from its effective
    date.

7.3 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest this policy for material
    misrepresentation during the first 2 years after issue while one
    or both insureds are living.

7.4 No war, terrorism, or aviation exclusion applies.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE VIII - TRUST AND TAX ADMINISTRATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 If this policy is owned by a dynasty trust, all owner rights
    are exercisable only by the acting trustee.

8.2 We are not responsible for determining whether this policy is
    excluded from any taxable estate.

8.3 The owner should consult legal and tax advisors regarding
    generation-skipping transfer tax, Crummey notice procedures,
    and split-dollar restrictions.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE IX - SIMULTANEOUS DEATH
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 If both insureds die in the same event and there is no
    sufficient evidence of the order of death, the death benefit
    will be treated as due immediately.

9.2 If applicable law requires a minimum survival period, that law
    will control unless this policy expressly provides otherwise.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE X - ASSIGNMENT AND OWNERSHIP RIGHTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10.1 The owner may assign this policy by written assignment filed
     with us and recorded by us.

10.2 We are not bound by any assignment until recorded.

10.3 An assignment is subject to any existing beneficiary
     designation unless the assignment states otherwise and the law
     permits.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE XI - FREE LOOK
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11.1 The owner may return this policy within 30 days after receipt
     for a full refund of premiums paid.
