COVERAGE DETAILS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Face Amount: $1,800,000
Guaranteed Annual Premium: $58,950
Premium Period: 15 policy years only
Paid-Up Date: On the 15th policy anniversary if all premiums due
through that date have been paid.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE I - COVERAGE BASIS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This policy insures two people under one survivorship whole
    life contract.

1.2 We will pay the death benefit only upon the second death.

1.3 The death of the first insured does not end the policy.

1.4 After the first death, no additional premiums are waived unless
    specifically stated in a rider attached to this policy.

1.5 This policy is intended for family legacy, charitable giving,
    and liquidity planning.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE II - LIMITED PAY DESIGN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 Premiums are due annually for 15 policy years.

2.2 No premium is due after the 15th policy anniversary if all
    required premiums have been paid when due or within the grace
    period.

2.3 If the first insured dies during the premium-paying period, the
    remaining scheduled premiums continue to be due.

2.4 If the policy is in force and fully paid-up, the death benefit
    continues for life on a survivorship basis without further
    premium.

2.5 Premiums may not be skipped during the 15-pay period except by
    use of available dividend values when allowed by us.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT SETTLEMENT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 The base death benefit is $1,800,000.

3.2 Any outstanding loan balance and accrued interest will reduce
    the amount payable.

3.3 The beneficiary may elect:
    • Lump-sum payment
    • Interest-only settlement for up to 5 years
    • Fixed installment payments under an option agreement

3.4 We require proof of death for both insureds and reasonable
    evidence of beneficiary entitlement.

3.5 If the beneficiary is a trust, we may require certification of
    trust authority before settlement.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE IV - GUARANTEED PAID-UP ADDITION RIDER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 This policy includes a scheduled paid-up addition rider for the
    first 10 policy years.

4.2 The rider permits optional additional premiums up to the
    maximum amount accepted by us without creating modified
    endowment contract status based on information available to us
    at the time of payment.

4.3 Additional paid-up insurance increases cash value and the total
    death benefit.

4.4 We may decline excess unscheduled payments.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE V - CASH VALUE AND LOANS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 Guaranteed cash values are shown in the policy schedule.

5.2 Loans are available after the policy has been in force for one
    full policy year.

5.3 Maximum loan value is 90% of net cash surrender value.

5.4 Fixed loan interest rate: 4.75% per annum.

5.5 Loan interest not paid when due will be added to policy debt.

5.6 This policy does not permit partial surrenders.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE VI - NONFORFEITURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 If premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, and this
    policy has cash value, the default nonforfeiture option will be
    reduced paid-up insurance unless another option has been elected
    in writing.

6.2 The owner may instead elect cash surrender.

6.3 Extended term insurance is not available under this limited-pay
    survivorship whole life form.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 SUICIDE: If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after
    the issue date, our liability is limited to premiums paid less
    any indebtedness.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest this policy during the first
    2 years after issue for material misrepresentation.

7.3 No aviation, war, foreign residence, or travel exclusion
    applies.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE VIII - OWNER RIGHTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 During the lifetime of both insureds, any ownership action must
    be signed by both joint owners unless we accept an alternative
    instruction previously filed with us.

8.2 After the first death, the surviving joint owner may exercise
    ownership rights unless ownership has passed by assignment or
    court order.

8.3 Owner rights include changing beneficiaries, borrowing,
    assigning, and surrendering the policy.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE IX - SIMULTANEOUS DEATH AND COMMON DISASTER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 If both insureds die in a common disaster and the order of
    death cannot be determined, the death benefit will be payable
    as though both deaths have occurred.

9.2 If one insured survives the other by less than 15 days, we will
    still treat the second death as the triggering event, and only
    one death benefit is payable.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ARTICLE X - FREE LOOK
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10.1 The owner may cancel this policy within 20 days after receipt
     for a full refund of all premiums paid.
