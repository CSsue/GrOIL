ARTICLE I - COVERAGE
1.1 This policy is a survivorship whole life policy.
1.2 We will pay the death benefit only upon the death of the second insured.
1.3 The policy remains in force after the first death, subject to the premium deposit arrangement, policy values, and policy debt.

ARTICLE II - PREMIUM DEPOSIT AGREEMENT
2.1 At issue, the owner deposits $540,000 into a premium deposit account administered by us.
2.2 The premium deposit account is not part of the policy cash value and is governed by a separate agreement.
2.3 Each year, the amount of premium then due may be withdrawn from the premium deposit account and applied to this policy.
2.4 Interest on the premium deposit account will be credited at a rate declared by us, subject to any minimum required by law.
2.5 If the premium deposit account is exhausted, future premiums remain due directly from the owner.
2.6 The owner may terminate the premium deposit arrangement in accordance with the separate agreement.

ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT
3.1 The death benefit equals the face amount plus paid-up additions, if any, less policy debt.
3.2 Any amount remaining in the premium deposit account at second death is not added to the policy death benefit unless the separate deposit agreement provides otherwise.
3.3 Proof of both deaths and beneficiary entitlement are required.

ARTICLE IV - DIVIDENDS AND POLICY GROWTH
4.1 This is a participating policy and dividends are not guaranteed.
4.2 Unless changed by the owner, dividends will purchase paid-up additions.
4.3 Paid-up additions increase death benefit and cash value.

ARTICLE V - LOANS, CASH VALUES, AND NONFORFEITURE
5.1 Policy loans are available after one full policy year.
5.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of net cash surrender value.
5.3 Fixed loan interest rate: 4.85%.
5.4 The owner may elect cash surrender or reduced paid-up insurance.
5.5 Partial withdrawals are not permitted.

ARTICLE VI - FIRST DEATH SERVICE OPTION
6.1 At first death, the owner may request a service review to determine whether remaining premium deposits are sufficient to carry the policy for the life expectancy of the surviving insured under current assumptions.
6.2 The review is informational only and does not guarantee future policy performance.
6.3 The owner may supplement the premium deposit account or pay premiums directly.

ARTICLE VII - PREMIUMS AND GRACE PERIOD
7.1 Premiums are payable annually for life unless the policy is surrendered, reduced paid-up, or otherwise supported by policy values. Such support is not guaranteed.
7.2 Grace period is 61 days.

ARTICLE VIII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
8.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, our liability is limited to premiums paid less debt.
8.2 Contestability period is 2 years after issue.
8.3 No war or aviation exclusion applies.

ARTICLE IX - COMMON DISASTER
9.1 If both insureds die in the same disaster and the order of death cannot be determined, this policy will be treated as payable under the second-death provision.

ARTICLE X - OWNER RIGHTS
10.1 The owner may change beneficiaries, assign the policy, surrender the policy, borrow against the policy, and maintain the premium deposit agreement.
10.2 We may require evidence of LLC authority before acting on any request.

ARTICLE XI - FREE LOOK
11.1 The owner may return this policy within 30 days after receipt for cancellation and refund of premiums paid under the policy. Treatment of the premium deposit account will be governed by the separate deposit agreement.
