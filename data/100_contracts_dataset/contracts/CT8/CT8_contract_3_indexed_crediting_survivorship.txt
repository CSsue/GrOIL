Policy Value Feature: Guaranteed whole life cash values plus an indexed dividend enhancement feature.

ARTICLE I - NATURE OF COVERAGE
1.1 This is a survivorship whole life insurance policy.
1.2 The death benefit is payable only upon the death of the second insured.
1.3 No policy proceeds are payable on the first death.
1.4 Following the first death, all policy rights remain vested in the owner of record.

ARTICLE II - INDEXED DIVIDEND ENHANCEMENT FEATURE
2.1 This policy is participating.
2.2 We may credit an indexed dividend enhancement amount if declared by us.
2.3 The enhancement is based on a designated broad-market index, subject to a participation factor, zero percent floor, and maximum cap.
2.4 The indexed enhancement is not guaranteed.
2.5 Negative index performance will not reduce guaranteed cash values already provided by the base policy.
2.6 Any declared indexed enhancement will purchase paid-up additions unless we permit another dividend option.

ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT
3.1 The death benefit equals the base face amount plus paid-up additions, less policy debt and accrued interest.
3.2 Claim payment requires proof of both deaths and satisfactory beneficiary documentation.
3.3 We may request partnership resolutions or authority documents from the owner or beneficiary.

ARTICLE IV - PREMIUMS
4.1 Planned annual premium is $76,000.
4.2 The policy is designed on a current assumptions basis with guaranteed minimum values and non-guaranteed enhancement values.
4.3 Premiums are payable for life unless the policy becomes self-supporting according to then-current non-guaranteed values. We do not guarantee that result.
4.4 Grace period is 62 days.

ARTICLE V - CASH VALUE, LOANS, AND COLLATERAL ASSIGNMENT
5.1 The policy accumulates guaranteed cash surrender value.
5.2 Loans are available beginning in policy year 2.
5.3 Maximum loan amount is 95% of net cash surrender value.
5.4 Variable loan rate: currently 5.85% per annum.
5.5 The owner may collaterally assign this policy by filing the assignment with us.

ARTICLE VI - FIRST DEATH OPTION
6.1 If elected at issue and shown in the schedule, the owner may receive an advance benefit of $150,000 at first death.
6.2 Any first-death advance reduces the death benefit otherwise payable at second death.
6.3 The first-death advance is not a separate death benefit and does not terminate the policy.

ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
7.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, our liability is limited to premiums paid less policy debt.
7.2 We may contest this policy for material misrepresentation during the first 2 years after issue.
7.3 No hazardous occupation exclusion applies.

ARTICLE VIII - CHARITABLE BENEFICIARY PROVISIONS
8.1 If the named charitable beneficiary has ceased to exist at the second death, proceeds will be paid to the contingent beneficiary on file.
8.2 We are not responsible for determining tax-deductibility or charitable qualification.

ARTICLE IX - SIMULTANEOUS DEATH
9.1 If both insureds die in the same occurrence and order of death cannot be determined, the claim will be processed as a valid second-death claim upon proof of both deaths.

ARTICLE X - FREE LOOK
10.1 This policy may be returned within 30 days after delivery for cancellation and refund.
