ARTICLE I - COVERAGE
1.1 This policy pays a death benefit only at second death.
1.2 If one insured dies, no death claim is payable and the policy remains in force.
1.3 This policy is intended for family support and inheritance planning.

ARTICLE II - FAMILY SURVIVOR INCOME OPTION
2.1 In lieu of a full lump sum, the beneficiary may elect an initial lump sum of 20% of the net death benefit and monthly income payments over 10, 15, or 20 years.
2.2 The amount of monthly income depends on the net death benefit, settlement period selected, and the interest rate applicable under our settlement option rules then in effect.
2.3 Once monthly income payments begin, the settlement option may not be changed except as permitted by law.

ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT
3.1 The net death benefit equals the face amount plus paid-up additions, if any, less policy debt.
3.2 We require proof of both deaths and beneficiary documentation.
3.3 If a trust is beneficiary, we may pay the trustee under the terms of the trust certification furnished to us.

ARTICLE IV - PREMIUMS AND DIVIDENDS
4.1 This is a participating policy.
4.2 Dividends are not guaranteed.
4.3 Unless the owner elects otherwise, dividends will accumulate at interest.
4.4 Premiums are payable for life unless the policy is surrendered or reduced paid-up.
4.5 Grace period is 60 days.

ARTICLE V - LOANS AND CASH VALUES
5.1 Loans are available after one policy year.
5.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of available loan value.
5.3 Fixed loan interest rate: 5.0%.
5.4 Cash surrender and reduced paid-up values are available as shown in the schedule.
5.5 Partial withdrawals are not permitted.

ARTICLE VI - FIRST-DEATH CONTINUATION ENDORSEMENT
6.1 After first death, the owner may elect within 180 days to convert this policy administratively to a premium-offset status if available dividends and cash values are sufficient under our then-current rules.
6.2 We do not guarantee that premium-offset status will be available.
6.3 Election of premium-offset status may reduce future policy growth.

ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
7.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, liability is limited to premiums paid less indebtedness.
7.2 The policy is contestable for material misrepresentation during the first 2 years after issue.
7.3 There is no hazardous hobby exclusion unless specifically added by endorsement.

ARTICLE VIII - SIMULTANEOUS DEATH
8.1 If both insureds die in the same occurrence and the order of death cannot be established, this policy will be treated as payable at second death.

ARTICLE IX - FREE LOOK
9.1 The owner may cancel this policy within 30 days after receipt for a full refund of premium paid.
