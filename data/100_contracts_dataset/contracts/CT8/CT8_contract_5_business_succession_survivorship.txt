ARTICLE I - POLICY PURPOSE AND COVERAGE
1.1 This policy insures two key shareholder spouses on a survivorship whole life basis.
1.2 The death benefit becomes payable only upon the death of the second insured.
1.3 No proceeds are payable at first death, but the policy remains in force.
1.4 This policy is intended to support corporate succession, deferred compensation reimbursement, and liquidity planning.

ARTICLE II - BENEFIT PROVISIONS
2.1 We will pay the face amount less any policy debt at second death.
2.2 The beneficiary may apply all or part of the proceeds toward redemption obligations, deferred compensation liabilities, reserve funding, or general corporate purposes.
2.3 We do not monitor the beneficiary's actual use of proceeds once paid.
2.4 Proof of both deaths and corporate authority to receive proceeds may be required.

ARTICLE III - EXECUTIVE BONUS CONTINUATION OPTION
3.1 If this option is elected and shown in the schedule, the corporate owner may assign ownership to a designated executive trust after the retirement of both insureds.
3.2 Any assignment must be in writing and recorded by us.
3.3 A recorded assignment changes future owner rights but does not change the insureds or the survivorship nature of the policy.

ARTICLE IV - PREMIUMS
4.1 Premiums are payable for life unless the policy becomes reduced paid-up.
4.2 Grace period is 31 days for monthly mode and 62 days for annual mode.
4.3 If the first insured dies, premium obligations continue.

ARTICLE V - CASH VALUES AND ACCESS
5.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates under this policy.
5.2 Loans are available after the second policy year.
5.3 Maximum loan amount is 90% of net cash surrender value.
5.4 Variable loan rate: currently 5.25%.
5.5 The owner may surrender the policy or elect reduced paid-up insurance.
5.6 Partial withdrawals are not allowed.

ARTICLE VI - INSURED PURCHASE OPTION UPON CORPORATE SALE
6.1 If the corporate owner sells substantially all of its assets, the surviving insured or a trust for the surviving insured may apply to purchase the policy from the owner.
6.2 The insurer is not obligated to approve or value that transfer.
6.3 Any ownership transfer must comply with law, tax rules, and our administrative requirements.

ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
7.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, our liability is limited to premiums paid minus debt.
7.2 Contestability period is 2 years from issue.
7.3 This policy contains no military service exclusion.

ARTICLE VIII - COMMON DISASTER
8.1 If the insureds die in the same incident and no order of death can be established, the policy will be treated as matured at the time satisfactory proof is received.

ARTICLE IX - OWNER RIGHTS
9.1 The corporate owner may change beneficiaries, borrow, assign, surrender, or elect nonforfeiture options.
9.2 Any corporate act must be authorized by an officer or other person acceptable to us.

ARTICLE X - FREE LOOK
10.1 This policy may be cancelled within 15 days after delivery for a refund of premiums paid.
