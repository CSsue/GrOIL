ARTICLE I - SURVIVORSHIP COVERAGE
1.1 This policy provides whole life coverage on a second-to-die basis.
1.2 The benefit is payable only after both insureds have died.
1.3 If one insured dies, the policy remains in force.
1.4 This policy is intended to help equalize inheritances among beneficiaries receiving operating and non-operating assets.

ARTICLE II - CO-OWNER ADMINISTRATION
2.1 All co-owners must sign any request to change beneficiaries, assign ownership, surrender the policy, elect a nonforfeiture option, or change the face amount.
2.2 Any one co-owner may authorize payment of premium.
2.3 After the first death, a deceased co-owner's rights pass as provided in the ownership agreement or applicable law.
2.4 We may rely on a co-owner certification or court order if there is a dispute among owners.

ARTICLE III - DEATH BENEFIT
3.1 We will pay the face amount plus any paid-up additions, less policy debt, upon second death.
3.2 If the beneficiary is a trust, we may require trust certification and tax identification information.
3.3 Settlement options include lump sum and fixed installments.

ARTICLE IV - DIVIDENDS AND PAID-UP ADDITIONS
4.1 This is a participating policy.
4.2 Unless changed by the owner, dividends will purchase paid-up additions.
4.3 Dividends are not guaranteed.

ARTICLE V - PREMIUMS, LOANS, AND CASH VALUES
5.1 Premiums are due for life unless the policy becomes reduced paid-up or fully supported by dividends and internal values. Such support is not guaranteed.
5.2 Grace period is 61 days.
5.3 Loans are available after policy year 1.
5.4 Maximum loan amount is 90% of net cash surrender value.
5.5 Fixed loan interest rate is 5.25%.
5.6 Partial withdrawals are not permitted.

ARTICLE VI - OPTIONAL ESTATE LIQUIDITY ADVANCE
6.1 If elected at issue, the owner may request an advance of up to 10% of the face amount at first death for tax, legal, or appraisal expenses connected with estate administration.
6.2 Any amount advanced under this article reduces the amount otherwise payable at second death.
6.3 Proof of first death and evidence of need satisfactory to us are required.

ARTICLE VII - EXCLUSIONS AND CONTESTABILITY
7.1 If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years after issue, our liability is limited to premiums paid less indebtedness.
7.2 The policy is contestable for 2 years after issue.
7.3 No residence or foreign travel exclusion applies.

ARTICLE VIII - SIMULTANEOUS OR CLOSE-IN-TIME DEATH
8.1 If both insureds die and it cannot be determined who survived, the claim will be processed as a second-death claim.
8.2 If the second insured dies within 60 days after the first, only one death benefit is payable.

ARTICLE IX - ASSIGNMENT
9.1 Any assignment must be signed by all persons whose consent is required under Article II.
9.2 We are not responsible for the effect of any assignment on ownership percentages or tax treatment.

ARTICLE X - FREE LOOK
10.1 The owner may cancel this policy within 25 days after receipt for a refund of premiums paid.
