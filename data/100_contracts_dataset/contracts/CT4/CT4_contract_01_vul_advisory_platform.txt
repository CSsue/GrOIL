SECTION 1: IMPORTANT DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Separate Account" means the segregated asset account supporting variable investment options under this policy.
1.2 "Fixed Account" means the guaranteed interest account declared by us from time to time, subject to a current credited rate and a guaranteed minimum annual effective rate of 2.0%.
1.3 "Net Premium" means premium received less premium expense charges and any rider charges deducted at the time of receipt.
1.4 "Monthly Deduction Day" means the monthly anniversary of the policy date on which monthly policy charges are deducted.

SECTION 4: DEATH BENEFIT AND FACE AMOUNT CHANGES
4.1 OPTION I - LEVEL DEATH BENEFIT (CURRENTLY SELECTED)
Death Benefit equals the face amount, adjusted for any outstanding loan and any rider benefits then payable.
4.2 OPTION II - FACE PLUS ACCOUNT VALUE
Death Benefit equals the face amount plus variable account value, less indebtedness.
4.3 Subject to our rules, you may request a change in death benefit option after the first policy year.
4.4 Increases in face amount require evidence of insurability and are subject to our then-current minimum increase amount of $25,000.
4.5 Decreases in face amount may not reduce coverage below $250,000.

SECTION 5: RIDERS AND POLICY FEATURES
5.1 CHRONIC ILLNESS ACCELERATION RIDER:
If the insured is certified chronically ill under rider terms, a portion of the death benefit may be accelerated.
5.2 NO-LAPSE PREMIUM TEST:
This policy will remain in force through policy year 15 if the cumulative no-lapse test premium is satisfied and no withdrawals have been taken.
5.3 AUTOMATIC PORTFOLIO REBALANCING:
At your election, we will rebalance allocations quarterly to the most recent target percentages on file.

SECTION 6: LOANS, WITHDRAWALS, AND ACCOUNT ACCESS
6.1 POLICY LOANS:
• Maximum: 95% of available loan value
• Loan Interest Rate: 4.75% variable, reset annually
• Loaned amounts remain in the Separate Account under a variable loan method unless you elect the Fixed Account loan option
6.2 PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS:
• Minimum partial withdrawal: $500
• Maximum partial withdrawal: account value less 3 monthly deductions
• Partial withdrawals reduce face amount proportionately under Option I
6.3 A partial withdrawal in the first 10 policy years may reduce or terminate the no-lapse premium test.

SECTION 7: LAPSE, DEFAULT, AND REINSTATEMENT
7.1 The policy enters default if account value is insufficient to cover the monthly deduction on a Monthly Deduction Day.
7.2 We will provide written notice and a 61-day grace period.
7.3 Reinstatement is available within 5 years after lapse, upon:
• Written application
• Evidence of insurability satisfactory to us
• Payment sufficient to restore coverage
• Repayment or reinstatement of policy debt as required by us

SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
8.1 SUICIDE: If the insured dies by suicide within 2 years from issue or reinstatement, our liability is limited to premiums paid less indebtedness and withdrawals.
8.2 MISSTATEMENT OF AGE OR SEX: Benefits and charges will be adjusted to reflect the correct age or sex.
8.3 CONTESTABILITY: Except for nonpayment of charges, this policy is incontestable after it has been in force during the insured's lifetime for 2 years from issue.

9.1 This policy is supported by HarborStone Separate Account VUL-2.
9.2 Interests in the variable investment options are not bank deposits, are not FDIC insured, and may lose value.
9.3 A current prospectus for the Separate Account and underlying funds is available upon request and must be reviewed before allocation.
