SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Survivorship Death Benefit" means the amount payable upon the death of the second insured to die.
1.2 "Insured-Specific Charges" means cost factors determined separately for each insured based on age, sex, class, and risk factors.
1.3 "Accumulation Account" means the aggregate value of all sub-accounts and the guaranteed interest option, less outstanding deductions.
1.4 "Trust Owner" means the legal owner of this policy and the party authorized to exercise ownership rights.

SECTION 2: INVESTMENT PROGRAM
2.1 Net premium may be allocated among:
• Core Equity Strategy
• Dividend Value Strategy
• Intermediate Bond Strategy
• Global Allocation Strategy
• Short-Term Reserve Account
• Guaranteed Interest Option (minimum 2.25%)
2.2 Current Allocation:
Core Equity Strategy: 25%
Dividend Value Strategy: 25%
Intermediate Bond Strategy: 20%
Global Allocation Strategy: 20%
Guaranteed Interest Option: 10%
2.3 Transfer requests may be submitted by the trustee or an authorized registered representative.
2.4 Excessive trading restrictions apply under our market timing policy.

SECTION 3: CHARGES AND TRUST ADMINISTRATION
3.1 PREMIUM LOAD: 3.75% of each premium
3.2 MORTALITY AND EXPENSE CHARGE: 0.95% annually, deducted daily
3.3 MONTHLY POLICY CHARGE: $18
3.4 JOINT COST OF INSURANCE: Determined monthly using rates applicable to both insureds and the survivorship risk assumption.
3.5 TRUST CERTIFICATION REVIEW FEE: $100 for any ownership change, successor trustee certification, or collateral assignment filing.
3.6 SURRENDER CHARGE SCHEDULE:
Years 1-3: 8%
Years 4-6: 6%
Years 7-8: 4%
Years 9-10: 2%
Years 11 and later: 0%

SECTION 4: BENEFIT DESIGN
4.1 No death benefit is payable at the first death except as provided under the First Death Rider, if attached.
4.2 The Survivorship Death Benefit is payable upon proof that both insureds have died while the policy is in force.
4.3 The face amount may be increased after the third policy year, subject to evidence of insurability on both insureds.
4.4 A request to split this policy into two individual policies may be allowed only if a Policy Split Endorsement is attached.

SECTION 5: RIDERS AND SPECIAL PROVISIONS
5.1 ESTATE LIQUIDITY RIDER:
Provides advance access to a portion of the death benefit after the first death if estate tax liquidity conditions in the rider are met.
5.2 TRUST PROTECTOR ENDORSEMENT:
Allows ownership direction by a named trust protector for limited investment and allocation instructions.
5.3 GUARANTEED MINIMUM DEATH BENEFIT RIDER:
If required premiums are paid when due, the policy guarantees a minimum second-to-die death benefit to age 95.

SECTION 6: LOANS AND WITHDRAWALS
6.1 POLICY LOANS:
• Maximum: 90% of net surrender value
• Loan Interest Rate: 5.25% fixed
• Loans reduce the survivorship death benefit and may impair rider guarantees
6.2 PARTIAL SURRENDERS:
• Allowed only after policy year 2
• Minimum: $2,500
• Each partial surrender reduces the face amount proportionately
6.3 Any withdrawal requested by the trustee must include a certification that the withdrawal is permitted under the governing trust instrument.

SECTION 7: LAPSE AND REINSTATEMENT
7.1 This policy lapses if the accumulation account is insufficient to cover the monthly deduction and any applicable rider charges.
7.2 A grace period of 62 days applies after written notice.
7.3 Reinstatement is available within 3 years after lapse if both insureds are living and reinstatement conditions are satisfied.

SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITS
8.1 SUICIDE: If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years of issue or reinstatement, the policy proceeds are adjusted under our survivorship suicide provision stated in the endorsement page.
8.2 CONTESTABILITY: Statements made for each insured may be contested during the applicable contestable period.
8.3 BENEFICIARY LIMITATION: No assignee or beneficiary acquires any rights greater than those held by the policy owner.

SECTION 9: SECURITIES DISCLOSURE
9.1 This policy is issued through TwoHarbor Separate Account C.
9.2 Investment performance is not guaranteed and is borne by the owner.
9.3 Read the prospectus for the Separate Account and each underlying portfolio before making allocations.
