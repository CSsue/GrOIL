4. DEFINITIONS
"Indexed Whole Life" means whole life insurance with credited interest to
a policy value segment based in part on the performance of a designated
external index, subject to caps and floors.
"Index Segment" means a 12-month crediting segment established on each
policy anniversary.
"Participation Rate" means the percentage of index growth recognized for
crediting purposes.
"Floor Rate" means the minimum credited rate, which may be zero.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy combines guaranteed whole life coverage with indexed value
    credits.
5.2 The owner may allocate each year's policy value among:
    (a) Fixed Account;
    (b) One-Year Point-to-Point Index Segment; and
    (c) Capped Monthly Average Index Segment.
5.3 Index-linked credits are not direct investments in any security.
5.4 This policy is non-participating and does not pay dividends.
5.5 A Chronic Illness Accelerated Benefit Rider is attached.

6. CASH VALUE AND INDEX CREDITS
6.1 Guaranteed minimum cash value is shown in the policy values table.
6.2 Guaranteed minimum cash value at end of Year 10: $42,700
    Guaranteed minimum cash value at end of Year 20: $104,900
6.3 The declared Fixed Account rate for the first policy year is 3.25%.
6.4 The initial Participation Rate for the One-Year Point-to-Point Index
    Segment is 85%.
6.5 The initial Cap Rate for the Capped Monthly Average Index Segment is 9%.
6.6 Negative index performance will not reduce the credited value below the
    applicable Floor Rate.

7. PREMIUMS AND NO-LAPSE GUARANTEE
7.1 Quarterly premiums are payable in advance.
7.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
7.3 If the policy account value is insufficient to support policy charges,
    a secondary no-lapse guarantee will keep the policy in force through the
    end of policy year 15 if cumulative premiums paid equal the required
    guarantee premium schedule.

8. POLICY LOANS AND WITHDRAWALS
8.1 Loans are available after the first policy year.
8.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of cash surrender value.
8.3 Fixed loan interest rate is 5.5% per annum.
8.4 Partial withdrawals of at least $1,000 are permitted after policy year 5.
8.5 Loans and withdrawals reduce cash value, index segments, and death
    benefit.

9. DEATH BENEFIT AND ACCELERATION
9.1 The death benefit equals the Base Face Amount less policy debt, plus any
    benefit from attached paid-up rider coverage.
9.2 Under the Chronic Illness Accelerated Benefit Rider, the owner may elect
    advance payment of a portion of the death benefit if the insured meets
    rider eligibility requirements.
9.3 Any accelerated benefit paid will reduce the remaining death benefit and
    cash values.

10. NON-FORFEITURE AND REINSTATEMENT
10.1 Available non-forfeiture options are cash surrender and reduced paid-up
     insurance.
10.2 Extended term insurance is not available under this indexed design.
10.3 Reinstatement is available within 3 years after lapse upon proof of
     insurability and payment of overdue premiums with 5% interest.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits payment to
     premiums paid less withdrawals and debt.
11.2 Misstatement of age or sex will adjust benefits accordingly.
11.3 No index-linked amount is guaranteed beyond the stated minimum values.

12. FREE LOOK AND CONTESTABILITY
12.1 This policy may be returned within 21 days after delivery for a full
     refund of premium paid.
12.2 This policy is incontestable after it has been in force during the
     insured's lifetime for 2 years, except for nonpayment and rider terms.
