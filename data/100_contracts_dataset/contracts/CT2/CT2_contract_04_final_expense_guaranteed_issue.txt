4. DEFINITIONS
"Guaranteed Issue" means no medical examination and no health questions are
required for issue.
"Graded Death Benefit" means a reduced death benefit payable during the
graded period.
"Accidental Death" means death resulting directly and independently from an
accidental bodily injury occurring while the policy is in force.
"Final Expense Policy" means whole life insurance intended primarily to cover
burial and related end-of-life expenses.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy is issued on a guaranteed issue basis.
5.2 No rider may increase the face amount after issue.
5.3 This policy is non-participating and has no dividend feature.
5.4 The death benefit is graded during the first 2 policy years.
5.5 Beginning on the third policy year, the full Base Face Amount is payable
    subject to policy terms.

6. GRADED DEATH BENEFIT
6.1 If death occurs during policy year 1 from causes other than accidental
    death, we will pay 110% of premiums paid.
6.2 If death occurs during policy year 2 from causes other than accidental
    death, we will pay 120% of premiums paid.
6.3 If death occurs during the first 2 policy years due to accidental death,
    we will pay the full Base Face Amount.
6.4 After completion of 2 policy years, the full Base Face Amount is payable
    for death from any cause not otherwise excluded.

7. CASH VALUE
7.1 Cash value begins at the end of policy year 3.
7.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 5: $1,480
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $4,020
7.3 Cash surrender value equals cash value less any policy debt.

8. PREMIUMS AND GRACE PERIOD
8.1 Premiums are payable monthly by bank draft, direct bill, or other method
    we approve.
8.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
8.3 If premium is unpaid after the grace period, the policy will lapse.

9. POLICY LOANS
9.1 Loans are available after the policy has developed a cash surrender
    value.
9.2 Maximum loan amount is 80% of cash surrender value.
9.3 Loan interest rate is 7% per annum.

10. NON-FORFEITURE AND REINSTATEMENT
10.1 After 3 full years of premium payments, the owner may elect cash
     surrender or reduced paid-up insurance.
10.2 Extended term insurance is not available under this policy.
10.3 Reinstatement is available within 2 years after lapse upon payment of
     overdue premiums with 6% interest.
10.4 No evidence of insurability is required for reinstatement if requested
     within 90 days after lapse.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 Suicide during the first 2 policy years is treated under the graded
     death benefit provisions of Section 6.
11.2 Misstatement of age will adjust the face amount to the amount purchased
     by the premium at the correct age.
11.3 We do not provide additional accidental death coverage for death caused
     by self-inflicted injury.

12. FREE LOOK AND CONTESTABILITY
12.1 This policy may be cancelled within 30 days after delivery for a full
     refund of premiums paid.
12.2 Because no health representations are required at issue, contestability
     is limited to fraud in identity or age.
