4. DEFINITIONS
"Supplemental Income Rider" means a rider allowing scheduled policy
withdrawals or loans during a designated income phase, subject to rider
limits and policy sufficiency.
"Income Phase" means the period elected by the owner, beginning no earlier
than policy year 15.
"Overloan Protection Rider" means a rider that may preserve coverage if
specified debt thresholds are met and required rider conditions are satisfied.
"Illustrated Value" means a hypothetical projected value and is not
guaranteed.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy is designed to provide lifetime death benefit protection and
    supplemental retirement income flexibility.
5.2 Premiums are scheduled for 30 years.
5.3 This policy is non-participating.
5.4 A Supplemental Income Rider and Overloan Protection Rider are attached.
5.5 Income distributions under the rider may be taken as policy loans,
    withdrawals, or a combination approved under the rider terms.

6. CASH VALUE

6.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $27,800
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 20: $74,400

7. PREMIUMS, GRACE PERIOD, AND INCOME PHASE
7.1 Monthly premiums are payable in advance.
7.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
7.3 The owner may elect an Income Phase beginning on any policy anniversary
    after completion of policy year 15, subject to minimum cash value and
    rider rules.
7.4 Election of an Income Phase does not guarantee that requested income can
    be maintained for any specific duration.

8. POLICY LOANS AND WITHDRAWALS
8.1 Loans are available after the first policy year.
8.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of cash surrender value.
8.3 Loan interest rate is 5.25% per annum.
8.4 Partial withdrawals of at least $500 are available after policy year 5.
8.5 Loans and withdrawals reduce cash value and death benefit.
8.6 Overloan Protection may become effective only if the rider election is
    made while the policy remains in force and all rider conditions are met.

9. DEATH BENEFIT
9.1 Upon proof of death, we will pay the beneficiary:
    Base Face Amount
    - Policy Debt
    - Withdrawals that permanently reduced face amount
    + Rider-Based Paid-Up Coverage, if any
9.2 Any accelerated or supplemental income transaction will reduce policy
    values as provided under the rider.

10. NON-FORFEITURE AND REINSTATEMENT
10.1 After 3 full years of premium payments, the owner may elect cash
     surrender, reduced paid-up insurance, or automatic premium loan if
     elected by endorsement.
10.2 Extended term insurance is not available.
10.3 Reinstatement is available within 5 years after lapse upon evidence of
     insurability and payment of overdue premiums with 5% interest.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits our liability to
     premiums paid less withdrawals and debt.
11.2 Misstatement of age or sex will adjust policy benefits accordingly.
11.3 We do not guarantee the tax treatment of policy loans or withdrawals.

12. FREE LOOK AND CONTESTABILITY
12.1 This policy may be returned within 30 days after delivery for a full
     refund of premiums paid.
12.2 This policy is incontestable after 2 years during the insured's life,
     except for nonpayment and rider-specific exceptions permitted by law.
