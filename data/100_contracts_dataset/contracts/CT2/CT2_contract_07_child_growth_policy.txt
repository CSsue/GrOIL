4. DEFINITIONS
"Juvenile Whole Life" means whole life insurance issued on the life of a
minor.
"Ownership Transfer Date" means the policy anniversary following the
insured's 21st birthday unless the current owner elects otherwise.
"Guaranteed Purchase Option" means the right to buy additional coverage on
specified dates without new evidence of insurability.
"Payor" means the person responsible for premium payments.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy is designed for lifetime insurability and future ownership
    transfer to the insured.
5.2 Premiums are scheduled only until the insured reaches age 25.
5.3 This policy is participating and may pay annual dividends if declared.
5.4 A Payor Benefit Rider is attached and waives premiums if the payor dies
    or becomes totally disabled before the insured reaches age 21.
5.5 Guaranteed Purchase Options are available at ages 25, 28, 31, and 34.

6. CASH VALUE AND DIVIDENDS
6.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates according to the policy value table.
6.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $5,900
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 20: $18,700
6.3 Dividends may be taken in cash, applied to premium, or used to purchase
    paid-up additions.
6.4 Current dividend option: Premium Reduction.

7. PREMIUMS, GRACE PERIOD, AND OWNERSHIP TRANSFER
7.1 Premiums are payable annually in advance.
7.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
7.3 On the Ownership Transfer Date, all policy rights automatically vest in
    the insured unless the owner elects to retain ownership as permitted by
    law and company rules.

8. POLICY LOANS
8.1 Loans are available after the policy has been in force for 3 years.
8.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of cash surrender value.
8.3 Loan interest rate is 5% per annum.

9. DEATH BENEFIT
9.1 Upon proof of death, we will pay the beneficiary:
    Base Face Amount
    + Paid-Up Additions
    - Policy Debt
9.2 After transfer of ownership to the insured, beneficiary changes may be
    made only by the insured as owner.

10. NON-FORFEITURE AND REINSTATEMENT
10.1 After 3 full years of premium payments, the owner may elect cash
     surrender, reduced paid-up insurance, or extended term insurance.
10.2 If no election is made after lapse, reduced paid-up insurance will apply.
10.3 Reinstatement is available within 5 years after lapse upon evidence of
     insurability and payment of overdue premiums with interest.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits our liability to
     premiums paid.
11.2 Misstatement of age will adjust the amount of insurance to the amount
     the premium would have purchased at the correct age.
11.3 The Guaranteed Purchase Option is unavailable if the policy is in lapse
     or reduced paid-up status on the option date.

12. FREE LOOK AND CONTESTABILITY
12.1 This policy may be returned within 20 days after delivery for a full
     refund of premiums paid.
12.2 This policy is incontestable after 2 years during the insured's
     lifetime, except for nonpayment or fraud.
