4. KEY DEFINITIONS
"Cash Value" means the guaranteed reserve accumulated under this policy,
plus any dividend additions and less any indebtedness.
"Paid-Up Additions" means additional whole life insurance purchased by
dividends or rider premiums.
"Policy Anniversary" means each annual anniversary of the Effective Date.
"Owner" means the person entitled to exercise all policy rights while the
insured is living.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy is issued as a participating whole life contract and is
    eligible for annual dividends if declared.
5.2 The death benefit consists of the Base Face Amount plus any Paid-Up
    Additions, subject to policy deductions.
5.3 This policy includes an optional Guaranteed Insurability Rider allowing
    the owner to purchase additional coverage on specified option dates
    without new evidence of insurability.
5.4 Option dates occur at ages 46, 49, 52, 55, and 58.
5.5 Maximum additional coverage under the rider on any option date is
    $50,000.

6. CASH VALUE AND DIVIDENDS
6.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates according to the policy value table.
6.2 Dividends are not guaranteed and depend on mortality, persistency,
    investment, and expense experience.
6.3 Dividend options are:
    Cash Payment
    Premium Reduction
    Accumulate at Interest
    Paid-Up Additions
    [Current selection: Paid-Up Additions]
6.4 Dividend accumulations left on deposit earn interest at a rate declared
    by us, which may change from time to time.
6.5 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $36,900
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 20: $92,100

7. PREMIUMS
7.1 Premiums are payable in advance on each policy anniversary.
7.2 A grace period of 31 days applies to each premium after the first.
7.3 If a premium remains unpaid at the end of the grace period, this policy
    will lapse subject to any available non-forfeiture benefit.

8. POLICY LOANS
8.1 Loans are available after the first policy year.
8.2 The maximum loan amount is 92% of the cash surrender value.
8.3 Loan interest accrues at 4.75% per annum.
8.4 Unpaid interest is added to the loan balance on each policy anniversary.
8.5 If total indebtedness equals or exceeds the cash value, the policy will
    terminate after 31 days' notice unless sufficient payment is made.

9. DEATH BENEFIT
9.1 Upon receipt of due proof of death, we will pay:
    Base Face Amount
    + Paid-Up Additions
    + Dividend Accumulations
    - Policy Debt
9.2 If any beneficiary dies before the insured, that beneficiary's share
    will pass per stirpes to that beneficiary's descendants unless the owner
    later changes the designation.
9.3 Settlement may be made as a lump sum or under an interest settlement
    option elected by the owner.

10. NON-FORFEITURE OPTIONS
After three full years of premium payments, the owner may elect:
10.1 Cash Surrender
10.2 Reduced Paid-Up Insurance
10.3 Extended Term Insurance
10.4 Automatic Premium Loan, if elected by endorsement
10.5 If no election is made, Reduced Paid-Up Insurance will apply.

11. REINSTATEMENT
11.1 This policy may be reinstated within 5 years after lapse.
11.2 Reinstatement requires:
     (a) evidence of insurability satisfactory to us;
     (b) payment of overdue premiums with 5% interest; and
     (c) repayment or reinstatement of all policy debt.

12. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
12.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits our liability to
     premiums paid less any indebtedness.
12.2 Misstatement of age or sex will result in adjustment of benefits to the
     amount the premium would have purchased at the correct age and sex.

13. FREE LOOK AND CONTESTABILITY
13.1 This policy may be returned within 20 days after delivery for a full
     refund of premiums paid.
13.2 We may contest this policy only during the first 2 years while the
     insured is living, and only for material misrepresentation.
