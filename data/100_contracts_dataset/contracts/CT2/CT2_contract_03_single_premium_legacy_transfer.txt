4. DEFINITIONS
"Single Premium Whole Life" means whole life insurance funded by one
premium payment at issue.
"Modified Endowment Contract" or "MEC" means a contract treated under
federal tax law as a modified endowment contract.
"Net Amount at Risk" means the death benefit minus the cash value.
"Surrender Charge Period" means the 10-year period beginning on the
Effective Date during which a surrender charge may apply.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy is funded by a single premium and no additional scheduled
    premiums are required.
5.2 This policy is intended for legacy transfer and conservative cash value
    accumulation.
5.3 This policy is not participating and no dividends are payable.
5.4 This policy is classified as a Modified Endowment Contract.
5.5 Any loans, withdrawals, or assignments may have adverse tax consequences,
    and the owner should consult a tax adviser.

6. CASH VALUE AND PARTIAL SURRENDERS
6.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates according to the policy values table.
6.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 5: $142,300
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $158,200
6.3 Partial surrenders are permitted after policy year 2 in minimum amounts
    of $2,500.
6.4 A partial surrender reduces cash value and may reduce the death benefit.
6.5 A surrender charge applies during the first 10 policy years.

7. POLICY LOANS
7.1 Policy loans are available after the first policy anniversary.
7.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of net cash surrender value.
7.3 Loan interest accrues daily at 5.25% per annum.
7.4 Unpaid interest is capitalized on each policy anniversary.
7.5 Policy debt may cause the contract to lapse.

8. DEATH BENEFIT
8.1 Upon proof of death, we will pay the beneficiary:
    Base Face Amount
    - Partial Surrenders
    - Policy Debt
8.2 Death proceeds are payable as a lump sum unless another settlement option
    is on file.
8.3 If the beneficiary is a minor at the time of claim, we may pay to a court-
    appointed guardian or under a minor settlement endorsement.

9. NON-FORFEITURE
9.1 Because this is a fully paid policy, no premium lapse can occur from
    failure to pay scheduled premiums.
9.2 If policy debt causes the policy to terminate, the owner may elect before
    termination to surrender the policy for its net cash surrender value.
9.3 Reduced Paid-Up Insurance is not available because this policy is already
    fully paid-up at issue.

10. REINSTATEMENT
10.1 If the policy terminates due solely to policy debt, reinstatement may be
     requested within 2 years of termination.
10.2 Reinstatement requires repayment of debt, proof of insurability if
     requested by us, and restoration of surrender value adjustments.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits our liability to
     the single premium paid less any withdrawals and debt.
11.2 Misstatement of age or sex will adjust the death benefit to the amount
     the single premium would have purchased at correct values.

12. FREE LOOK, CONTESTABILITY, AND REPORTING
12.1 The owner may return this policy within 10 days after delivery for a
     full refund of the single premium.
12.2 This policy is incontestable after 2 years during the insured's life
     except for nonpayment of amounts due under a loan agreement.
12.3 We reserve the right to issue tax reporting required by law for any
     reportable distribution.
