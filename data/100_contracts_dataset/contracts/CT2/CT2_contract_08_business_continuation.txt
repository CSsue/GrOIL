4. DEFINITIONS
"Business Continuation Whole Life" means whole life insurance intended to
fund a buy-sell arrangement or key-person continuity plan.
"Entity Purchase Agreement" means a business agreement under which the owner
entity may use proceeds to redeem or purchase an ownership interest.
"Key Person Disability Waiver Rider" means a rider that waives premiums if
the insured becomes totally disabled as defined in the rider.
"Restricted Assignment" means an assignment permitted only to a lender
approved by the owner entity.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy is designed for business continuation planning.
5.2 Premiums are payable for 15 years only.
5.3 This policy is non-participating.
5.4 The Buy-Sell Funding Endorsement restricts beneficiary changes without
    the written consent of the owner entity.
5.5 A Key Person Disability Waiver Rider is attached.

6. CASH VALUE
6.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates according to the policy value table.
6.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $63,500
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 15: $118,900
6.3 Cash value may be used by the owner entity as collateral subject to any

7. PREMIUMS AND GRACE PERIOD
7.1 Monthly premiums are payable by electronic transfer.
7.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
7.3 If the insured becomes totally disabled as defined in the rider, future
    premiums falling due during the period of approved disability will be
    waived.

8. POLICY LOANS, ASSIGNMENTS, AND ACCESS
8.1 Loans are available after the first policy year.
8.2 Maximum loan amount is 85% of cash surrender value.
8.3 Loan interest rate is 5.75% per annum.
8.4 No loan, partial surrender, or beneficiary change may be made by the
    insured unless also the owner.
8.5 Any restricted assignment on file will take priority over unsecured
    owner requests to the extent of the assignee's interest.

9. DEATH BENEFIT
9.1 Upon proof of death, we will pay the owner-beneficiary the Base Face
    Amount less policy debt and subject to assignment.
9.2 Proceeds are intended to support obligations under the owner entity's
    business continuation or buy-sell agreement, but we are not responsible
    for administration of that agreement.

10. NON-FORFEITURE AND REINSTATEMENT
10.1 After 3 full years, the owner may elect cash surrender, reduced paid-up
     insurance, or automatic premium loan if elected by endorsement.
10.2 Extended term insurance is not available.
10.3 Reinstatement is available within 3 years after lapse upon evidence of
     insurability and payment of overdue amounts with 5% interest.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits our liability to
     premiums paid less debt.
11.2 Misstatement of age or sex adjusts values accordingly.
11.3 The company is not bound by any buy-sell agreement not filed with us.

12. FREE LOOK AND ENTIRE CONTRACT
12.1 This policy may be returned within 15 days after delivery for a full
     refund of premiums paid.
12.2 This policy, endorsements, riders, and application constitute the
     entire contract.
