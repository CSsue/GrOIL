4. DEFINITIONS
"Joint Survivorship Whole Life" means whole life insurance covering two
lives with death proceeds payable upon the death of the last surviving insured.
"First Death" means the earlier death of the two insureds.
"Second Death" means the later death of the two insureds.
"Survivorship Period" means the period after First Death and before Second
Death during which policy administration continues.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy insures two lives and pays the death benefit only upon the
    Second Death.
5.2 No death benefit is payable upon the First Death unless a rider provides
    otherwise.
5.3 A First-to-Die Premium Waiver Rider is attached. Following First Death,
    all future premiums are waived while the policy remains in force.
5.4 This policy is participating and may pay dividends if declared.

6. CASH VALUE AND DIVIDENDS
6.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates according to the policy values table.
6.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $120,800
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 20: $309,600
6.3 Dividends, if declared, may be taken in cash, left to accumulate, or
    used to purchase paid-up additions.
6.4 Current dividend option: Accumulate at Interest.

7. PREMIUMS AND GRACE PERIOD
7.1 Semiannual premiums are payable in advance.
7.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
7.3 Following First Death, no further premium is required due to the attached
    rider, but policy debt continues to accrue interest if outstanding.

8. POLICY LOANS
8.1 Loans are available after the first policy year.
8.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of cash surrender value.
8.3 Loan interest rate is 5% per annum, compounded annually.
8.4 During the Survivorship Period, only the owner may exercise loan rights.

9. DEATH BENEFIT
9.1 Upon Second Death, we will pay:
    Base Face Amount
    + Paid-Up Additions
    + Dividend Accumulations
    - Policy Debt
9.2 Upon First Death, we will endorse the policy to reflect continuation on
    the life of the surviving insured only for administrative purposes while
    preserving the second-to-die benefit structure.
9.3 Settlement is payable to the trust beneficiary designated herein.

10. NON-FORFEITURE AND REINSTATEMENT
10.1 After 3 full policy years, the owner may elect cash surrender or
     reduced paid-up survivorship insurance.
10.2 Extended term insurance is not available.
10.3 Reinstatement is available within 5 years after lapse upon evidence of
     insurability of the surviving insured if First Death has occurred, or of
     both insureds if both are living at reinstatement.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 If both insureds die by suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date,
     our liability is limited to premiums paid less indebtedness.
11.2 If one insured dies by suicide within 2 years and the other dies later,
     the policy remains in force subject to all other terms.
11.3 Misstatement of age of either insured will adjust values using the
     correct issue ages.

12. FREE LOOK, CONTESTABILITY, AND ASSIGNMENT
12.1 The owner may return this policy within 30 days after delivery for a
     full refund of premiums paid.
12.2 This policy is incontestable after 2 years during the lifetime of both
     insureds, except for nonpayment or fraud.
12.3 Assignment by the trust owner must be in writing and accepted by us.
