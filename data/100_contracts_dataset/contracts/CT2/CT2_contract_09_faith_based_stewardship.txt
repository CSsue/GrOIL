4. DEFINITIONS
"Charitable Benefit" means an additional death benefit payable to the named
charitable organization that does not reduce the Base Face Amount payable to
the primary beneficiary.
"Participating Policy" means a policy eligible for dividends when declared.
"Stewardship Dividend Option" means a dividend option under which declared
dividends are automatically donated to a designated charitable organization.
"Approved Charity" means an organization accepted by us under company rules.

5. POLICY DESIGN
5.1 This policy is a participating whole life policy with an attached
    Charitable Benefit Endorsement.
5.2 The Charitable Supplemental Beneficiary is entitled to an additional 1%
    of the Base Face Amount at the insured's death, up to $10,000.
5.3 The charitable supplemental amount is provided by endorsement and does
    not reduce the amount payable to other beneficiaries.
5.4 Dividend options are:
    Cash Payment
    Premium Reduction
    Paid-Up Additions
    Stewardship Dividend Option
    [Current selection: Stewardship Dividend Option]

6. CASH VALUE AND DIVIDENDS
6.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates according to the policy values table.
6.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $41,600
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 20: $102,700
6.3 Dividends are not guaranteed and depend on company experience.
6.4 Under the Stewardship Dividend Option, any declared dividend is remitted
    to the designated Approved Charity unless the owner changes the option.

7. PREMIUMS, GRACE PERIOD, AND CHARITY DESIGNATION
7.1 Premiums are payable annually in advance.
7.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
7.3 The owner may change the charitable designation only to another Approved
    Charity and only while the policy is in force.

8. POLICY LOANS
8.1 Loans are available after the first policy year.
8.2 Maximum loan amount is 90% of cash surrender value.
8.3 Loan interest rate is 4.9% per annum.
8.4 Policy debt reduces the Base Face Amount and any related policy values,
    but does not reduce the separate charitable supplemental percentage
    unless required by law.

9. DEATH BENEFIT
9.1 Upon proof of death, we will pay:
    Base Face Amount
    + Paid-Up Additions
    - Policy Debt
to the named beneficiary or beneficiaries.
9.2 We will also pay the Charitable Benefit amount directly to the named
    charitable organization if it remains an Approved Charity at the time
    of claim.

10. NON-FORFEITURE AND REINSTATEMENT
10.1 Available non-forfeiture options are cash surrender, reduced paid-up
     insurance, and extended term insurance.
10.2 If no election is made, reduced paid-up insurance will apply.
10.3 Reinstatement is available within 5 years after lapse upon evidence of
     insurability and payment of overdue premiums with 5% interest.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
11.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits our liability to
     premiums paid less any debt.
11.2 Misstatement of age adjusts the amount of insurance.
11.3 If the designated charity is not an Approved Charity at the time of
     claim, the charitable benefit will be paid to the owner's estate unless
     the owner has designated a successor Approved Charity.

12. FREE LOOK AND CONTESTABILITY
12.1 This policy may be returned within 20 days after delivery for a full
     refund of premiums paid.
12.2 This policy is incontestable after 2 years during the insured's life
     except for nonpayment and fraud.
