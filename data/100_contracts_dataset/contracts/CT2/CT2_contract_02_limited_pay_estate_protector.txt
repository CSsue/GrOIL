4. KEY DEFINITIONS
"Limited Pay Whole Life" means whole life insurance for which premiums are
scheduled to end after a fixed number of years while coverage continues for
life.
"Trust Owner" means the trustee acting on behalf of the named trust owner.
"Net Cash Surrender Value" means cash value less any surrender charge and
policy debt.
"Endowment Maturity" means the date on which the policy matures at age 121.

5. POLICY DESIGN AND PURPOSE
5.1 This policy is designed for estate liquidity planning.
5.2 Premiums are payable for 20 policy years only.
5.3 After the last scheduled premium is paid, coverage continues without
    further premium, subject to policy terms.
5.4 This is a non-participating policy and does not pay dividends.
5.5 An Estate Preservation Rider is attached and provides an accelerated
    underwriting review process for trust-owned policy increases requested
    within the first 5 policy years, subject to company approval.

6. CASH VALUE
6.1 Guaranteed cash value accumulates according to the policy values table.
6.2 Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 10: $88,400
    Guaranteed cash value at end of Year 20: $216,500
6.3 A surrender charge applies during the first 12 policy years and declines
    annually to zero thereafter.
6.4 Net cash surrender value equals the cash value less any surrender charge
    and less policy debt.

7. PREMIUMS AND GRACE PERIOD
7.1 Premiums are payable annually in advance.
7.2 A 31-day grace period applies after the first premium.
7.3 If premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, the policy will
    lapse unless continued under a non-forfeiture provision.

8. POLICY LOAN AND COLLATERAL ASSIGNMENT
8.1 Loans are available after the end of policy year 3.
8.2 Maximum loan amount is 85% of the net cash surrender value.
8.3 Loan interest rate is 6% per annum, payable in arrears.
8.4 A collateral assignment filed by the owner will take priority over
    beneficiary claims to the extent of the secured interest.
8.5 Any indebtedness will reduce death proceeds and surrender values.

9. DEATH BENEFIT
9.1 Upon proof of death, we will pay the beneficiary the Base Face Amount
    less policy debt, subject to any assignment.
9.2 Death proceeds may be paid to the trust owner in a single sum or under
    an interest settlement option elected by the trustee.
9.3 No death benefit will be paid if this policy has terminated before the
    insured's death.

10. NON-FORFEITURE OPTIONS
After 3 full years of premium payments, the owner may elect:
10.1 Cash Surrender
10.2 Reduced Paid-Up Insurance
10.3 Extended Term Insurance
10.4 If no election is made, Cash Surrender Value will automatically be
     placed in Extended Term Insurance.

11. REINSTATEMENT
11.1 This policy may be reinstated within 3 years after lapse.
11.2 Reinstatement requires evidence of insurability, payment of overdue
     premiums with 5.5% interest, and repayment or reinstatement of policy
     loans.

12. EXCLUSIONS AND POLICY LIMITATIONS
12.1 Suicide within 2 years from the Effective Date limits payment to total
     premiums paid less any unpaid loan interest.
12.2 Misstatement of age will adjust the face amount to the amount the paid
     premium would have purchased at the correct age.
12.3 Any increase approved under the Estate Preservation Rider will have its
     own contestability and suicide period from the date of increase.

13. FREE LOOK AND ENTIRE CONTRACT
13.1 The owner may cancel this policy within 30 days after delivery for a
     full refund of premiums paid.
13.2 This policy, the application, and any attached riders constitute the
     entire contract.
