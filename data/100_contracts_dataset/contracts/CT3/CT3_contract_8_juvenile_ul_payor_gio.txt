═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Minor Insured" means the Insured while under the age 
    of majority in the state of policy issue.

1.2 "Age of Majority" means age 18 in the state of issue.

1.3 "Payor" means the person obligated to pay premiums, 
    being the Owner as listed on the Policy Data Page.

1.4 "Guaranteed Insurability Option" (GIO) means the 
    right granted under the GIO Rider to purchase 
    additional insurance without evidence of insurability.

1.5 "Account Value" means the accumulation of net premiums 
    with credited interest less monthly charges.

1.6 "Ownership Transfer Date" means the date, after the 
    Insured attains age 18, that the Owner executes 
    a written transfer of ownership to the Insured.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - PREMIUM PAYMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums may be paid at any time:
    Minimum: $25/payment
    Annual maximum: $25,000 (MEC limits for face amount)

2.2 Premium expense load: 3.0% of each premium.

2.3 The Payor (Owner) pays all premiums. The Minor Insured 
    has no obligation to pay premiums.

2.4 Upon Ownership Transfer to the adult Insured, 
    the new Owner assumes full premium responsibility.

2.5 Premiums are flexible; skipping is permitted as long 
    as Account Value covers monthly deductions.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 PAYOR WAIVER OF PREMIUM RIDER (Form CFL-PWP-R):
    If the Payor (Owner/Mother) becomes totally disabled 
    for more than 6 consecutive months, or dies, all 
    monthly premiums are waived until the earlier of:
    (a) The Payor's recovery from disability
    (b) The Payor's death — in which case waiver 
        continues until the Insured attains age 25
    (c) The Insured's age 25

    "Total Disability" means the inability to perform 
    any occupation for remuneration, as defined in 
    rider form CFL-PWP-R.

    Monthly waiver benefit = planned premium ($75/month).
    Elimination period: 6 consecutive months.
    No additional premium is charged for this rider 
    for the first 10 policy years.

3.2 GUARANTEED INSURABILITY OPTION RIDER (Form CFL-GIO-R):
    On each GIO Option Date, the Insured may purchase 
    additional life insurance (on a new policy) up to 
    $50,000 without evidence of insurability.

    GIO Option Dates:
    - Upon Insured attaining age 18, 21, 25, 28, and 31
    - Upon Insured's marriage
    - Upon birth or legal adoption of Insured's first child
    - 30 days before and after each option date

    Maximum additional insurance per option: $50,000
    GIO options not exercised lapse; no rollover.
    GIO expires when Insured attains age 35.
    Rider premium: $2.50/month.

3.3 CHILDREN'S TERM RIDER (Form CFL-CTR-R):
    Not applicable (Insured IS the child).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 On the 1st of each month, we deduct:
    (a) Cost of Insurance charge (juvenile rates — 
        very low due to Insured's young age)
    (b) Administrative fee: $5.00/month
    (c) GIO Rider charge: $2.50/month
    (d) Outstanding loan interest

4.2 Juvenile COI rates are based on 2017 CSO Table, 
    age 5, female, standard class. Rates increase 
    as the Insured ages, per the attached schedule.

4.3 Because juvenile COI rates are very low, Account 
    Value growth in early years is dominated by 
    interest crediting, not COI drag.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - INTEREST CREDITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 Interest is credited monthly.
    Guaranteed minimum: 2.00% per annum
    Current rate: 4.00% per annum

5.2 The long accumulation period (policy designed to 
    build value over 15-20+ years) means small rate 
    differences compound significantly. The 
    guaranteed minimum is the only rate we promise.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 If the Minor Insured dies, the death benefit 
    ($100,000) is paid to the Owner (Payor/Mother).

6.2 STATE LAW COMPLIANCE: Some states limit or restrict 
    the amount of life insurance that may be placed 
    on a minor child. This policy's face amount 
    complies with all applicable state regulations 
    for the state of issue.

6.3 Death proceeds are not subject to income tax 
    (IRC §101(a)).

6.4 After Ownership Transfer: beneficiary designation 
    governs per Article 1.6 and current beneficiary 
    form on file.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Minimum withdrawal: $500
    Maximum: Account Value less $1,000 minimum reserve

7.2 OWNERSHIP RESTRICTION: Only the Owner (Payor/Parent) 
    may exercise withdrawal rights while the Insured 
    is a minor. After Ownership Transfer, only the 
    new Owner (adult Insured) may exercise such rights.

7.3 Withdrawal fee: $15/withdrawal, years 1-5; 
    no charge thereafter.

7.4 Withdrawals reduce Account Value and may reduce 
    available GIO election amounts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Maximum loan: 90% of Account Value

8.2 Loan interest rate: Variable — Prime Rate + 1.0%, 
    minimum 4.0%, adjusted annually.

8.3 Only the Owner (Payor) may request a loan while 
    the Insured is a minor.

8.4 Outstanding loans reduce death benefit dollar-for-dollar 
    and are disclosed in annual statements.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - SURRENDER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge 
    - Outstanding Loans

9.2 Surrender Charge Schedule (% of Specified Amount):
    Year 1: 9% | Year 2: 8% | Year 3: 7% | Year 4: 6%
    Year 5: 5% | Year 6: 4% | Year 7: 3% | Year 8: 2%
    Year 9: 1% | Year 10+: 0%

9.3 OWNERSHIP TRANSFER NOTE: If the Owner transfers 
    ownership to the Insured and the Insured later 
    surrenders the policy, no additional surrender 
    charge reset occurs due to the transfer.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Does not apply to Minor Insureds. 
     If the adult Insured (post-age 18) dies by suicide 
     within 24 months of an increase in Specified Amount, 
     the increase only is subject to the suicide 
     exclusion (return of premiums for that increase).

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest within 2 years of 
     issue based on misrepresentations by the Owner 
     regarding the Insured's health history.

10.3 INSURABLE INTEREST: The Owner represents that 
     an insurable interest exists in the Minor Insured's 
     life at the time of application and policy issue.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 - GRACE PERIOD & LAPSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 If Account Value is insufficient to cover monthly 
     deductions, we notify the Owner. A 61-day grace 
     period follows. Payor Waiver Rider may cover 
     any premiums due during this period.

11.2 Reinstatement: Within 5 years of lapse, on evidence 
     of good health of the Insured and payment of 
     overdue charges plus 6% interest.
