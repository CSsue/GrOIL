ATTACHED RIDER (INCORPORATED BY REFERENCE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
LONG-TERM CARE ACCELERATION BENEFIT RIDER (Form SBL-LTC-R)
This rider is governed by NAIC Long-Term Care Model Act 
provisions as adopted in the state of issue.

LTC Benefit Pool: $250,000 (equal to Specified Amount)
Monthly LTC Benefit Maximum: 2% of original Specified Amount 
                              = $5,000/month
Elimination Period: 90 days of qualifying care services
Inflation Protection: 3% Simple on monthly maximum

QUALIFYING TRIGGERS (any 2 of 6 ADLs or severe cognitive 
impairment, as certified by a licensed healthcare practitioner):
ADLs: Bathing, Dressing, Eating, Continence, 
      Toileting, Transferring

Qualifying Care Settings:
  - Licensed nursing facility
  - Assisted living facility (state licensed)
  - Home health care (minimum 3 visits per week)
  - Adult day care center

Tax Treatment: LTC benefits paid under this rider are intended 
to qualify as tax-free under IRC §7702B(a). Insured should 
consult a tax advisor.

Benefit Reduction: Each dollar of LTC benefit paid reduces 
the Specified Amount and LTC Benefit Pool dollar-for-dollar. 
When the LTC Benefit Pool is exhausted, the policy terminates.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Account Value" means the single premium paid, plus 
    credited interest, less all charges and distributions.

1.2 "Option C (Return of Premium Enhanced) Death Benefit" 
    means the greater of: (a) the Specified Amount, or 
    (b) the greater of the total premiums paid or 
    Account Value × 1.10.

1.3 "LTC Benefit Pool" means the total amount available for 
    long-term care acceleration, equal to the original 
    Specified Amount reduced by prior LTC claims paid.

1.4 "Elimination Period" means the waiting period of 
    continuous qualifying care before LTC benefits begin.

1.5 "ADL" means Activity of Daily Living.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - SINGLE PREMIUM DESIGN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 This policy is funded by a single lump-sum premium. 
    No further premiums are required or expected.

2.2 Additional premiums are not accepted after the issue date 
    without prior underwriting approval and may not be 
    accepted if they would alter MEC status undesirably.

2.3 The entire single premium is allocated to the general 
    account upon receipt. A premium expense load of 1.0% 
    is deducted before crediting.

2.4 Because no ongoing premiums are required, there is 
    no planned premium, target premium, or minimum 
    monthly premium applicable to this policy.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 On the 20th of each month, we deduct from Account Value:
    (a) Cost of Insurance charge
    (b) LTC Rider charge: $18.50/month (guaranteed 
        not to exceed $31.00/month through age 85)
    (c) Administrative fee: $7.00/month

3.2 COI is calculated as:
    (Specified Amount Remaining - Account Value) × 
    COI Rate / 1,000

3.3 As Specified Amount is reduced by LTC claims, the 
    net amount at risk — and therefore the COI — 
    decreases accordingly.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 - INTEREST CREDITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Interest is credited monthly.
    Guaranteed minimum rate: 3.00% per annum
    Current credited rate: 4.50% per annum

4.2 The single-premium design allows us to offer a higher 
    guaranteed minimum than multi-pay products.

4.3 Current rate is declared annually each January 1 
    and applies to all Account Value throughout that year.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 OPTION C (Return of Premium Enhanced) — In Force
    Death Benefit = greatest of:
    (a) Current Specified Amount (reduced by LTC claims)
    (b) Total single premium paid: $95,000
    (c) Account Value × 1.10

5.2 If LTC benefits have been paid, the death benefit is 
    reduced by total LTC benefits paid. The minimum 
    death benefit is 10% of original Specified Amount 
    ($25,000) so long as the LTC Benefit Pool is not 
    fully exhausted.

5.3 Death benefit proceeds are paid within 14 business days 
    of receipt of certified death certificate and completed 
    claim form.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - LONG-TERM CARE CLAIM PROCEDURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 To initiate LTC benefits, the Owner must submit:
    (a) Written claim form (Form SBL-LTC-C1)
    (b) Attending physician statement certifying ADL 
        impairments or cognitive impairment
    (c) Plan of care from licensed healthcare practitioner
    (d) Proof of qualifying care setting

6.2 We will respond to a complete claim within 10 business 
    days with a benefit determination.

6.3 Monthly LTC benefits are paid directly to Owner or 
    to the licensed care provider at Owner's election.

6.4 Concurrent Care Review: We may require re-certification 
    of qualifying status every 12 months.

6.5 If the Insured recovers and no longer meets qualifying 
    criteria, LTC benefits cease. Remaining LTC Benefit 
    Pool is preserved for future qualifying periods.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Minimum withdrawal: $1,000

7.2 TAX WARNING (MEC): All withdrawals are treated as 
    income first under IRC §72(e). Withdrawals before 
    age 59½ may incur a 10% federal penalty tax.

7.3 Withdrawal charges: None (single-pay policy, 
    no surrender charge applies after year 5).

7.4 Surrender Charge Schedule:
    Year 1: 5% | Year 2: 4% | Year 3: 3%
    Year 4: 2% | Year 5: 1% | Year 6+: 0%

7.5 Withdrawals reduce Account Value and may reduce 
    the death benefit under Option C.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Maximum loan: 90% of Account Value

8.2 Loan interest rate: 4.00% per annum, fixed.

8.3 TAX WARNING (MEC): Policy loans from a MEC are 
    treated as distributions and are taxable as 
    ordinary income to the extent of gain in the policy, 
    and may be subject to a 10% federal penalty tax 
    before age 59½.

8.4 Outstanding loan reduces death benefit dollar-for-dollar 
    and is also excluded from the LTC Benefit Pool balance.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Death by suicide within 24 months: 
    proceeds limited to premiums paid, without interest.

9.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest within 2 years of issue.

9.3 LTC EXCLUSIONS: No LTC benefits are payable for care:
    (a) Resulting from intentionally self-inflicted injury
    (b) Caused by alcoholism or drug addiction
    (c) Provided by an immediate family member 
        (unless a licensed care provider)
    (d) Outside the United States or its territories 
        (except emergency care up to 30 days per year)
    (e) Resulting from an act of war (declared or undeclared)

9.4 PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS: LTC benefits for conditions 
    that were diagnosed or treated in the 12 months before 
    issue are subject to a 6-month waiting period from 
    the issue date.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - CONTINUATION & LAPSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 Given the single-premium design, lapse due to 
     nonpayment is unlikely. However, if Account Value 
     is reduced (by charges, LTC benefits, or withdrawals) 
     to an amount insufficient to cover 2 months of 
     monthly deductions, we notify the Owner.

10.2 Owner has 61 days from notice to submit a premium 
     payment. No additional premiums are guaranteed 
     to be accepted without evidence of insurability.

10.3 Upon full exhaustion of the LTC Benefit Pool, 
     the LTC Rider terminates. The base policy 
     remains in force on the residual death benefit 
     of 10% of the original Specified Amount.
