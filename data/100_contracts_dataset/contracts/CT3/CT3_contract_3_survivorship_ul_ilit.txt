═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Last Surviving Insured" means the Insured who dies after 
    the other Insured.

1.2 "First Death" means the death of the first Insured to die.

1.3 "Account Value" means the total of accumulated premiums, 
    plus credited interest, less monthly charges since issue.

1.4 "Waiver Period" means the period following First Death 
    during which certain charges are modified (see Article 3).

1.5 "COI" means the joint Cost of Insurance charge, 
    calculated on the basis of both Insureds' ages and 
    mortality rates.

1.6 "Surrender Charge" means the charge applicable if the 
    policy is surrendered within 20 years of issue.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - PREMIUM PAYMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums may be paid at any frequency and amount, subject 
    to:
    Minimum per payment: $500
    Annual maximum: $750,000 (MEC limits apply — see §2.5)
    Additional premium may require evidence of insurability.

2.2 A premium expense load of 2.5% applies to each premium.

2.3 Crummey withdrawal rights: This policy is designed to be 
    owned by an ILIT. Premium payments by the Grantors may 
    qualify for the gift tax annual exclusion only if the 
    Trust instrument contains valid Crummey withdrawal 
    powers. We make no representation as to the tax 
    treatment of contributions. Consult an estate planning 
    attorney.

2.4 After First Death, the premium payment obligation may 
    be modified as described in Article 3.

2.5 MODIFIED ENDOWMENT CONTRACT (MEC): If premiums exceed 
    the 7-pay limit under IRC §7702A, this policy becomes 
    a MEC. MEC status does not affect the death benefit 
    but subjects loans and withdrawals to income tax and 
    possible 10% penalty. We will notify you if a premium 
    would cause MEC status.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - POST-FIRST-DEATH PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Upon First Death, the surviving Insured's name, DOB, 
    and risk class become the sole basis for ongoing COI 
    calculations.

3.2 PREMIUM WAIVER ON FIRST DEATH (included at no 
    additional charge): All monthly COI charges are waived 
    for 12 months following First Death, providing the 
    Owner premium relief during estate settlement.

3.3 After the 12-month waiver period, regular monthly 
    deductions resume based on the surviving Insured's 
    attained age and current COI rates.

3.4 The Owner retains full rights to make premium payments, 
    take loans, and make withdrawals after First Death.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 On the 1st of each month, we deduct from Account Value:
    (a) Joint COI charge (pre-First Death):
        Calculated using last-survivor mortality table for 
        both Insureds' ages simultaneously
    (b) Administrative fee: $15.00/month
    (c) Outstanding loan interest

4.2 Post-First Death COI (single life basis):
    COI = (Specified Amount - Account Value) × Monthly 
    COI Rate for surviving Insured / 1,000

4.3 COI rates are guaranteed not to exceed the maximum rates 
    in the attached Joint Last-Survivor COI Schedule.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - INTEREST CREDITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 Interest is credited daily.
    Guaranteed minimum rate: 2.25% per annum
    Current credited rate: 4.00% per annum

5.2 We may declare different rates for different account 
    value tiers:
    Tier 1 (up to $500,000 AV): Current rate
    Tier 2 ($500,001 - $2,000,000): Current rate + 0.25%
    Tier 3 (above $2,000,000): Current rate + 0.50%

5.3 Tiered rates are subject to change, but the guaranteed 
    minimums apply to all tiers.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 The death benefit is payable only upon the death of the 
    Last Surviving Insured.

6.2 No benefit is paid upon First Death. The policy 
    continues in force following First Death.

6.3 Death Benefit = Specified Amount (Level Option A)
    Minimum: greater of Specified Amount or 101% of 
    Account Value at time of Last Surviving Insured's death.

6.4 Death benefit is reduced by outstanding policy loans.

6.5 In the event both Insureds die in a common accident or 
    within 24 hours of each other, the Last Surviving 
    Insured is deemed to be the Insured with the greater 
    Account Value allocation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Minimum withdrawal: $1,000
    Maximum per year: 10% of Account Value (greater of 
    greater than any applicable surrender charge)

7.2 No withdrawal fee in any policy year.

7.3 Withdrawals reduce Account Value. If Account Value 
    falls below total of 12 months of projected monthly 
    deductions, we will notify the Trustee.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Maximum loan: 80% of Account Value
    (Reduced from 90% given estate planning context and 
    complexity of Trustee borrowing authority)

8.2 Loan interest: Fixed 5.00% per annum

8.3 Loan interest credited on collateral: 4.00% per annum
    Net cost of borrowing: 1.00% per annum

8.4 TRUSTEE CERTIFICATION: Any loan request must be 
    accompanied by a certification from the Trustee that 
    the loan is authorized under the Trust instrument and 
    applicable state law. We are not responsible for the 
    validity of Trustee borrowing authority.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - SURRENDER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge - 
    Outstanding Loans

9.2 Surrender Charge Schedule (% of Specified Amount):
    Year 1-2:  9% | Year 3-4:  7% | Year 5-6:  5%
    Year 7-8:  4% | Year 9-10: 3% | Year 11-15: 2%
    Year 16-20: 1% | Year 21+: 0%

9.3 The extended 20-year schedule reflects the long-term 
    estate planning nature of this product.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: If either Insured dies by suicide 
     within 24 months of issue, the death benefit is 
     replaced by a return of Account Value only if that 
     Insured is the Last Surviving Insured. First Death 
     by suicide has no effect on policy terms.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: Each Insured's application may be 
     contested separately within 2 years of issue.

10.3 COMMUNITY PROPERTY: If the Insureds reside in a 
     community property state, community property rights 
     may affect policy ownership and beneficiary 
     designations. We recommend consultation with 
     an estate attorney.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 - GRACE PERIOD & LAPSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 If Account Value is insufficient to cover monthly 
     deductions, we notify the Trustee and the policy 
     enters a 61-day grace period.

11.2 Lapse of a survivorship policy may have unintended 
     estate planning consequences. We strongly encourage 
     the Trustee to notify the Grantors immediately 
     of any grace period notice.

11.3 Reinstatement: within 5 years of lapse with evidence 
     of insurability of the surviving Insured (if 
     applicable) and payment of overdue charges.
