SPLIT-DOLLAR DISCLOSURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This policy is issued in connection with a Split-Dollar Life 
Insurance Arrangement between the Employer (policy owner) and 
the Employee/Insured. The arrangement is documented in a 
separate Split-Dollar Agreement dated November 4, 2024, which 
governs allocation of premium payments, policy rights, and 
death benefit between the parties.

Split-Dollar Regime: Endorsement Method (Employer owns policy; 
Employee's interest endorsed per agreement).

Tax Guidance: This arrangement is subject to Treasury 
Regulations §1.61-22 and §1.7872-15. The economic benefit 
reportable by the Employee is the cost of current life 
insurance protection allocated to the Employee under the 
split-dollar agreement. Employer and Employee should consult 
independent tax counsel.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Account Value" means accumulated premiums net 
    of expenses, plus credited interest, less monthly 
    deductions.

1.2 "Split-Dollar Agreement" means the separate agreement 
    between Employer and Employee governing their 
    respective rights in this policy.

1.3 "Employer's Recoverable Amount" means the greater 
    of (a) total premiums paid by Employer or (b) 
    the Account Value at any relevant date, per the 
    Split-Dollar Agreement.

1.4 "Employee's Family Benefit" means the death 
    benefit proceeds remaining after payment of the 
    Employer's Recoverable Amount.

1.5 "Rollout" means the termination of the split-dollar 
    arrangement and the transfer of policy ownership 
    to the Employee or Employee's designee.

1.6 "Economic Benefit" means the cost of the current 
    life insurance protection allocated to the Employee, 
    reportable as income under IRS PS-58 rates or 
    insurer's published alternative term rates.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - PREMIUM PAYMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums paid by Employer (Owner):
    Minimum: $5,000/payment
    Annual maximum: Governed by IRC §7702; this policy 
    is designed to remain below MEC thresholds.

2.2 Premium expense load: 2.0% of each premium 
    (reduced load reflecting large case economics).

2.3 The Employee does not pay premiums. The Employee 
    reports the economic benefit of the term insurance 
    coverage as imputed income on Form W-2.

2.4 Employee may elect to pay the economic benefit 
    cost directly to the Employer to avoid imputed 
    income, per the Split-Dollar Agreement. This does 
    not make the Employee a premium payer under 
    this policy.

2.5 Premiums are paid flexibly; Employer may pay 
    additional amounts above Target Premium subject 
    to IRC §7702 corridor limits.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 On the 4th of each month, we deduct from Account Value:
    (a) Cost of Insurance charge
    (b) Administrative fee: $25.00/month
    (c) Outstanding loan interest

3.2 COI Basis: 2017 CSO Preferred Plus Table; guaranteed 
    maximum rates per COI Schedule. Due to the large 
    face amount, net COI rates are highly competitive.

3.3 COI = (Option B Net Amount at Risk) × COI Rate / 1,000
    Option B Net Amount at Risk = Specified Amount 
    (Account Value is additive to death benefit, 
    so Specified Amount is always at risk)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 - INTEREST CREDITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Interest credited daily.
    Guaranteed minimum: 2.25% per annum
    Current rate: 5.10% per annum

4.2 LARGE CASE ENHANCEMENT: For Account Values above 
    $2,000,000, an additional 0.30% per annum 
    enhancement is credited (guaranteed minimum 
    enhancement: 0.10% per annum).

4.3 Current rate declared monthly; guaranteed minimum 
    applies always.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 OPTION B (Increasing) — In Force
    Total Death Benefit = Specified Amount + Account Value

5.2 Upon death, proceeds are bifurcated:
    (a) Employer receives: Employer's Recoverable Amount
    (b) Employee's Family receives: Remainder

5.3 Minimum Family Benefit per Rollout Agreement: 
    Subject to the Split-Dollar Agreement, a minimum 
    family benefit of $1,000,000 is guaranteed for 
    the first 10 years of the arrangement.

5.4 If death occurs after Rollout (ownership transferred 
    to Employee), all proceeds go to Employee's 
    designated beneficiaries.

5.5 COLI Income Tax Exclusion: Employer's share of 
    death proceeds is excludable from Employer's 
    gross income under IRC §101(a) if §101(j) 
    requirements are satisfied.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Only the Owner (Employer) may exercise withdrawal 
    rights. Employee has no unilateral withdrawal rights 
    under the Endorsement Method split-dollar arrangement.

6.2 Minimum withdrawal: $10,000
    Maximum: Account Value less Employer's Recoverable 
    Amount minimum balance as required by the 
    Split-Dollar Agreement.

6.3 No withdrawal fee.

6.4 Each withdrawal reduces Account Value and may 
    reduce the Employee's Family Benefit.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Maximum loan: 85% of Account Value

7.2 Loan interest rate: Applicable Federal Rate (AFR) 
    for mid-term loans, as published by the IRS monthly, 
    plus 0.50%, adjusted quarterly.

7.3 AFR is used to ensure the loan does not constitute 
    a below-market loan under IRC §7872, which could 
    trigger additional imputed income to the Employee.

7.4 Only the Owner (Employer) may request loans.

7.5 Loans are reflected in the Employer's Recoverable 
    Amount calculation under the Split-Dollar Agreement.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - ROLLOUT PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 "Rollout" refers to the termination of the 
    split-dollar arrangement and transfer of policy 
    ownership from Employer to Employee (or Employee's 
    designee, such as an ILIT).

8.2 Upon Rollout, the Employer is entitled to receive 
    the Employer's Recoverable Amount before or 
    simultaneously with ownership transfer.

8.3 Rollout triggers:
    (a) Scheduled rollout per Split-Dollar Agreement 
        (typically after 10-15 years)
    (b) Employee's termination of employment (all rights)
    (c) Employer's dissolution or insolvency
    (d) Mutual agreement of parties

8.4 TAX CONSEQUENCES OF ROLLOUT: If the policy is 
    transferred to the Employee for less than fair 
    market value, the difference may be includable 
    in the Employee's income. Consult tax counsel.

8.5 We will cooperate with ownership change requests 
    that satisfy our administrative requirements 
    and are accompanied by a copy of the Split-Dollar 
    Agreement and a signed transfer form.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - SURRENDER CHARGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge 
    - Outstanding Loans

9.2 Surrender Charge Schedule (% of Specified Amount):
    Year 1: 5% | Year 2: 4% | Year 3: 3%
    Year 4: 2% | Year 5: 1% | Year 6+: 0%

9.3 The shorter surrender charge period reflects 
    the large case / executive market design.

9.4 Upon Rollout, if Employer transfers the policy, 
    the surrender charge (if any) transfers with 
    the policy; the charge period does not reset.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Death by suicide within 24 months 
     of issue: Account Value paid to Employer 
     (as Recoverable Amount); no Family Benefit paid.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest within 2 years of 
     issue for material misrepresentation by Insured 
     in the application.

10.3 NO WAR OR AVIATION EXCLUSION: Neither war nor 
     aviation hazard exclusions apply to this policy. 
     The Insured's executive travel (including private 
     aviation) was disclosed and accepted at standard 
     Preferred Plus rates.

10.4 EMPLOYER INSOLVENCY: If Employer becomes insolvent, 
     this policy is a general asset of the Employer's 
     estate to the extent of the Employer's Recoverable 
     Amount. The Employee's Family Benefit may be at 
     risk. Employee is advised to monitor the 
     arrangement and consider requesting Rollout 
     if Employer's financial condition deteriorates.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 - CHRONIC ILLNESS ACCELERATION RIDER (ATTACHED)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 CHRONIC ILLNESS ACCELERATION RIDER (Form VEB-CIA-R):
     Benefit: Up to 2% of Specified Amount per month, 
              maximum $100,000/month, lifetime maximum 
              equal to Specified Amount
     Trigger: Permanent inability to perform 2+ ADLs 
              or severe cognitive impairment, 
              certified by licensed physician
     Waiting Period: 90 days of continuous qualifying 
                     condition
     Benefit reduces Specified Amount and death benefit.
     Only the Owner (Employer) may invoke this rider 
     unless the Split-Dollar Agreement grants the 
     Employee authority to do so.
     Rider charge: $110.00/month

11.2 This rider is intended to provide liquidity to 
     the Employer if the Insured becomes chronically 
     ill and is no longer serving as a productive 
     executive. See IRC §7702B for potential 
     tax treatment of benefits.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 12 - GRACE PERIOD & LAPSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
12.1 If Account Value is insufficient to cover monthly 
     deductions, we notify the Owner (Employer). 
     A 61-day grace period commences.

12.2 Lapse of this policy during a split-dollar 
     arrangement may have significant adverse tax 
     and business consequences for both Employer 
     and Employee. We strongly recommend the 
     Employer maintain adequate funding.

12.3 Reinstatement: Within 3 years of lapse with 
     evidence of insurability of the Insured and 
     payment of all overdue charges plus 6% interest.
