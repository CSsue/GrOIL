ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Account Value" means the accumulated fund within this 
    policy, being the sum of net premiums credited with 
    interest, reduced by monthly charges.

1.2 "High Early Cash Value" (HECV) means the feature 
    of this policy design whereby the surrender charge 
    structure is inverted compared to conventional UL 
    products, creating greater liquidity in early years.

1.3 "Target Premium" is the planned premium amount 
    used in pricing the HECV feature. Premiums 
    below Target in the first 5 years may reduce 
    HECV benefits.

1.4 "Overfund Amount" means premiums paid above the 
    Target Premium in a given year, subject to 
    IRC §7702 limits.

1.5 "COI" means the monthly Cost of Insurance.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - PREMIUM PAYMENTS & OVERFUNDING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums may be paid at any time:
    Minimum: $200/payment
    Annual maximum: Guideline Annual Premium per IRC §7702
    (We will calculate and communicate this limit annually)

2.2 Premium expense load: 
    On premiums up to Target Premium: 4.0%
    On Overfund Amounts above Target: 1.5%
    The lower load on overfunding encourages higher 
    contributions to accelerate tax-sheltered growth.

2.3 OVERFUNDING STRATEGY: This policy is designed for 
    owners who wish to "stuff" the policy with premiums 
    to grow a tax-deferred, potentially tax-free 
    supplemental retirement fund. We recommend working 
    with a financial advisor to optimize premium levels 
    without triggering MEC status.

2.4 Skipping premiums: Permitted so long as Account Value 
    exceeds 6 months of projected monthly deductions.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - HIGH EARLY CASH VALUE FEATURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Unlike conventional UL products, this policy has 
    NO surrender charge in years 1 through 5.

3.2 A minimal surrender charge applies only if the 
    policy is surrendered in years 6-10 while Account 
    Value is below the cumulative Target Premiums paid:

    Year 6: 3.0% of shortfall | Year 7: 2.5%
    Year 8: 2.0% | Year 9: 1.0% | Year 10+: 0%

    The "shortfall" is defined as cumulative Target 
    Premiums less actual premiums paid.

3.3 Implication: If the Owner pays Target Premium 
    consistently, there is NO surrender charge in 
    any policy year.

3.4 The HECV feature is funded by a slightly higher 
    COI charge compared to conventional products 
    of the same face amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 On the 9th of each month, we deduct from Account Value:
    (a) Cost of Insurance charge
    (b) Administrative fee: $10.00/month
    (c) Outstanding loan interest

4.2 COI Basis: 2017 CSO Preferred Plus Table. Rates are 
    competitive because the HECV design self-selects 
    for healthy, financially sophisticated insureds.

4.3 Monthly COI is calculated as:
    (Specified Amount - Account Value) × COI Rate / 1,000

4.4 IMPORTANT: Because this policy is designed to 
    accumulate significant Account Value, the net 
    amount at risk (and thus COI) decreases over time 
    as Account Value approaches Specified Amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - INTEREST CREDITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 Interest is credited daily.
    Guaranteed minimum: 2.00% per annum
    Current rate: 5.00% per annum

5.2 ENHANCED INTEREST TIERS:
    AV < $100,000:       Current rate (5.00%)
    AV $100,000-$499,999: Current rate + 0.25% (5.25%)
    AV ≥ $500,000:        Current rate + 0.50% (5.50%)

5.3 Tiered rate differential is guaranteed to be at 
    least +0.10% and +0.20% respectively, regardless 
    of the then-current base rate.

5.4 Interest rate declared and credited in arrears 
    monthly; rate may change from month to month.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT & IRC §7702 COMPLIANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Death Benefit = greater of:
    (a) Specified Amount, or
    (b) Account Value × applicable corridor percentage 
        per IRC §7702(d) based on insured's attained age

6.2 Option A is in force. As Account Value grows, 
    the IRC §7702 corridor may require the death 
    benefit to temporarily exceed the Specified Amount. 
    This "corridor" benefit is provided at no charge.

6.3 If Account Value reaches Specified Amount (full 
    accumulation), the policy is treated as an 
    endowment for IRC purposes. We will notify the 
    Owner before this occurs.

6.4 Death proceeds are paid within 14 business days 
    of receipt of required documentation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Minimum withdrawal: $500
    Maximum per year: No annual limit (subject to 
    minimum $5,000 remaining Account Value)

7.2 No withdrawal fee in any policy year.

7.3 Withdrawals are income-tax-free to the extent they 
    do not exceed the policy "basis" (total premiums 
    paid net of prior distributions), per IRC §72(e). 
    This is a key planning feature.

7.4 For MEC-classified policies: All distributions 
    (including withdrawals) are income-first.

7.5 Withdrawals permanently reduce Account Value and 
    the Owner's cost basis.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Maximum loan: 95% of Account Value (higher than 
    conventional products to maximize retirement 
    income distribution access)

8.2 STANDARD LOAN: Interest charged at Prime Rate + 
    0.50%, minimum 3.50%. Loaned amount earns 
    guaranteed minimum rate of 2.00%.

8.3 PARTICIPATING LOAN: Available from policy year 1. 
    Interest charged at 4.00% fixed. Loaned amount 
    continues to earn the full credited interest rate 
    (currently 5.00%), resulting in a potential 
    net gain on the loan cost.

8.4 TAX TREATMENT: Policy loans from a non-MEC policy 
    are not income, and are generally income-tax-free 
    if the policy remains in force. Surrender or lapse 
    with outstanding loans may trigger a taxable event.

8.5 MAXIMUM LOAN WARNING: Borrowing 95% of Account 
    Value significantly increases lapse risk. We 
    will provide annual loan statements.

ARTICLE 10 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Death by suicide within 24 months: 
     Account Value as of date of death, without interest, 
     less outstanding loans.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: 2 years from issue date.

10.3 MATERIAL CHANGE: Any increase in Specified Amount 
     restarts contestability and suicide period for 
     the increased portion.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 - GRACE PERIOD & LAPSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 If Account Value is insufficient to cover 3 months 
     of monthly deductions, we notify the Owner. 
     A 61-day grace period follows.

11.2 Upon lapse, any Account Value exceeding outstanding 
     loans is paid as a Cash Surrender Value; no 
     surrender charge applies if the Owner has paid 
     Target Premiums in all prior years.

11.3 LAPSE TAX WARNING: If this policy lapses or is 
     surrendered with outstanding loans exceeding 
     cost basis, the excess is ordinary income 
     in the year of lapse. Consult a tax advisor.
