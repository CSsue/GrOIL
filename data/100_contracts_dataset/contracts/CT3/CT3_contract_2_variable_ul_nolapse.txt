ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Separate Account" means Meridian Variable Life Account B, 
    a segregated asset account of Meridian Life Insurance 
    Company of New York, insulated from the company's 
    general account creditors.

1.2 "Sub-Account" means a subdivision of the Separate Account 
    investing in shares of a designated underlying mutual fund.

1.3 "Account Value" means the sum of all Sub-Account values 
    plus any Fixed Account balance.

1.4 "Net Investment Factor" means the ratio used to calculate 
    daily Sub-Account unit values, reflecting investment 
    performance and mortality and expense charges.

1.5 "No-Lapse Guarantee" (NLG) means the contractual 
    guarantee that the policy will not lapse provided 
    cumulative NLG Premiums are satisfied.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - PREMIUM PAYMENTS & ALLOCATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums are subject to:
    Minimum per payment: $100
    Annual maximum: $150,000 (IRC §7702 compliance)

2.2 A premium expense charge of 4.5% is deducted from each 
    premium before allocation.

2.3 Owner designates premium allocation among Sub-Accounts 
    and Fixed Account on the Policy Data Page. Allocation 
    changes are permitted twice per policy year at no charge; 
    each additional change: $25.

2.4 If No-Lapse Guarantee Premium is underpaid in any month, 
    the NLG is suspended. The NLG is reinstated when 
    cumulative premiums paid return to the cumulative NLG 
    Premium schedule.

ARTICLE 4 - CHARGES & DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 SEPARATE ACCOUNT CHARGES (deducted daily from Sub-Accounts):
    Mortality & Expense Risk Charge: 0.60% per annum
    Administrative Charge: 0.15% per annum
    (Underlying fund expense ratios charged separately 
     within each portfolio — see fund prospectus)

4.2 MONTHLY POLICY CHARGES (deducted on the 15th of each month):
    (a) Cost of Insurance charge (see COI Schedule)
    (b) Policy administration fee: $10.00/month
    (c) Outstanding loan interest not yet paid

4.3 COI is calculated on the net amount at risk using 
    2017 CSO Aggregate Unisex Table, nonsmoker rates.

4.4 No No-Lapse Guarantee charge is assessed separately; 
    it is embedded in the mortality and expense charge.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 OPTION A (Level) — Currently Selected
    Death Benefit = greater of Specified Amount or the 
    IRC §7702 corridor percentage of Account Value

5.2 OPTION B (Increasing) — Available upon request
    Death Benefit = Specified Amount + Account Value

5.3 Death proceeds are paid from the general account. 
    They are not subject to market risk after the date 
    of death.

5.4 IF NO-LAPSE GUARANTEE IS ACTIVE: Death benefit equals 
    at minimum the Specified Amount regardless of Account 
    Value, for the duration of the NLG Period.

ARTICLE 6 - TRANSFERS AMONG SUB-ACCOUNTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 The Owner may transfer Account Value among Sub-Accounts 
    and the Fixed Account at any time.

6.2 Transfers from the Fixed Account: maximum 25% of the 
    Fixed Account balance per policy year.

6.3 First 12 transfers per year: free.
    Each additional transfer: $25.

6.4 Market timing restrictions: We reserve the right to 
    refuse transfer requests that we determine, in our 
    sole discretion, constitute disruptive short-term 
    trading that harms other policyholders.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Minimum withdrawal: $500
    Maximum: Account Value less $500 reserve and less 
    any applicable surrender charge

7.2 Withdrawals are taken pro-rata from all Sub-Accounts 
    and Fixed Account unless Owner specifies otherwise.

7.3 Withdrawal fee: $25 per withdrawal in years 1-10; 
    no fee thereafter.

7.4 WARNING: Withdrawals during the NLG period may reduce 
    the NLG Premium required to maintain the guarantee. 
    The NLG is permanently reduced, not suspended, by 
    withdrawals.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Maximum loan: 90% of Account Value excluding amounts in 
    Sub-Accounts pledged as collateral

8.2 Loaned amounts are transferred to the Fixed Account 
    as collateral and credited at 2.00% per annum.

8.3 Loan interest rate: 3.50% per annum, fixed.

8.4 Net loan cost: 1.50% (interest charged less interest 
    credited on collateral).

8.5 If outstanding loans reduce Account Value to zero, 
    the policy lapses (NLG does not protect against 
    loan-induced lapse).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - SURRENDER CHARGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge - 
    Outstanding Loans

9.2 Surrender Charge Schedule (applied to each premium 
    within 10 years of payment):
    Months 1-12: 7% of premium | Months 13-24: 6%
    Months 25-36: 5% | Months 37-48: 4%
    Months 49-60: 3% | Months 61-72: 2%
    Months 73-84: 1% | Months 85-120: 0.5%
    After 120 months from each premium payment: 0%

9.3 The NLG is void upon full surrender.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Death by suicide within 24 months of 
     issue: proceeds limited to Account Value (including 
     any Sub-Account gains) as of date of death, without 
     deduction for surrender charges.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest within 2 years of the 
     issue date for material misrepresentation.

10.3 RIGHT TO EXAMINE: Owner has 30 days from receipt to 
     return this policy for a full refund of premiums paid.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 - LAPSE, GRACE PERIOD & REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 If Account Value is insufficient to pay monthly charges 
     and the NLG is not active, a 61-day grace period begins. 
     Notice is sent to the Owner's address of record.

11.2 If the NLG is active, the policy will not enter grace 
     period solely because Account Value is insufficient, 
     so long as cumulative NLG Premiums are current.

11.3 Reinstatement within 3 years of lapse requires evidence 
     of insurability and repayment of all outstanding charges.
