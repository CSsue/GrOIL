ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Guarantee Premium" means the fixed monthly premium 
    shown on the Policy Data Page, which, when paid on 
    time and in full, maintains the Lifetime Death 
    Benefit Guarantee without reference to Account Value.

1.2 "Account Value" means the accumulation of net premiums 
    credited with interest less charges. Account Value 
    may be zero or near-zero at various points in the 
    policy's life; this does NOT cause the policy to 
    lapse so long as the Lifetime Guarantee is active.

1.3 "Lifetime Death Benefit Guarantee" means our contractual 
    commitment to pay the Specified Amount upon death 
    of the Insured so long as all Guarantee Premiums 
    have been paid on time, without regard to Account Value.

1.4 "Guarantee Reset" means the adjustment of Guarantee 
    Premium following a permitted change event.

1.5 "Simplified Issue" means underwriting based solely on 
    application questions and database checks, without 
    a medical examination or attending physician statement.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - GUARANTEE PREMIUM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 The Guarantee Premium is FIXED at the amount shown 
    on the Policy Data Page for the life of the policy. 
    It will not increase due to age, health changes, 
    or interest rate movements.

2.2 The Guarantee Premium must be received by us on or 
    before the Monthly Due Date (the 12th of each month). 
    We allow a 30-day tolerance window (see §2.3).

2.3 TOLERANCE PROVISION: If a Guarantee Premium is paid 
    within 30 days after its Monthly Due Date, the 
    Guarantee is considered uninterrupted. If payment 
    is more than 30 days late, the Lifetime Guarantee 
    is suspended (see Article 10).

2.4 The Guarantee Premium may be redirected to: pay 
    annual, semi-annual, or quarterly, as follows:
    Annual:       $3,489.00  (6.5% discount)
    Semi-annual:  $1,791.00  (3.5% discount)
    Quarterly:    $917.00    (1.5% discount)
    Monthly:      $312.00    (no discount)

2.5 Premiums in excess of the Guarantee Premium are 
    applied to increase Account Value but do NOT 
    reduce the required Guarantee Premium.

2.6 Premiums less than the Guarantee Premium do NOT 
    maintain the Guarantee. Partial payments are 
    credited to Account Value but may not prevent 
    Guarantee suspension.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - ACCOUNT VALUE & CHARGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Account Value is maintained separately from the 
    Lifetime Guarantee. The two operate independently.

3.2 On each Monthly Due Date, we deduct from Account Value:
    (a) Cost of Insurance charge (current rates)
    (b) Administrative fee: $8.50/month
    (c) Outstanding loan interest

3.3 If Account Value is zero or insufficient to cover 
    monthly deductions, charges are absorbed; no 
    negative Account Value is permitted. So long as 
    the Lifetime Guarantee is active, the policy 
    remains in force.

3.4 Interest is credited at:
    Guaranteed minimum: 2.00% per annum
    Current rate: 3.25% per annum (declared annually)

3.5 Because COI charges in advanced ages typically 
    exceed the credited interest, Account Value may 
    decline to zero in later years. This is expected 
    for this product design.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Specified Amount is paid upon death, regardless 
    of Account Value, so long as the Lifetime Guarantee 
    is active OR Account Value is positive.

4.2 If the Lifetime Guarantee has been suspended 
    and Account Value is zero, the policy has lapsed 
    and no death benefit is payable.

4.3 Death benefit is reduced by outstanding policy loans.

4.4 MATURITY PROVISION: If the Insured is living on the 
    policy maturity date (the Insured's age 121), we 
    pay the greater of Account Value or Specified Amount 
    as an endowment benefit.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 IMPORTANT LIMITATION: This product is designed for 
    death benefit protection. Partial withdrawals are 
    permitted but strongly discouraged because:
    (a) Withdrawals reduce Account Value permanently
    (b) Withdrawals do NOT affect the Guarantee Premium
    (c) Withdrawals may reduce Cash Surrender Value 
        to near-zero

5.2 Minimum withdrawal: $500
    Maximum: Cash Surrender Value minus $1,000

5.3 Withdrawal fee: $50 per withdrawal.

5.4 Partial withdrawals do NOT affect the Lifetime 
    Guarantee so long as Guarantee Premiums are paid.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Maximum loan: 90% of Account Value. 
    NOTE: If Account Value is low, borrowing capacity 
    may be minimal.

6.2 Loan interest rate: Fixed 5.50% per annum.

6.3 Policy loans reduce death benefit dollar-for-dollar 
    but do NOT affect the Lifetime Guarantee (so long 
    as Guarantee Premiums are paid).

6.4 Outstanding loans that exceed Account Value will 
    trigger a loan demand notice. Failure to repay 
    within 60 days terminates the policy.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - SURRENDER & CASH VALUE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge 
    - Outstanding Loans (minimum $0)

7.2 Surrender Charge Schedule:
    Years 1-5: $2,000 flat charge
    Years 6-10: $1,000 flat charge
    Years 11+: $0

7.3 For this product, Cash Surrender Value may be 
    significantly less than premiums paid, particularly 
    in early years. This reflects the cost of the 
    lifetime guarantee.

7.4 Upon surrender, the Lifetime Guarantee is permanently 
    terminated. Reinstatement after surrender requires 
    new underwriting.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - SIMPLIFIED ISSUE UNDERWRITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 This policy was issued on a Simplified Issue basis. 
    No medical examination was required. Approval was 
    based on:
    (a) Application health questions answered by Insured
    (b) MIB database search
    (c) Prescription drug database check
    (d) Motor Vehicle Record check

8.2 CONTESTABILITY AND SIMPLIFIED ISSUE: Because no 
    medical exam was performed, we reserve the right 
    to investigate all claims, regardless of the 
    contestability period, if we have evidence of 
    fraud or intentional misrepresentation.

8.3 Material misrepresentation in application health 
    questions, if discovered within 2 years of issue, 
    entitles us to rescind this policy and refund 
    premiums paid without interest, rather than 
    pay the death benefit.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Death by suicide within 24 months: 
    premiums paid returned without interest.

9.2 CONTESTABILITY: 2-year period from issue date.

9.3 GRADED BENEFIT PROVISION: Because this policy was 
    issued on a simplified issue basis without full 
    medical underwriting, the following graded benefit 
    applies during the first 24 months:

    Death due to ACCIDENT: Full Specified Amount paid.
    Death due to ILLNESS or NATURAL CAUSES within 
    first 12 months: Return of premiums paid + 10%.
    Death due to ILLNESS or NATURAL CAUSES in months 
    13-24: 75% of Specified Amount.
    Death after 24 months: Full Specified Amount.

    "Accident" means death resulting from an 
    unintentional, external, violent cause, 
    independent of illness or disease.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - GUARANTEE SUSPENSION & REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUSPENSION: If a Guarantee Premium is received more 
     than 30 days after its Monthly Due Date (or not 
     received at all), the Lifetime Guarantee is suspended.

10.2 During suspension, the policy remains in force only 
     so long as Account Value can cover monthly deductions 
     (a 61-day standard grace period applies).

10.3 REINSTATEMENT OF GUARANTEE: To restore the Lifetime 
     Guarantee after suspension, the Owner must pay 
     all missed Guarantee Premiums plus interest at 
     6.00% per annum within 6 months of suspension. 
     No evidence of insurability is required for 
     Guarantee reinstatement within 6 months.

10.4 After 6 months of suspension, reinstatement of the 
     Guarantee requires evidence of insurability and 
     may require a Guarantee Premium reset.
