ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Account Value" means the actual policy accumulation 
    value, determined by crediting interest and deducting 
    charges each month.

1.2 "Shadow Account" means a hypothetical accumulation 
    account used solely to test whether the Secondary 
    Guarantee is in effect. The Shadow Account has no 
    cash value and cannot be accessed.

1.3 "Shadow Account Premium" means the level monthly 
    premium, specified on the Policy Data Page, that 
    must be paid (on a cumulative basis) to maintain 
    the Shadow Account in force.

1.4 "Shadow Account Crediting Rate" means the fixed rate 
    of 3.75% per annum used exclusively in Shadow Account 
    calculations. This rate is guaranteed.

1.5 "Shadow Account COI Rate" means the guaranteed maximum 
    COI rates used in Shadow Account calculations 
    (separate, and different from, current COI rates).

1.6 "Secondary Guarantee" (SG) means the guarantee that 
    the policy will remain in force so long as the 
    Shadow Account has a positive value, regardless 
    of actual Account Value.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - PREMIUM PAYMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums may be paid at any time:
    Minimum per payment: $100
    Annual maximum: $120,000 (IRC §7702 compliance)

2.2 Premium expense load: 3.5% of each premium payment.

2.3 Premiums are applied to actual Account Value. Premiums 
    also credit the Shadow Account as follows:
    Shadow Account Credit = Premium paid × 96.5%
    (reflecting the same 3.5% load assumption)

2.4 IMPORTANT: The Secondary Guarantee depends on the 
    Shadow Account balance, not the actual Account Value. 
    Paying only the planned premium does not guarantee 
    the SG will remain active if premiums are skipped 
    or paid late.

2.5 Shadow Account Test: On each Monthly Deduction Date, 
    we test whether the Shadow Account Value (as defined 
    in Article 11) is ≥ $0. If yes, the SG is active. 
    If no, the SG is inactive and the policy is subject 
    to ordinary lapse provisions.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS (ACTUAL ACCOUNT)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    (a) Current Cost of Insurance charge
    (b) Administrative fee: $9.00/month

3.2 Current COI rates are based on the 2017 CSO Table,

3.3 INTEREST RATE SENSITIVITY: This is a Current Assumption

ARTICLE 5 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 OPTION A (Level) — In Force
    Death Benefit = Specified Amount, subject to 
    IRC §7702 corridor minimum.

5.2 Death benefit is payable whether or not the Secondary 
    Guarantee is active, so long as the policy is in force.

5.3 Death Benefit Changes: Owner may request an increase

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Minimum withdrawal: $500
    Maximum: Account Value less ($500 + outstanding loans)

6.2 Withdrawals in years 1-7: $30 per withdrawal
    Withdrawals in years 8+: No charge

6.3 WARNING: Withdrawals reduce actual Account Value. 
    They do NOT reduce the Shadow Account. However, 
    if Account Value falls to zero due to withdrawals, 
    the policy lapses regardless of Shadow Account status.

6.4 No more than one partial withdrawal per calendar month.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Maximum loan: 90% of current Account Value

7.2 Loan interest rate:
    Years 1-10: Variable — 10-year Treasury + 1.00%, 
                minimum 4.00%, adjusted annually
    Years 11+: Fixed option available at 4.50% per annum

7.3 Policy loans reduce actual Account Value and may 
    cause lapse if Account Value is insufficient to 
    cover monthly deductions, even if the SG is active.

7.4 Loans do NOT affect Shadow Account calculations.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge -

8.2 Surrender Charge Schedule (% of Initial Specified Amount):
    Year 1: 7.0% | Year 2: 6.5% | Year 3: 6.0%
    Year 4: 5.5% | Year 5: 5.0% | Year 6: 4.0%
    Year 7: 3.0% | Year 8: 2.0% | Year 9: 1.0%

8.3 If the policy is surrendered while the SG is active, 
    the surrender charge still applies. The SG only 
    prevents lapse; it does not waive surrender charges.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Death by suicide within 24 months 
    of issue date: proceeds limited to Account Value 
    as of date of death, without surrender charge 
    reduction but less outstanding loans.

9.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest this policy within 
    2 years of issue for material misrepresentation.

9.3 AVIATION HAZARD EXCLUSION: If Insured dies as a result 
    of operating or riding in any aircraft, other than 
    as a fare-paying passenger on a scheduled commercial 
    airline, and the Insured had such activity noted in 
    the application, no aviation exclusion applies. 
    If aviation activity was not disclosed, we reserve 
    the right to apply standard rescission.

9.4 TOBACCO USE: If Insured uses tobacco within 3 years 
    of issue date and fails to disclose this, we may 
    re-rate the policy at standard tobacco rates and 
    collect the additional COI charge retroactively 
    or reduce the death benefit accordingly.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - GRACE PERIOD & LAPSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 If actual Account Value is insufficient to cover 
     monthly deductions AND the Secondary Guarantee 
     is NOT active, a 61-day grace period commences.

10.2 If the Secondary Guarantee IS active (Shadow Account 
     ≥ $0), no grace period is triggered, and the policy 
     remains in force regardless of actual Account Value.

10.3 Reinstatement within 3 years of lapse: requires 
     evidence of insurability and payment of premium 
     sufficient to restore both the actual Account Value 
     and the Shadow Account to positive balances.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 - SECONDARY GUARANTEE — SHADOW ACCOUNT PROVISION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 SHADOW ACCOUNT MECHANICS:
     The Shadow Account is a separate hypothetical ledger. 
     It begins at $0 on the issue date and evolves as:

     Shadow AV(n) = Shadow AV(n-1)
                  + Shadow Premium Credit
                  - Shadow COI Charge
                  - Shadow Admin Charge
                  + Shadow Interest Credit

11.2 Shadow Premium Credit: Each premium payment (net of 
     3.5% load) is credited to the Shadow Account.

11.3 Shadow COI: Uses guaranteed maximum COI rates 
     from the COI Schedule — typically higher than 
     current COI rates, making the SG conservative.

11.4 Shadow Interest: Credited at the guaranteed rate 
     of 3.75% per annum, regardless of actual current rate.

11.5 Shadow Admin Charge: $6.00/month (guaranteed).

11.6 If Shadow Account Value < $0 on any Monthly 
     Deduction Date, the Secondary Guarantee is 
     immediately inactive. It cannot be restored 
     without a catch-up premium payment sufficient 
     to return the Shadow Account to ≥ $0.

11.7 We will provide Shadow Account balance information 
     in the annual policy statement.
