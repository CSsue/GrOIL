ATTACHED RIDERS (INCLUDED AT NO ADDITIONAL PREMIUM 
UNLESS NOTED)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
RIDER 1 — TERMINAL ILLNESS ACCELERATION RIDER (Form ESL-TIA):
  Benefit: Up to 50% of Specified Amount (max $150,000)
  Trigger: Insured diagnosed with terminal illness with 
           life expectancy of 24 months or less, 
           certified by two independent licensed physicians
  Payment: Lump sum; reduces death benefit dollar-for-dollar
  Charge: Included in base policy at no separate premium

RIDER 2 — ACCIDENTAL DEATH BENEFIT RIDER (Form ESL-ADB):
  Additional Benefit: $300,000 (equal to Specified Amount)
  Total death benefit if accident: $600,000
  "Accident" defined as: bodily injury caused solely 
    by external, violent, accidental means, occurring 
    within 90 days of the accident
  Exclusions: self-inflicted injury, drugs/alcohol, 
    war, felony, aviation (other than as commercial 
    fare-paying passenger)
  Benefit ceases at Insured's age 70
  Rider premium: $18.00/month

RIDER 3 — DISABILITY WAIVER OF MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS 
           (Form ESL-DWD):
  Benefit: All monthly deductions waived if Insured 
           is totally disabled for more than 6 months
  "Total Disability" (years 1-24 of disability): 
    Unable to perform all material duties of own 
    occupation as a commercial electrician
  "Total Disability" (after 24 months): 
    Unable to perform any gainful occupation 
    for which qualified by education, training, experience
  Elimination Period: 6 consecutive months
  Maximum waiver period: To Insured's age 65
  Rider premium: $22.50/month

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Account Value" means accumulated premiums plus 
    interest minus monthly deductions.

1.2 "Terminal Illness" means a medically confirmed 
    condition that is expected to result in death 
    within 24 months from the date of certification, 
    with no reasonable prospect of recovery.

1.3 "Accident" has the meaning ascribed in the 
    Accidental Death Benefit Rider (Form ESL-ADB).

1.4 "Total Disability" has the meaning ascribed in 
    the Disability Waiver of Monthly Deductions 
    Rider (Form ESL-DWD), varying by duration.

1.5 "Increasing Death Benefit" (Option B) means the 
    death benefit equals the Specified Amount plus 
    the Account Value at the time of death.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - PREMIUM PAYMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums may be paid at any time:
    Minimum: $60/payment
    Annual maximum: $75,000

2.2 Premium expense load: 4.0% of each premium.

2.3 If the Disability Waiver Rider is active, the 
    monthly deductions are waived — meaning no 
    deduction is taken from Account Value — but 
    the Owner is not required to pay premiums. 
    The policy remains in force even without premium 
    payments during a valid disability period.

2.4 Premiums may be skipped; policy stays in force 
    so long as Account Value covers deductions.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 3 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 On the 7th of each month, we deduct from Account Value:
    (a) Cost of Insurance charge
    (b) Administrative fee: $8.00/month
    (c) Accidental Death Benefit Rider charge: $18.00/month
    (d) Disability Waiver Rider charge: $22.50/month
    (e) Outstanding loan interest

3.2 If Disability Waiver is active, items (a), (b), 
    (c), (d), and (e) are all waived — NO deduction 
    is taken from Account Value during the disability.

3.3 COI is calculated on:
    Option B Net Amount at Risk = Specified Amount 
    (Account Value is separately included in death 
    benefit, so net amount at risk = Specified Amount)

3.4 COI rates are based on the 2017 CSO Standard 
    Nonsmoker table; guaranteed maximum rates are 
    in the attached COI Schedule.

ARTICLE 4 - INTEREST CREDITING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Interest is credited monthly.
    Guaranteed minimum: 2.00% per annum
    Current rate: 3.75% per annum

4.2 Rate declared quarterly; subject to change 
    but never below guaranteed minimum.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 OPTION B (Increasing) — In Force
    Base Death Benefit = Specified Amount + Account Value

5.2 ACCIDENTAL DEATH: If death is due to a qualifying 
    accident per Rider ESL-ADB, an additional $300,000 
    is paid from the ADB Rider, for a total of:
    $300,000 + Account Value + $300,000 (ADB) 
    = $600,000 + Account Value

5.3 TERMINAL ILLNESS ACCELERATION: If the TIA Rider 
    has been triggered and benefits paid, the death 
    benefit is reduced by the amount of TIA benefits paid.

5.4 Death benefit is reduced by outstanding policy loans.

5.5 All death benefits are paid within 30 days of 
    receipt of a certified death certificate and 
    completed claimant's statement.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - TERMINAL ILLNESS CLAIM PROCEDURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 To file a terminal illness claim:
    (a) Submit Form ESL-TIA-C
    (b) Certification from two licensed, board-certified 
        physicians (one may be the Insured's attending 
        physician; one must be a specialist)
    (c) Current medical records (last 12 months)

6.2 We will adjudicate a complete claim within 15 
    business days.

6.3 TIA benefit may be taken as:
    (a) Lump sum up to 50% of Specified Amount
    (b) Monthly installments over 12-24 months

6.4 Any TIA benefit paid is an advance on the death 
    benefit, not an additional benefit. Remaining 
    death benefit is reduced accordingly.

6.5 Tax Treatment: TIA benefits may be excludable 
    from income under IRC §101(g). Consult a tax advisor.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Minimum withdrawal: $500
    Maximum: Account Value less $500 minimum reserve

7.2 Withdrawal fee:
    Years 1-5: $20/withdrawal
    Years 6+: No charge

7.3 Under Option B, withdrawals reduce Account Value 
    (and therefore reduce the death benefit). Owner 
    should understand this linkage before withdrawing.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Maximum loan: 90% of Account Value

8.2 Loan interest rate: Prime Rate + 1.50%, 
    minimum 4.50%, adjusted annually each January 1.

8.3 Outstanding loan reduces death benefit dollar-for-dollar 
    under both base policy and ADB Rider.

8.4 Policy loans are not available during an active 
    disability waiver period unless the Owner 
    submits a written waiver of the disability benefit 
    for the period of the loan.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - SURRENDER CHARGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge 
    - Outstanding Loans

9.2 Surrender Charge Schedule (% of Specified Amount):
    Year 1: 8% | Year 2: 7% | Year 3: 6% | Year 4: 5%
    Year 5: 4% | Year 6: 3% | Year 7: 2% | Year 8: 1%
    Year 9+: 0%

9.3 Riders terminate upon full surrender.
