ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Account Value" means the sum of all Segment Values plus any 
    Fixed Account balance, less outstanding policy loans.

1.2 "Index Segment" means a portion of Account Value allocated 
    to an indexed crediting strategy for one Segment Term.

1.3 "Segment Term" means a one-year or two-year period, as 
    elected, during which an Index Segment earns indexed credits.

1.4 "Index Cap Rate" means the maximum credited rate for a 
    given Segment, declared at Segment inception.

1.5 "Floor Rate" means the minimum indexed credit rate, 
    guaranteed to be not less than 0% (protection from loss).

1.6 "Participation Rate" means the percentage of positive index 
    change applied to calculate indexed credits; guaranteed 
    minimum 100%.

1.7 "Cost of Insurance" (COI) means the monthly charge based on 
    net amount at risk, insured age, sex, and rate class.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 2 - FLEXIBLE PREMIUM PAYMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Premiums may be remitted at any time subject to:

    Minimum per payment: $250
    Annual maximum: $250,000 (IRS corridor compliance)
    Guideline Single Premium limit applies per IRC §7702

2.2 A premium load of 5% is deducted from each premium before 
    allocation to Account Value.

2.3 Premiums received outside a Segment allocation window are 
    credited to the Fixed Account until the next Segment 
    start date.

2.4 Policy remains in force so long as Account Value exceeds 
    total monthly deductions. No grace period applies during 
    an active Segment Term without written request.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 4 - MONTHLY DEDUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 On the 1st of each calendar month, the following are 
    deducted from the Fixed Account (or, if insufficient, 
    pro-rata from Segment Values):

    (a) Cost of Insurance charge
    (b) Policy fee: $12.00/month
    (c) Per-unit charge: $0.09 per $1,000 of Specified Amount 
        (applies for first 10 policy years)
    (d) Outstanding loan interest, if not paid separately

4.2 COI Rate Basis: 2017 CSO Gender-Distinct Aggregate Table, 
    nonsmoker rates apply; rates are guaranteed not to exceed 
    maximums shown in the attached COI Schedule.

4.3 Net Amount at Risk = Death Benefit - Account Value

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 5 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 OPTION B (Increasing) — Currently Selected
    Death Benefit = Specified Amount + Account Value

5.2 OPTION A (Level) — Available by Request
    Death Benefit = Specified Amount

5.3 IRC §7702 Corridor: Regardless of elected option, the 
    death benefit shall not be less than the applicable 
    percentage of Account Value as required by federal law.

5.4 Death benefit is paid net of outstanding policy loans. 
    Payment is made within 30 days of receipt of due proof 
    of death and all required claim forms.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Withdrawals are permitted only from the Fixed Account or 
    at the end of a Segment Term. Mid-term withdrawals from 
    an Index Segment trigger a Segment Withdrawal Adjustment.

6.2 Segment Withdrawal Adjustment = withdrawal amount × 
    (1 - days remaining in Segment Term / total Segment days)

6.3 Minimum withdrawal: $1,000
    Maximum: Account Value less $5,000 reserve

6.4 First two withdrawals per policy year: no charge.
    Each additional withdrawal: $35 per transaction.

6.5 Withdrawals reduce Account Value and, if Option B is in 
    force, reduce death benefit dollar-for-dollar.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 7 - POLICY LOANS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 Maximum loan: 85% of current Account Value

7.2 Standard Loan Interest Rate: Prime Rate + 1.00%, 
    minimum 4.00%, adjusted each January 1.

7.3 Participating (Wash) Loan: Beginning in policy year 11, 
    loaned amounts may earn indexed credits at the same rate 
    as if unloaned. Loan interest charged at a fixed 5.00%.

7.4 Outstanding loans reduce death benefit dollar-for-dollar 
    and may cause lapse if Account Value is insufficient 
    to cover monthly deductions.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 8 - SURRENDER & SURRENDER CHARGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Cash Surrender Value = Account Value - Surrender Charge - 
    Outstanding Loans

8.2 Surrender Charge Schedule (% of Target Premium × Years 
    of Target Premiums paid, capped at Specified Amount):
    Year 1: 100% | Year 3: 80% | Year 5: 60% | Year 7: 40%
    Year 9: 20%  | Year 10: 10% | Year 11+: 0%

8.3 No surrender charge applies to the Fixed Account balance 
    accumulated from premiums paid above Target Premium.

8.4 Upon full surrender, all Segment Values are liquidated; 
    no indexed credit is applied for the incomplete Segment.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - LIMITATIONS & EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: Death by suicide within 24 months of 
    issue date: proceeds limited to premiums paid, without 
    interest, less outstanding loans.

9.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may rescind this policy within 2 years 
    of issue date for material misrepresentation in the 
    application while the Insured is living.

9.3 WAR EXCLUSION: No war exclusion applies under this contract.

9.4 AVIATION EXCLUSION: No aviation exclusion applies.

9.5 MATERIAL CHANGE RULE: Any benefit increase triggers a new 
    2-year contestability and suicide period for the increase.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - LAPSE AND REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 If Account Value is insufficient to cover monthly 
     deductions, a 61-day grace period commences.

10.2 During grace period, coverage remains in force. If the 
     Insured dies during the grace period, the death benefit 
     is reduced by the overdue monthly deductions.

10.3 REINSTATEMENT: Within 5 years of lapse, the policy may 
     be reinstated upon:
     (a) Evidence of insurability satisfactory to us
     (b) Payment of all overdue charges plus interest at 6%
     (c) No outstanding loan may be reinstated without 
         full repayment

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 11 - RIDERS (ATTACHED AND INCORPORATED)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 OVERLOAN PROTECTION RIDER (no additional premium):
     If policy loans exceed 95% of Account Value after 
     policy year 15 and insured is age 75+, policy is 
     converted to a reduced paid-up status rather than 
     lapsing, preserving a reduced death benefit.

11.2 CHRONIC ILLNESS ACCELERATION RIDER (no additional premium):
     Up to 24% of Specified Amount per year (maximum $240,000) 
     may be accelerated if Insured is certified as chronically 
     ill by a licensed healthcare practitioner.
