SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: UNEMPLOYMENT PREMIUM WAIVER RIDER
Premium: Included in base premium

If either insured becomes involuntarily unemployed for 6+ months:
• Premiums waived for up to 12 months per unemployment event
• Maximum 2 unemployment waiver events per policy
• Insured must document receipt of state unemployment benefits
• Does not apply to self-employed insureds or voluntary separation
• Available during policy years 1–15 only

RIDER 2: MORTGAGE DISABILITY INCOME RIDER
Premium: $410/year (additional)

If either insured becomes Totally Disabled and unable to work:
• Pays the monthly mortgage payment amount ($2,380/month) directly
  to the mortgage servicer after 60-day elimination period
• Maximum benefit period: 24 months per disability event
• Total lifetime cap: 36 months of mortgage payments
• Only one insured's disability benefit can be active at any time

RIDER 3: ACCELERATED TERMINAL ILLNESS BENEFIT
Premium: No additional charge

If either insured is diagnosed with a terminal condition with life
expectancy of 12 months or less:
• May accelerate up to 50% of the scheduled death benefit
• Minimum: $25,000; Maximum: $190,000
• Payment reduces the beneficiary death benefit dollar-for-dollar

SECTION 3: TOBACCO RECLASSIFICATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Sandra Lee Whitmore may apply for Non-Tobacco reclassification
    after 12 consecutive months of tobacco abstinence. Reclassification
    requires passing a cotinine urine or saliva test and submitting
    a written statement of tobacco cessation.

3.2 If approved, premiums will be recalculated prospectively at the
    Non-Tobacco rate for her attained age at time of reclassification.
    No retroactive adjustment will be made.

3.3 If Sandra Lee Whitmore resumes tobacco use and fails to disclose
    this at reclassification, any subsequent claim may be subject
    to a benefit reduction not to exceed the premium difference
    between tobacco and non-tobacco rates multiplied by years of
    undisclosed use.
