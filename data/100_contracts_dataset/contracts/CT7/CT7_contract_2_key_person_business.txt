POLICY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Key Person Loss" means the death of the insured during the policy
period, resulting in financial loss to the business owner.

"Disability" means total and permanent inability of the insured to
perform the material duties of his or her regular occupation, as
certified by a physician acceptable to us, lasting at least 12
consecutive months.

"Business Continuation Event" means the death, disability, or
departure of the insured from active participation in the business.

SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 We will pay the $2,000,000 death benefit to Vertex Dynamics LLC
    upon the death of the insured while this policy is in force and
    all premiums have been paid.

1.2 Payment will be made within 15 business days of receiving a
    completed claim, certified death certificate, and the owner's
    written authorization.

1.3 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: Death by suicide within 2 years of policy
    date results in return of premiums paid only, without interest.

1.4 EXCLUSION – FRAUD/CRIMINAL ACT: No benefit is payable if the
    insured's death results from the insured's own commission of a
    felony, as established by final criminal judgment or civil
    court finding.

1.5 EXCLUSION – WAR: No benefit is payable if death results from
    declared or undeclared war or armed conflict in which the insured
    voluntarily participates as a combatant. Accidental death in a
    war zone while present in a civilian capacity is covered.

SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: BUSINESS OVERHEAD EXPENSE DISABILITY RIDER
Premium: $520/year

If the insured becomes Totally Disabled and cannot perform his or
her occupational duties for 60+ consecutive days:
• Pays up to $15,000/month for covered business overhead expenses
• Benefit period: up to 24 months per disability
• Elimination period: 60 days
• Covered expenses include: rent/mortgage, utilities, staff salaries,
  equipment leases, professional subscriptions, and insurance premiums
• Benefit ceases when insured returns to work, dies, or 24 months expire

RIDER 2: TERMINAL ILLNESS ACCELERATED BENEFIT
Premium: Included (no additional charge)

If the insured is certified by two independent physicians to have a
life expectancy of 12 months or less:
• Up to 75% of the face amount may be accelerated (maximum $1,500,000)
• Benefit subject to actuarial discount of approximately 6% per year
• Remaining death benefit reduced dollar-for-dollar by amount advanced
• Owner consent and insured consent both required to trigger this rider

RIDER 3: PREMIUM WAIVER – BUSINESS DISABILITY
Premium: $260/year

If the insured suffers a qualifying Total Disability lasting 6+
months and becomes unable to contribute materially to the business:
• All policy premiums waived during period of continuous disability
• Waiver terminates upon return to work, death, or policy expiration
• No age limit for disability onset under this rider (standard
  policies typically cap at 60; this rider extends to age 65)
