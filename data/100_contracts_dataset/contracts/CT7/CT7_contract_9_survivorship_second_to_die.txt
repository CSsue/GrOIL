SURVIVORSHIP STRUCTURE: This policy pays the death benefit only
upon the death of the SECOND insured to die. No benefit is paid
upon the death of the first insured. Coverage continues in force
following the death of the first insured; premiums continue until
the second insured's death or policy expiration.

UNDERWRITING ADVANTAGE: The rated classification of Robert Pemberton
(Table C) has a substantially lower impact on this survivorship
policy's premium than it would on an individual policy, because
the benefit is not paid until both insureds have died.

POLICY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Second Insured to Die" means the surviving insured at the time of
the later death of the two insureds.

"Joint Life Expectancy" means the actuarially estimated period
until the death of the second surviving insured.

"Survivor Paid-Up Provision" means the feature described in
Section 1.3 below.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 We will pay $1,500,000 to the Pemberton Dynasty Trust (the
    named beneficiary) upon satisfactory proof of the death of the
    second insured to die during the 20-year policy period.

1.2 No benefit is paid upon the death of the first insured to die.
    This policy remains in force following such death.

1.3 SURVIVOR PAID-UP PROVISION: If Robert Allen Pemberton dies
    first, Margaret Louise Pemberton (the surviving insured) may
    elect, within 90 days of Robert's death, to stop paying premiums
    and accept a paid-up reduced death benefit. The reduced paid-up
    benefit is calculated actuarially based on the reserve at the
    time of election relative to the premiums otherwise due. This
    election is irrevocable.

    Alternatively, Margaret may continue paying premiums and maintain
    the full $1,500,000 benefit. The choice is hers; the company
    will provide a written illustration of both options within 30
    days of the first death.

1.4 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: If either insured's death is by suicide
    within 2 years of the policy date, the policy remains in force
    for the surviving insured's benefit, but upon the eventual second
    death (when the benefit would otherwise be paid), the benefit
    is reduced by the premiums that were paid after the suicide of
    the first insured. If both insureds die by suicide within 2 years,
    only premiums paid are returned.

1.5 EXCLUSION – SIMULTANEOUS DEATH: If both insureds die in the
    same event and it cannot be determined who survived longer, the
    benefit is paid as if the Second Insured named herein (Margaret
    Louise Pemberton) survived the First Insured (Robert Allen
    Pemberton). This is a contractually stipulated order of survival.

1.6 EXCLUSION – FELONY: No benefit is payable if the death of the
    surviving insured is caused feloniously by a beneficiary of the
    trust. In such case the trust distribution must be reformed
    to exclude that beneficiary, and the benefit is paid to the
    trust for distribution to remaining trust beneficiaries.

1.7 NO WAR EXCLUSION: This policy does not contain a war exclusion.
    Death from any cause, including acts of war, is covered.

SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: FIRST DEATH NOTIFICATION BENEFIT
Premium: $480/year

Upon the death of the first insured to die, a lump-sum payment of
$50,000 is made to the policy owner (the trust) as a first-death
benefit to assist with estate settlement, funeral, and transition
costs associated with the first death:
• This does NOT reduce the $1,500,000 survivorship death benefit
• Payment made upon proof of first death, within 21 days
• This rider represents the only benefit payable on the first death
• Rider terminates once the first-death payment is made

RIDER 2: ACCELERATED BENEFIT – TERMINAL ILLNESS (SURVIVING INSURED)
Premium: $380/year (applies after first insured's death)

After the death of the first insured, if the surviving insured
is diagnosed with a terminal illness (life expectancy ≤ 12 months):
• Up to 70% of the remaining death benefit may be advanced
• Subject to 8% per annum actuarial discount
• Trustee authorization required
• Remaining benefit paid to trust upon surviving insured's death

RIDER 3: PREMIUM CONTINUATION – FIRST DEATH WAIVER
Premium: $320/year

Upon the death of the first insured to die (here, if Robert dies
first), all remaining premiums are waived and coverage continues
at the full $1,500,000 until the second insured dies or the
policy expires. This rider ensures that the surviving spouse (or
trust) is not burdened with ongoing premiums after the first loss.

Note: This rider applies regardless of which insured dies first.

SECTION 3: CHANGE OF INSURED PROVISION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If either insured remarries after the death of the other
    insured, the owner (trustee) may request substitution of the
    new spouse as the surviving insured, subject to:
    • New spouse is between ages 45 and 80
    • New spouse submits to full underwriting
    • Premium adjustment reflects new underwriting class
    This provision is available only in Year 1 following the
    first insured's death and only if Premium Continuation Rider
    (Rider 3) has not yet been triggered.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: POLICY CONVERSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 The owner may convert this survivorship term policy to a
    survivorship permanent policy (whole life or universal life)
    without evidence of insurability before the earlier of the
    policy's 15th anniversary or the younger insured's 80th birthday.

5.2 After the death of the first insured, the surviving insured
    may convert the remaining coverage to an individual permanent
    life policy on his or her own life, without evidence of
    insurability, within 1 year of the first insured's death.
    The conversion amount may not exceed the original face amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: INCONTESTABILITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This policy becomes incontestable, except for non-payment of
premiums, after 2 years from the policy date during the joint
lifetime of both insureds. After the first insured's death, a
separate 2-year contestability period runs with respect to the
surviving insured's participation in the application.
