POLICY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Covered Avocation" means any one of the four avocations declared
and endorsed in the Avocation Endorsement Schedule of this policy.

"Undeclared Hazardous Activity" means any extreme or hazardous
activity not listed in the Avocation Endorsement Schedule above.

"Escalation Event" means a material increase in the risk profile
of a Covered Avocation, such as: increasing skydiving to wingsuit
proximity flying; progressing SCUBA to cave diving; ascending
above 18,000 feet in mountaineering.

"Incident Report" means a written summary of any accident, near-
miss, or injury occurring during a Covered Avocation, submitted
to the company within 60 days.

SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 We will pay the death benefit of $1,000,000 upon the insured's
    death during the policy period, from any cause including the
    Covered Avocations, subject to the exclusions below.

1.2 Payment within 30 days of satisfactory proof of death.

1.3 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: Death by suicide within 2 years of the
    policy date limits benefit to return of premiums paid.

1.4 EXCLUSION – UNDECLARED HAZARDOUS ACTIVITIES: Death or injury
    arising from any Undeclared Hazardous Activity is excluded.
    Examples of excluded activities (non-exhaustive): base jumping,
    wingsuit proximity flying, solo high-altitude mountaineering
    above 18,000 ft, cave or wreck penetration SCUBA, free solo
    climbing, whitewater class V+ kayaking, motorsport racing.
    Adding a new hazardous activity requires prior underwriting
    approval and may result in additional surcharge.

1.5 EXCLUSION – ESCALATION EVENTS: If the insured engages in an
    Escalation Event without notifying the company and obtaining
    written approval, any claim arising from that activity will be
    treated as arising from an Undeclared Hazardous Activity and
    will be denied or adjusted accordingly.

1.6 EXCLUSION – INTOXICATION: No benefit for death or injury
    occurring while the insured is impaired by alcohol or unprescribed
    controlled substances while engaged in any Covered Avocation.

1.7 COVERED AVOCATION CLAIM PROCEDURE: For claims involving a
    Covered Avocation, the beneficiary must provide:
    • Certified death certificate
    • Incident report if available
    • Names of at least one witness or guide, if applicable
    • Equipment logs or jump logs (for skydiving claims)
    Failure to provide avocation documentation may delay claim
    processing but will not by itself result in denial if cause
    of death is otherwise confirmed.

SECTION 2: AVOCATION ENDORSEMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ENDORSEMENT A – ROCK AND BIG WALL CLIMBING
Coverage extended to: technical rock climbing up to Grade 5.13;
  trad and sport climbing; multi-pitch routes; big wall aid climbing
Excluded within this endorsement: free solo climbing without rope;
  ice climbing in avalanche zones; competition climbing (covered
  under standard terms as a spectator sport, not a hazardous activity)

ENDORSEMENT B – OPEN-WATER SCUBA DIVING
Coverage extended to: recreational and advanced open-water SCUBA
  to 130-foot depth maximum; night diving; drift diving; shore diving
Excluded within this endorsement: cave, cavern, or wreck penetration
  SCUBA; mixed-gas rebreather diving; commercial diving; diving
  at altitude above 8,000 feet above sea level

ENDORSEMENT C – BACKCOUNTRY AND OFF-PISTE SKIING
Coverage extended to: off-piste skiing with guide or certified
  companion; backcountry touring; heli-skiing (commercial operators)
Excluded within this endorsement: free solo backcountry skiing in
  known avalanche areas without a beacon and partner; ski racing
  events on courses not sanctioned by a national federation

ENDORSEMENT D – RECREATIONAL SKYDIVING
Coverage extended to: AFF-certified recreational skydiving; tandem
  jumps; formation skydiving; canopy piloting; up to 500 logged jumps
  over the policy period without re-underwriting
Excluded within this endorsement: BASE jumping; wingsuit proximity
  flying; wingsuit formation from aircraft is covered up to 300 jumps

JUMP LOG REVIEW: At each policy anniversary, the insured must
submit a current skydiving logbook summary showing total jumps
accumulated. If jumps exceed 500 without re-underwriting, the
skydiving endorsement is suspended (coverage for other avocations
continues) until a re-underwriting review is completed. Additional
premium may apply for > 500 jumps.

SECTION 3: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: AVOCATION INJURY INDEMNITY RIDER
Premium: $275/year

Pays a lump-sum indemnity upon a qualifying non-fatal injury
sustained during a Covered Avocation, regardless of loss of income:
  • Fracture requiring surgery: $10,000
  • Fracture not requiring surgery: $3,000
  • Dislocation requiring reduction: $2,500
  • Decompression sickness (SCUBA, treated): $5,000
  • Concussion with hospitalization (> 24 hours): $7,500
  • Spinal injury with neurological deficit: $25,000
  • Amputation of digit (climbing-related frostbite): $8,000
  Per-occurrence maximum: $25,000; annual maximum: $50,000
  Lifetime maximum: $150,000; rider terminates on lifetime max
  No elimination period; benefit paid within 30 days of documentation

RIDER 2: TOTAL DISABILITY INCOME SUPPLEMENT RIDER
Premium: $200/year

If the insured becomes totally disabled (unable to practice medicine
as an emergency physician) due to injury sustained during any
Covered Avocation OR from any other cause:
  • Pays $6,000/month after 90-day elimination period
  • Maximum benefit period: 24 months
  • No occupation-switch clause; benefit based on physician occupation
  • Terminates at rider's anniversary following insured's 55th birthday

RIDER 3: AVOCATION SURCHARGE REMOVAL OPTION
Premium: Included at no additional charge

If the insured ceases engaging in any Covered Avocation for 24+
consecutive months and provides written certification and declaration:
  • Corresponding avocation surcharge is removed
  • Premium is recalculated for remaining policy years
  • Coverage for that avocation is terminated
  • If the insured wishes to resume the avocation, re-underwriting
    is required and a new surcharge (based on attained age and
    current risk profile) will apply

SECTION 4: AVOCATION NOTIFICATION OBLIGATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 The insured must notify the company within 60 days of any
    material change in avocation risk, including:
    • Initiation of a new hazardous activity not covered
    • Escalation Event (as defined above)
    • Achievement of instructor-level certification in any avocation
      (which may trigger reclassification of risk level)
    • Any avocation-related injury resulting in hospitalization

4.2 Failure to notify may result in suspension of avocation-related
    coverage pending review, but will not affect coverage for
    non-avocation causes of death.

4.3 The company may request an annual avocation questionnaire.
    Failure to return the questionnaire within 30 days of receipt
    may result in avocation endorsement review.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

of the ongoing nature of avocation risk disclosures and the
