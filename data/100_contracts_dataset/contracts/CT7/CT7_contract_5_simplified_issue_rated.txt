POLICY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Pre-Existing Condition" means any condition for which the insured
received medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment within 24 months
prior to the policy date.

"Accidental Bodily Injury" means bodily injury caused directly and
exclusively by accidental means, independent of illness or disease.

"Natural Death" for purposes of this policy means death from any
cause other than accidental bodily injury.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 We will pay $150,000 upon the insured's death during the policy
    period, subject to the exclusions and limitations below.

1.2 Payment will be made to the beneficiary within 30 days of
    receiving satisfactory proof of death and a completed claim form.

1.3 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: Death by suicide within 2 years of the
    policy date is limited to a return of premiums paid without
    interest.

1.4 PRE-EXISTING CONDITION LIMITATION (Years 1–2 Only):
    If death occurs within the first 2 policy years and results
    from a Pre-Existing Condition, the benefit is limited to a
    return of all premiums paid plus 10% interest, compounded
    annually. This limitation does not apply after the end of the
    second policy year (May 20, 2026), at which point the full face
    amount is payable for any cause.

    The following conditions are expressly acknowledged as Pre-Existing:
      • Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (diagnosed 2019)
      • Hypertension (diagnosed 2021, currently medicated)
      • Elevated triglycerides (managed with diet and medication)

1.5 EXCLUSION – ILLEGAL ACTIVITY: No benefit is payable if death
    results directly from the insured's commission of or attempt
    to commit an illegal act classified as a felony under applicable
    state or federal law.

1.6 EXCLUSION – NARCOTICS: No benefit is payable if death results
    from the use of non-prescribed narcotics or controlled substances,
    except in cases of accidental overdose of prescribed medication
    taken as directed.

SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: ACCIDENTAL DEATH BENEFIT RIDER
Premium: $180/year

Pays an additional $150,000 (doubling the total benefit to $300,000)
if death results directly from Accidental Bodily Injury:
• Accident must be the sole cause; death must occur within 180 days
• Excludes: suicide, self-inflicted injury, drug or alcohol influence,
  piloting non-commercial aircraft, war, professional athletics
• NOT subject to the pre-existing condition limitation in Section 1.4;
  accidental death benefit is payable from policy date forward
• Rider terminates at the insured's age 65 (in Year 8)
  After age 65, the rider premium ceases and no ADB is payable

RIDER 2: TERMINAL ILLNESS BENEFIT RIDER
Premium: $145/year

If the insured is diagnosed by a licensed physician as having a
terminal illness with life expectancy of 12 months or fewer:
• Up to 60% of the in-force face amount may be advanced ($90,000 max)
• Subject to the pre-existing condition limitation in Year 1 only
  (not Year 2, as the rider has its own 12-month waiting period)
• Minimum advance: $15,000
• No actuarial discount applied; full requested amount disbursed
• Death benefit reduced dollar-for-dollar; remaining benefit paid
  to beneficiary at death
