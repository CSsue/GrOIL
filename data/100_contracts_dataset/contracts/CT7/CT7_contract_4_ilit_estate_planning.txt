POLICY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Terminal Condition" means a condition expected to result in death
within 18 months, certified by two licensed physicians including
at least one specialist in the relevant field.

"Cognitive Impairment" means a deterioration or loss of intellectual
capacity as measured by clinical evidence or standardized tests,
which requires substantial supervision to protect oneself or others.

"Substantial Activity" means a functional limitation requiring
assistance with 3 or more Activities of Daily Living.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 We will pay $3,000,000 to the named beneficiary upon receipt
    of satisfactory proof of the insured's death during the policy
    period, provided all premiums have been paid.

1.2 Claims are processed within 30 calendar days of complete
    documentation. Interest at the current federal applicable rate
    will accrue on any undisputed claim not paid within 30 days.

1.3 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: Suicide within 2 years of the policy date
    limits the benefit to a return of premiums paid without interest.

1.4 AVIATION EXCLUSION: Death while operating or training as crew
    in any experimental, military, or ultralight aircraft is excluded.
    Passenger travel in commercial or private certificated aircraft
    is fully covered.

1.5 WAR EXCLUSION: Death resulting from voluntary participation
    in armed combat or insurrection is excluded. Death in a war zone
    in a non-combatant capacity is covered.

SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT – TERMINAL CONDITION
Premium: Included at no additional charge

• Up to 80% of the face amount accelerated upon certification of
  Terminal Condition (maximum $2,400,000)
• Minimum advance: $50,000
• Actuarial discount: 7% per year, applied to expected benefit period
• Trustee authorization required in addition to insured consent
• Remaining death benefit paid to trust upon insured's death
• Trigger: Life expectancy ≤ 18 months (more generous than standard)

RIDER 2: ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT – CHRONIC/COGNITIVE DECLINE
Premium: $1,800/year

• May access up to 3% of face amount per month upon qualifying
  Cognitive Impairment or Substantial Activity limitation
• Annual maximum: 24% of face amount ($720,000/year)
• Lifetime maximum: 60% of face amount ($1,800,000)
• No elimination period if hospitalized; 60-day elimination period
  otherwise
• Independent geriatric assessment required at time of claim
• Monthly benefit coordinated with any LTC insurance in force

RIDER 3: CHARITABLE GIVING RIDER
Premium: $800/year

Upon the insured's death, the policy owner (trustee) may elect,
within 90 days of receiving the death benefit, to redirect up to
10% of the net death benefit (up to $100,000) to one or more
qualified 501(c)(3) organizations designated by the trust instrument.
This election is irrevocable once made. Charitable redirect does
not reduce the taxable death benefit for estate tax purposes but
may generate an estate tax charitable deduction.

SECTION 3: PREMIUM PAYMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Annual Premium: $46,400.00
    Due Date: October 7 of each year
    Payment Mode: Annual (wire transfer or ACH from trust account)

3.2 PREMIUM NOTICE: Notice will be sent to the Trustee at the
    address of record 60 days in advance of the due date to allow
    Crummey notices to be issued to trust beneficiaries.

3.3 GRACE PERIOD: 62 days (extended for estate planning policies
    to allow trust administration procedures to be followed). If
    death occurs during the grace period, unpaid premium is deducted
    from the death benefit.

3.4 PREMIUM FLEXIBILITY: The owner may request a one-time premium
    deferral of up to 90 additional days in any policy year, subject
    to approval and payment of a deferral fee of $500. Not available
    in consecutive years.

SECTION 4: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 The trustee may convert all or part of this policy to permanent
    life insurance without evidence of insurability before the
    earlier of:
    • The policy's 15th anniversary, or
    • The insured's 80th birthday
    Minimum conversion amount: $500,000

4.2 The insured's advanced age at conversion will be reflected in
    permanent policy premiums. Meridian Legacy's current whole life,
    survivorship, or universal life products are available for
    conversion.

4.3 All riders terminate upon conversion unless re-elected and
    approved on the new permanent policy.
