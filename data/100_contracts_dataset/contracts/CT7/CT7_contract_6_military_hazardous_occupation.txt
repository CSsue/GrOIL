POLICY DEFINITIONS

"Declared Combat Zone" means any area designated as a combat zone
by Executive Order of the President of the United States or by
declaration of the Secretary of Defense.

United States or its allies, as distinguished from enemy action.

"Occupational Separation" means honorable discharge, retirement,
or release from active duty service.

SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE – FULL WAR COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 FULL COMBAT COVERAGE: This policy provides full death benefit
    coverage for death occurring in any combat zone, including:
    • Death from enemy action, hostile fire, or improvised explosive
      devices (IEDs)
    • Death from friendly fire while on deployment
    • Death from training accidents at any domestic or overseas
      installation
    • Death from disease or illness contracted while on deployment
    • Death occurring during authorized rest and recuperation (R&R)

    This represents an express waiver of all standard war exclusions
    for this policy, in consideration of the military surcharges
    collected.

1.2 EXCLUSION – UNAUTHORIZED ABSENCE (UA/AWOL): Death occurring
    while the insured is in an Unauthorized Absence (UA) or AWOL
    status is excluded. If death occurs while UA/AWOL and the insured
    is later found to have been a prisoner of war or under duress,
    the exclusion will not be applied and the benefit will be paid.

1.3 EXCLUSION – TREASON OR COLLABORATION: No benefit is payable if
    death occurs as a direct consequence of the insured's voluntary
    collaboration with a foreign adversary or commission of treason
    against the United States, as established by final court-martial
    finding or federal court judgment.

1.4 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: Death by suicide within 2 years of the
    policy date limits the benefit to a return of premiums paid.

1.5 CIVILIAN TRANSITION: If the insured separates honorably from
    active duty, the military occupation surcharge is returned
    as a credit to future premiums (pro-rated for months remaining
    in that policy year). Coverage continues uninterrupted.

SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: TRAUMATIC INJURY / COMBAT WOUNDS RIDER
Premium: $135/year

Pays a lump-sum benefit if the insured sustains a qualifying
traumatic injury in the line of duty that results in permanent
impairment, regardless of whether death occurs. This rider pays
upon injury, not death, and does not reduce the death benefit.

Qualifying Injuries and Benefits:
• Loss of both hands or feet, or one of each: $200,000
• Loss of sight in both eyes: $200,000
• Loss of one hand or foot AND one eye: $150,000
• Loss of one hand or foot: $100,000
• Loss of sight in one eye: $75,000
• Traumatic brain injury (moderate to severe, VA-rated): $100,000
• 50%+ burns to face, body, or both: $150,000

Each injury benefit is payable once; total rider benefit capped at
$200,000 lifetime. Rider continues in force after traumatic injury
benefit is paid (death benefit unaffected). Rider terminates at
insured's age 55 or upon Occupational Separation, whichever is later.

RIDER 2: FAMILY READINESS BENEFIT RIDER
Premium: $80/year

Designed to assist military families during deployment:
• If the insured is deployed to a Declared Combat Zone, the rider
  pays a monthly family support stipend of $300/month while
  deployed, up to 36 months total over the policy life
• Benefit begins 60 days after confirmed deployment orders
• No elimination period for first deployment after policy date
• Funded by the rider premium; does not reduce the death benefit
• Insured must submit DD-214 or deployment orders to initiate

RIDER 3: WAIVER OF PREMIUM – COMBAT DISABILITY
Premium: $75/year

If the insured is 100% disabled (as determined by the VA or a
DoD Physical Evaluation Board) due to combat-related injury:
• All premiums are waived from date of disability determination
• Coverage continues in full
• Waiver continues for life or until death, whichever comes first
• This rider does not have an age-65 termination; it lasts for life
• Also triggered by VA rating of 100% disability from any
  service-connected condition, not limited to combat alone

SECTION 3: OCCUPATIONAL TRANSITION PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Upon Occupational Separation from military service, the insured
    must notify Valor Life within 90 days. At that time:
    • Military and combat surcharges are removed
    • Premium is recalculated at civilian rates for attained age
    • No evidence of insurability is required
    • Coverage amount remains unchanged

3.2 If the insured re-enlists or enters reserve duty after separation,
    he or she may request reinstatement of military coverage terms
    (including full war coverage) upon written notification and
    proof of service. Re-underwriting is not required; surcharges
    will be re-applied based on new duty status.

3.3 Transition to officer status does not automatically change the
    surcharge class; a new occupational review will be conducted.
