LADDERED COVERAGE STRUCTURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This policy provides coverage through three distinct, declining
tranches of term insurance, all issued under a single policy number
with a single application and one annual premium. Each tranche
has its own coverage amount and expiration date. The total death
benefit is the sum of all active tranches at the time of death.

TRANCHE 1 – PRIMARY COVERAGE
  Face Amount: $1,000,000
  Coverage Period: 30 years (expires June 1, 2054)
  Annual Premium: $1,890.00
  Purpose: Long-term income replacement; retirement bridge

TRANCHE 2 – MORTGAGE AND EDUCATION LAYER
  Face Amount: $500,000
  Coverage Period: 20 years (expires June 1, 2044)
  Annual Premium: $620.00
  Purpose: Mortgage payoff; college funding for dependents

TRANCHE 3 – EARLY-YEARS INTENSIVE COVERAGE
  Face Amount: $500,000
  Coverage Period: 10 years (expires June 1, 2034)
  Annual Premium: $280.00
  Purpose: Maximum protection during peak financial obligation years

TOTAL ANNUAL PREMIUM: $2,790.00 (including riders below)
  Rider Premiums: $155.00/year (see Section 2)
  Base Tranche Premiums: $2,790.00 total

DEATH BENEFIT BY PERIOD:
  Policy Years 1–10 (now–2034):    $2,000,000 (all 3 tranches active)
  Policy Years 11–20 (2034–2044):  $1,500,000 (tranches 1 and 2)
  Policy Years 21–30 (2044–2054):  $1,000,000 (tranche 1 only)

RATIONALE: This laddered structure mirrors anticipated decreasing
financial obligations as dependents mature, the mortgage is paid
off, and retirement assets accumulate, while delivering higher
death benefit in the early high-need period without purchasing
a single expensive 30-year policy for the full $2,000,000.

POLICY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Active Tranche" means any coverage tranche that has not yet reached
its scheduled expiration date.

"Total Death Benefit" means the sum of all active tranche face
amounts at the time of the insured's death.

"Tranche Conversion" means the exercise of the right to convert
an individual tranche to a permanent policy.

SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 We will pay the Total Death Benefit to the beneficiary upon
    the insured's death while this policy is in force and all
    premiums are current.

1.2 Payment within 30 days of satisfactory proof of death.

1.3 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: Death by suicide within 2 years of the
    policy date limits benefit to return of premiums paid without
    interest. After 2 years, full Total Death Benefit is payable.

1.4 EXCLUSION – AVIATION: Standard aviation exclusion applies only
    to Tranche 3. Tranches 1 and 2 provide full coverage regardless
    of aviation activity, including operation of private aircraft.
    This differential exclusion structure is in consideration of
    the insured's declared recreational pilot status and the
    correspondingly lower premium for Tranche 3.

1.5 CLAIM DOCUMENTATION: A single claim and proof of death applies
    to all active tranches. Payment will be made in one combined
    disbursement for all active tranches.

SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: WAIVER OF ALL TRANCHE PREMIUMS – DISABILITY
Premium: $155/year

If the insured becomes totally disabled (unable to engage in any
gainful occupation for which suited by education, training, or
experience) for 6+ continuous months before age 60:
• All tranche premiums are waived during the disability period
• Coverage in all active tranches continues in full
• Waiver continues until recovery, return to work, or age 65
• Upon recovery, regular premiums resume; waiver of premium rider
  premium itself is also waived during disability
• If disability begins before the expiration of Tranche 3 (2034),
  the waiver covers premiums of all tranches then active

ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT (no additional premium):
• Terminal illness (life expectancy ≤ 12 months): up to 75% of the
  Total Death Benefit at the time of acceleration ($1,500,000 max)
• Benefit paid from Tranche 1 first, then Tranche 2
• Actuarial discount: approximately 7% per year of remaining
  life expectancy
• Does not affect Tranche 3 unless explicitly requested

SECTION 3: TRANCHE MANAGEMENT PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 INDIVIDUAL TRANCHE CONVERSION: Each tranche may be converted
    independently to a permanent life insurance policy without
    evidence of insurability. Conversion of a tranche terminates
    that tranche under this policy. Remaining tranches continue.
    Conversion must occur before the tranche's expiration date or
    the insured's 70th birthday, whichever is earlier. Minimum
    conversion: $100,000.

3.2 TRANCHE REDUCTION: The insured may reduce the face amount of
    any tranche on any policy anniversary by written request. A
    reduction is irrevocable. Premium is adjusted downward
    accordingly. Minimum remaining tranche face amount: $100,000.

3.3 TRANCHE CANCELLATION: Any individual tranche may be cancelled
    without affecting the remaining tranches. Cancellation is
    irrevocable. Once cancelled, a tranche cannot be reinstated.

3.4 LAPSE PREVENTION: If premiums are unpaid at the end of the
    grace period, coverage lapses on the tranche(s) with the
    shortest remaining coverage period first, in order, preserving
    the longer-duration tranche(s) as long as possible. This
    sequential lapse provision is intended to protect the insured's
    long-term coverage in cases of financial hardship.

3.5 TRANCHE ADDITION: The insured may not add new tranches to this
    policy after the policy date. Additional term coverage requires
    a new policy application.
