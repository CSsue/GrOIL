POLICY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
"Total Disability" means complete inability, due to injury or
sickness, to engage in any gainful occupation for which the insured
is reasonably suited by education, training, or experience.

"Critical Illness" means any of the following conditions first
diagnosed after the policy date: heart attack, stroke, invasive
cancer, kidney failure, major organ transplant, or coronary artery
bypass surgery.

"Base Premium" means the annual premium attributable solely to the
base policy face amount, excluding all rider premiums.

SECTION 1: BASE POLICY COVERAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 We will pay the face amount upon the insured's death during the
    policy period, provided the policy is in force and all due
    premiums have been paid.

1.2 The death benefit is paid to the named beneficiary within 21
    business days of receiving a completed claim with satisfactory
    proof of death.

1.3 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: If death occurs by suicide, whether sane
    or insane, within 2 years of the policy date, the benefit is
    limited to premiums paid without interest.

1.4 EXCLUSION – MATERIAL MISREPRESENTATION: If the application
    contains a material misrepresentation discovered within 2 years
    of the policy date, we may void the policy and refund premiums.
    After 2 years, the policy is incontestable except for
    non-payment of premiums.

SECTION 2: ATTACHED RIDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RIDER 1: CRITICAL ILLNESS ACCELERATED BENEFIT RIDER
Premium: $155/year

Upon first diagnosis of a covered Critical Illness:
• Lump-sum advance of 50% of the face amount (up to $250,000)
• No actuarial discount applied to this advance
• Remaining 50% of death benefit preserved for beneficiaries
• May be used for any purpose (medical or non-medical)
• One-time benefit per policy; rider terminates after claim
• 30-day waiting period from policy date before benefit eligible
• Must survive at least 30 days after diagnosis to receive benefit

Covered Critical Illnesses:
Heart attack, stroke, invasive cancer (excluding skin cancer in situ),
end-stage renal failure, major organ transplant, coronary artery
bypass surgery, blindness (both eyes, permanent), ALS.

RIDER 2: WAIVER OF PREMIUM – TOTAL DISABILITY
Premium: $85/year

If the insured becomes Totally Disabled prior to age 65 and the
disability lasts at least 90 consecutive days:
• All future premiums (base + riders) are waived during disability
• Return of Premium benefit continues to accrue as if premiums paid
• Waiver terminates at recovery, death, or age 65
• Retroactive: premiums paid during 90-day elimination period
  will be refunded upon approval of waiver claim

SECTION 3: PREMIUM PAYMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Total Annual Premium: $1,420.00
    Due Date: March 3 of each year
    Payment Mode: Annual
    Electronic Funds Transfer discount: $25/year applied

3.2 GRACE PERIOD: 31 days. Coverage remains in force during this
    period. Any death benefit paid during grace period will have
    outstanding premium deducted at time of claim.

3.3 REINSTATEMENT: A lapsed policy may be reinstated within 3 years
    of lapse date upon payment of all back premiums plus 6% interest,
    satisfactory evidence of insurability, and a written application.
    Reinstatement restores the return-of-premium benefit clock but
    does not restart the contestability or suicide period.

SECTION 4: POLICY CONVERSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 The owner may convert all or part of this policy to any
    permanent life insurance product offered by BlueStar Life at
    time of conversion, without evidence of insurability, subject to:
    • Conversion must occur before the policy's 15th anniversary, or
    • Before the insured's 65th birthday, whichever is earlier
    • Minimum conversion amount: $100,000
    • Converted permanent policy will not include return-of-premium

4.2 Upon conversion, all riders attached to this policy lapse unless
    the equivalent rider is available and re-applied for on the new
    permanent policy.

SECTION 5: OWNERSHIP AND ASSIGNMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 The owner may assign this policy as collateral for a loan.
    Collateral assignment does not change the beneficiary designation
    but the assignee's interest takes priority over beneficiary.

5.2 Absolute assignment transfers all ownership rights. The company
    must receive written notice. The return of premium benefit
    transfers with absolute assignment.

5.3 Policy may be placed in an irrevocable trust. If so, the
    trustee becomes the owner and controls beneficiary designation.

SECTION 6: MISSTATEMENT OF AGE OR SEX
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
If the insured's age or sex was misstated in the application, the
death benefit will be adjusted to the amount that the premium paid
would have purchased at the correct age and sex. No rescission
will occur solely on account of age or sex misstatement.
