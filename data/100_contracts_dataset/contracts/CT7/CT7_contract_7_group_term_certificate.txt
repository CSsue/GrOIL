SECTION 1: COVERAGE PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 ACTIVE WORK REQUIREMENT: Coverage is effective only on days
    the employee is actively at work. If the employee is not
    actively at work on the effective date of coverage, coverage
    begins on the first day returned to active work.

1.2 DEATH BENEFIT PAYMENT: Upon satisfactory proof of death,
    the basic and supplemental life benefit will be paid to the
    designated beneficiary within 30 days.

1.3 EXCLUSION – SUICIDE: No suicide exclusion applies under this
    group plan after the employee's first 12 months of participation.
    During the first 12 months, death by suicide results in a
    return of premiums paid by the employee (not the employer-paid
    basic premium, which is forfeited to the plan).

1.4 SEAT BELT BENEFIT: If death occurs in an automobile accident
    and it is confirmed by official accident report that the insured
    was wearing a seat belt at the time of death, an additional
    10% of the life benefit (maximum $25,000) will be paid.

1.5 EXCLUSION – FELONY: No death benefit is payable to a beneficiary
    who feloniously and intentionally causes the death of the insured.
    In such case, the benefit is paid to the contingent beneficiary
    or the employee's estate.

SECTION 2: OPTIONAL BENEFIT FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT (ADB):
• Available on the basic and supplemental life coverage combined
• If diagnosed with terminal illness (life expectancy ≤ 12 months):
  up to 80% of total life benefit may be advanced ($360,000 max)
• No actuarial discount; benefit paid in lump sum
• Remaining benefit paid to beneficiary at death
• Does not affect dependent coverage

EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (EAP) DEATH BENEFIT:

death, including:
• Up to 5 sessions of grief counseling (in-person or telehealth)
• Claim assistance concierge service
• Estate administration resource referrals
No charge; included as part of the group plan.

SECTION 3: PORTABILITY AND CONVERSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 PORTABILITY: Upon termination of employment (for any reason
    other than non-payment of premiums), the employee may port
    (continue) the supplemental life coverage at group rates,
    without evidence of insurability, for up to the full face amount
    in force at termination. Ported coverage continues as
    individual group-rate coverage for 5 years or to age 70,
    whichever comes first. Election must be made within 31 days
    of termination; premiums will be direct-billed.

3.2 CONVERSION: The employee may also convert any or all group life
    coverage to an individual permanent life insurance policy issued
    by Pinnacle Group Benefits' affiliate (Pinnacle Individual Life)
    without evidence of insurability. Conversion must be elected
    within 31 days of losing group coverage. Premiums on the
    converted policy will be based on attained age and standard
    underwriting class; no preferred rates are guaranteed.

3.3 DEPENDENT CONVERSION: Dependent life coverage for a spouse or
    child who loses eligibility (e.g., divorce, child reaching plan
    maximum age) may be converted to individual coverage within
    31 days of losing eligibility.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: LIFE EVENTS AND ENROLLMENT CHANGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 QUALIFYING LIFE EVENTS: Marriage, divorce, birth or adoption
    of a child, or a spouse's loss of other group coverage entitles
    the employee to enroll in or increase coverage outside of the
    annual open enrollment period. Changes must be elected within
    31 days of the qualifying event.

4.2 ANNUAL OPEN ENROLLMENT: Each year, typically in November, the
    employee may increase supplemental coverage by up to one salary
    multiple (e.g., from 2x to 3x) without EOI, up to the plan
    maximum ($500,000 total supplemental coverage).

4.3 FAMILY MEDICAL LEAVE: Coverage continues in full during
    approved FMLA leave for up to 12 weeks. Employee must continue
    premium payments; the employer will establish a payment
    arrangement for the leave period.

SECTION 5: PLAN CHANGES AND TERMINATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 The employer (Northridge Medical Systems, Inc.) may amend,
    suspend, or terminate the group plan with 60 days' notice to
    covered employees. The insurer may non-renew the group policy
    on its annual renewal date with 90 days' notice to the employer.

5.2 Upon plan termination, all covered employees are entitled to
    exercise conversion rights as described in Section 3.2.

5.3 Coverage amounts are subject to a maximum of $2,000,000 in
    basic and supplemental life combined for any individual. Coverage
    in excess of this requires separate underwriting.
