SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy intended for mortgage
    protection and family security.

1.2 The death benefit decreases according to the schedule attached to the
    policy and is designed to broadly track a declining home loan balance.

1.3 If the insured survives the full term and all base premiums are paid,
    we refund 100% of paid base premiums.

1.4 This policy may be assigned as mortgage collateral.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies while the policy is in force, we will pay the
    death benefit applicable for that policy year.

2.2 The death benefit is:
    Years 1-5:   $420,000
    Years 6-10:  $360,000
    Years 11-15: $290,000
    Years 16-20: $220,000

2.3 Any valid collateral assignee on record is paid first to the extent
    of its secured interest. Any remaining proceeds are paid to the
    beneficiary.

2.4 We require proof of death, claim forms, and any release required by a
    recorded assignee.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is alive at the end of the term and the policy has
    remained in force, we will pay the owner $42,800.

3.2 EARLY SURRENDER VALUE:
    Policy Years 1-5:   0%
    Policy Years 6-9:   20%
    Policy Years 10-14: 50%
    Policy Years 15-18: 75%
    Policy Years 19-20: 100%

3.3 If the policy is surrendered while a collateral assignment is on
    record, any vested ROP value is first payable to the assignee.

3.4 Rider charges and assignment filing fees are not refundable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Annual premium of $2,140 is due each April 10.

4.2 Premium is level even though the death benefit decreases.

4.3 A 31-day grace period applies.

4.4 Two or more late payments reduce the end-of-term ROP by 3% per
    additional late year.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 This policy may be converted to eligible permanent insurance before
    the earlier of policy year 10 or age 60.

5.2 The converted amount may not exceed the death benefit then in force.

5.3 Upon full conversion, any vested ROP value is forfeited.

5.4 Partial conversion is permitted in increments of $25,000.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 The owner may remove a collateral assignment upon written release.

6.2 Refinancing of the mortgage does not automatically terminate this
    policy or any assignment.

6.3 This policy is not lender-issued mortgage insurance.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 SUICIDE: If the insured dies by suicide within 24 months from issue,
    we will refund premiums paid for the base policy.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest this policy within two years from
    issue for material misrepresentation.

7.3 No occupational, travel, or residential property exclusion applies.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8.2 Reinstatement requires satisfactory evidence of insurability and

8.3 Any prior assignment remains in effect unless released.
