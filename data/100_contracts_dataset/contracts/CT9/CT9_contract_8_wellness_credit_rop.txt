SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy with a wellness credit
    program.

1.2 If the insured survives the term and all base premiums are paid, we
    will refund 100% of paid base premiums.

1.3 We may add a non-guaranteed wellness credit at the end of term if the
    owner completed annual wellness certifications.

1.4 The wellness credit is not a dividend and is not guaranteed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies during the policy term while coverage is in
    force, we will pay $325,000 to the beneficiary.

2.2 Any wellness credit is payable only at the end of term and is not
    added to the death benefit.

2.3 Claimants must provide proof of death and identity as requested.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is living at end of term and all base premiums are
    paid, we will pay $37,800.

3.2 For each policy year in which the insured completes required wellness
    certification and no tobacco use is reported, we may credit 0.5% of
    the annual base premium to a non-guaranteed end-of-term wellness pool.

3.3 EARLY SURRENDER VALUE:
    Years 1-5:   0%
    Years 6-10:  30%
    Years 11-15: 60%
    Years 16-19: 85%
    Year 20:     100%

3.4 Upon surrender before end of term, only guaranteed vested ROP is
    payable. No wellness credit is payable on surrender or death.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Annual premium of $1,890 is due each April 10.

4.2 Grace period is 31 days.

4.3 No wellness credit is earned for a policy year in which premium
    remains unpaid beyond the due date for more than 30 days.

4.4 Premium discounts reduce the paid premium amount included in the
    guaranteed ROP calculation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 This policy may be converted to eligible permanent insurance before
    the end of policy year 15.

5.2 Guaranteed ROP on the converted portion is forfeited unless the
    permanent policy form allows a conversion credit.

5.3 Non-guaranteed wellness credit is forfeited on any conversion.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Wellness certification may include health questionnaires, biometric
    screening results, or physician confirmation.

6.2 Failure to complete a certification does not terminate the policy or
    reduce the guaranteed death benefit.

6.3 Administrative procedures for the wellness program may be modified on
    a non-discriminatory basis.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 SUICIDE: If death by suicide occurs within 24 months from issue, we
    return the base premiums paid.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: This policy is contestable for two years from issue
    for material misrepresentation.

7.3 Misrepresentation in an annual wellness certification may affect only
    wellness credit eligibility unless independently material to coverage.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 8: REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Reinstatement is available within 5 years after lapse if the insured
    is living and evidence of insurability is satisfactory to us.

8.2 Overdue premiums must be paid with 5% annual interest.

8.3 No wellness credit accrues for any lapsed period.
