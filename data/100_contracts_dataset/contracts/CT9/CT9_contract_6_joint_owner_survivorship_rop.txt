SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy.

1.2 If the insured survives the full policy term and all required base
    premiums are paid, we will return 100% of paid base premiums.

1.3 Joint ownership means either owner may exercise policy rights while
    both are living unless we receive written restriction signed by both.

1.4 Upon death of one joint owner, the surviving joint owner becomes sole
    owner, subject to any recorded assignment.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies while this policy is in force, we will pay
    $275,000 to the beneficiary.

2.2 If the beneficiary is also a joint owner and survives the insured,
    claim payment discharges our liability.

2.3 We require proof of death, claimant identity, and documents needed
    to establish ownership at the time of claim.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is alive on April 10, 2046 and the policy is in force,
    we will pay the then current owner the paid base premiums, projected
    to equal $44,400.

3.2 If the policy is surrendered before the end of term, the vested ROP
    percentage is:
    Years 1-5:   0%
    Years 6-10:  30%
    Years 11-15: 60%
    Years 16-18: 80%
    Years 19-20: 100%

3.3 If one joint owner dies before the insured and no surviving joint
    owner remains, the vested ROP becomes payable only to the successor
    owner recorded by us.

3.4 Any premium not actually paid because of a refund or reversal is
    excluded from the ROP amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Quarterly premium of $555 is due on April 10, July 10, October 10,
    and January 10 of each policy year.

4.2 Annual conversion of premium mode is allowed upon request.

4.3 Grace period is 31 days after each due date.

4.4 A quarterly premium paid more than 20 days late is refunded at end of
    term only at 90% of the amount paid.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 This policy may be converted to permanent insurance before the end of
    policy year 15.

5.2 If joint owners request conversion, both must sign unless only one
    owner remains of record.

5.3 Full conversion terminates ROP. Partial conversion proportionally
    reduces future ROP and the face amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Either joint owner may change beneficiary unless an irrevocable
    beneficiary consent is required.

6.2 Either joint owner may assign the policy as collateral unless we have
    recorded a restriction requiring both signatures.

6.3 We may rely on the most recent ownership instruction in good faith.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 SUICIDE: If the insured dies by suicide within two years from issue,
    we return premiums paid for the base policy.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest the policy for material
    misrepresentation within two years of issue.

7.3 No exclusion applies due to change in marital status.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8.2 Reinstatement requires payment of arrears with 5% interest and

8.3 If ownership changed during lapse, the recorded owner at time of
    reinstatement must request reinstatement.
