SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy with an Education Support
    Endorsement intended to coordinate death proceeds with a designated
    trust for dependent children.

1.2 If the insured survives the full term and all base premiums are paid,
    we will refund 100% of base premiums paid.

1.3 The endorsement does not create a separate trust and only directs
    payment if a valid trustee designation is on file.

1.4 This policy is intended for family planning rather than investment.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies while this policy is in force, we pay $300,000.

2.2 If a valid trust designation is on file, proceeds are payable to the
    trustee named in the beneficiary designation.

2.3 The owner may direct that 20% of the death benefit be paid
    immediately and the remainder be distributed annually over five years.

2.4 A settlement option election may be changed while the insured is living.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is living at end of term and this policy is in force,
    we will pay the owner all base premiums paid, up to $39,360.

3.2 EARLY SURRENDER VALUE:
    Years 1-4:   0%
    Years 5-9:   25%
    Years 10-14: 55%
    Years 15-18: 80%
    Years 19-20: 100%

3.3 If the owner dies but the insured remains living, ownership passes to
    the successor owner on file.

3.4 Rider charges and trust administration fees are not included in the
    ROP amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Monthly premium of $164 is due on the 10th day of each month.

4.2 A 31-day grace period applies.

4.3 If twelve or more premiums are paid in advance, annual billing may be
    permitted.

4.4 Two or more missed drafts in a single policy year require future
    payments by manual method until automatic draft is restored.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 Conversion without evidence of insurability is available before the
    earlier of policy year 12 or age 60.

5.2 The Education Support Endorsement does not continue automatically
    upon conversion unless available on the permanent form.

5.3 Full conversion ends future ROP. Partial conversion proportionally
    reduces the death benefit and future ROP.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 The owner may name a trustee or custodial payee to receive proceeds
    for benefit of dependent children.

6.2 If no valid trust or custodial arrangement exists at death, we may pay
    proceeds under a lawful settlement option.

6.3 This endorsement does not guarantee educational expenses.

7.3 There is no exclusion based solely on lawful foreign travel.
