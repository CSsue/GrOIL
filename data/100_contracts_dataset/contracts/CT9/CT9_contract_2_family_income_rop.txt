SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy designed to combine
    family income replacement with premium recovery at the end of term.

1.2 If the insured survives the term and all modal premiums due have been
    paid, we will refund 100% of paid base premiums.

1.3 If the insured dies during the term, we pay:
    • An immediate lump sum death benefit, and
    • A monthly family income benefit for the remaining months of the term

1.4 This policy is intended for personal income replacement and is not
    issued for business continuation purposes.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies while this policy is in force, we will pay
    $200,000 immediately to the beneficiary.

2.2 Beginning on the first monthly anniversary after death, we will pay
    $2,500 per month until the earlier of:
    • The end of the original policy term, or
    • Exhaustion of 240 monthly payments in total from policy issue

2.3 The beneficiary may elect a discounted commuted lump sum instead of
    monthly payments if requested within 60 days after claim approval.

2.4 Proof required includes standard death claim documents and payment
    election forms.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is living on April 10, 2046 and the policy has not
    terminated by death, we will pay the owner all base premiums paid,
    up to $42,720.

3.2 SURRENDER VALUE:
    Policy Years 1-7:   No return
    Policy Years 8-12:  40% of premiums paid
    Policy Years 13-17: 70% of premiums paid
    Policy Years 18-19: 85% of premiums paid
    Policy Year 20:     100% of premiums paid

3.3 If death occurs, no separate ROP is added to the family income claim.

3.4 Any premium discounts granted for automatic draft remain part of paid
    premiums and only the premium actually paid is refundable.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Premiums are due monthly on the 10th day of each month.

4.2 A 31-day grace period applies after each due date.

4.3 If three or more monthly premiums are paid after the due date during
    the policy term, the end-of-term ROP is reduced by 5%.

4.4 If automatic draft fails, the owner remains responsible for timely
    payment whether or not notice is received.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 This policy may be converted to a permanent policy before the end of
    policy year 12 without evidence of insurability.

5.2 Upon conversion, the monthly family income feature ends unless the
    new form expressly includes a comparable option.

5.3 On written request after policy year 10, the owner may reduce the
    monthly family income benefit and proportionally reduce premium.

5.4 Any reduction in premium proportionally reduces the maximum ROP.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Optional riders include Children's Term, Accidental Death, and

6.2 Rider charges are not refundable under the ROP feature.

6.3 Changing premium mode may change total refundable premiums.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 SUICIDE: If death by suicide occurs within 24 months from issue, we
    will refund the base premiums paid and terminate coverage.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: Statements in the application may be contested for
    material misrepresentation for two years after issue.

7.3 The family income feature is not payable for any month after the end
    of the policy term.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 8: REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 A lapsed policy may be reinstated within 4 years after lapse.

8.2 Reinstatement requires evidence of insurability, payment of overdue
    premiums, and 6% annual interest.

8.3 The commuted lump-sum option is unavailable for 12 months after
    reinstatement.
