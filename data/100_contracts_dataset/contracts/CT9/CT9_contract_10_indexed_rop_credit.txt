SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy with an inflation credit
    feature.

1.2 If the insured survives the policy term and required base premiums are
    paid, we refund 100% of paid base premiums.

1.3 This policy may also pay a separate non-guaranteed inflation credit.

1.4 The inflation credit is payable only if declared by us.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies during the policy term while the policy is in
    force, we pay $340,000 to the beneficiary.

2.2 The non-guaranteed inflation credit is not payable on death.

2.3 Claimants must provide documents reasonably required by us.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is alive on April 10, 2046 and this policy is in
    force, we will pay $39,200.

3.2 At the end of term, we may declare an additional amount not exceeding
    15% of the guaranteed ROP to reflect cumulative inflation experience
    under our administrative formula.

3.3 EARLY SURRENDER VALUE:
    Years 1-5:   0%
    Years 6-10:  30%
    Years 11-15: 60%
    Years 16-19: 90%
    Year 20:     100%

3.4 On surrender before end of term, only the vested guaranteed ROP is
    payable. No inflation credit is payable on surrender.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Annual premium of $1,960 is due each April 10.

4.2 Premiums are level during the policy term.

4.3 Grace period is 31 days.

4.4 A premium paid more than 10 days late reduces any non-guaranteed
    inflation credit for that policy year to zero, but does not reduce
    the guaranteed ROP if paid within the grace period.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 Conversion is available before the earlier of policy year 15 or age 65.

5.2 Guaranteed ROP on any converted portion is forfeited unless credited
    by the permanent form then offered.

5.3 No inflation credit survives conversion.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 We determine the reference Consumer Price Index and administrative

6.2 The inflation credit is non-guaranteed and may be zero.

6.3 Administrative method changes will apply on a uniform basis and will

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 SUICIDE: If the insured dies by suicide within 24 months from issue,
    liability is limited to return of premiums paid for the base policy.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest the policy within two years from issue
    for material misrepresentation.

7.3 No inflation credit is payable if the policy terminates by death,
    surrender, or lapse before end of term.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 8: REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8.2 Reinstatement requires evidence of insurability satisfactory to us

8.3 No inflation credit accrues for any lapsed period.
