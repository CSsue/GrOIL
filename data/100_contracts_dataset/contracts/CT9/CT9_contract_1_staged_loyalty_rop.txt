SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium (ROP) Term Life Insurance Policy.

1.2 If the insured survives the policy term and all required premiums
    are paid, we will refund 100% of base premiums paid for this policy.

1.3 This policy includes:
    • Level death benefit for 20 years
    • Staged vesting of return-of-premium value
    • Conversion option to permanent insurance
    • Loyalty bonus equal to 2% of returned premiums if no premium
      has been paid after the due date during the entire term

1.4 Rider charges, policy fees for optional riders, and reinstatement
    charges are not included in the return-of-premium amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies during the term while this policy is in force,
    we will pay $350,000 to the beneficiary.

2.2 If death occurs after at least ten full policy years, we will also
    pay any vested ROP amount shown under Section 3.2 that exceeds
    unpaid premiums then due.

2.3 Beneficiaries must provide:
    • Certified death certificate
    • Completed claim statement
    • Proof of identity
    • Any assignment documentation then on record

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 END OF TERM ROP:
    If the insured is alive on April 10, 2046 and the policy has remained
    continuously in force, we will pay $39,600, plus any applicable
    loyalty bonus under Section 1.3.

3.2 EARLY SURRENDER SCHEDULE:
    Policy Years 1-4:   0% of base premiums returned
    Policy Years 5-8:   25% of base premiums returned
    Policy Years 9-12:  50% of base premiums returned
    Policy Years 13-16: 75% of base premiums returned
    Policy Years 17-19: 90% of base premiums returned
    Policy Year 20:     100% of base premiums returned

3.3 A surrender request is effective on the date received at our home
    office. Coverage terminates at 12:01 a.m. on the next policy day.

3.4 ROP payments are made within 45 days after we receive all documents
    reasonably required to process the payment.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PAYMENT REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Annual premium of $1,980 is due each April 10.

4.2 Premiums are level for the full policy term.

4.3 A premium paid during the 31-day grace period keeps the policy in
    force but disqualifies that policy year from loyalty bonus credit.

4.4 If premium remains unpaid beyond the grace period, the policy lapses.
    Upon lapse, the vested ROP value is forfeited unless the policy is
    reinstated under Section 8.

4.5 Premiums waived under any separate rider do not count as paid for
    purposes of calculating returned premiums unless the rider expressly
    states otherwise.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 This policy may be converted to an eligible permanent life insurance
    policy without evidence of insurability.

5.2 Conversion is available before the earlier of:
    • The end of policy year 15, or
    • The insured's 65th birthday

5.3 Minimum conversion amount: $100,000
    Maximum conversion amount: the face amount then in force

5.4 Upon conversion of the entire policy:
    • Term coverage ends
    • No end-of-term ROP will be payable
    • Vested surrender value may be applied as a premium credit only if
      the permanent form then offered permits such credit

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 SUICIDE: If the insured dies by suicide within 24 months from issue,
    our liability is limited to the premiums paid for the base policy,
    less any indebtedness and less any rider benefits previously paid.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest this policy during the first two years
    for material misrepresentation in the application.

7.3 No war or aviation exclusion applies to the base death benefit.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 8: REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 A lapsed policy may be reinstated within 3 years after lapse if:
    • The insured is living
    • We receive evidence of insurability satisfactory to us
    • All overdue premiums are paid with interest at 5% per year

8.2 After reinstatement, prior vested ROP percentages resume, but no
    loyalty bonus is available for any policy year in which lapse
    occurred or for any later reinstated year.

8.3 A new suicide and contestability period applies only with respect to
    statements made in the reinstatement application.
