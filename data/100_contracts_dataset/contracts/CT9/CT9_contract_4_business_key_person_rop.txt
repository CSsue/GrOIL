SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy issued for business
    continuity and key employee risk management.

1.2 The policy owner is a business entity. All rights under the policy
    belong to the owner, subject to applicable law.

1.3 If the insured survives the full term and all base premiums are paid,
    we will pay 100% of those base premiums to the owner.

1.4 This policy is not intended to fund a qualified employee benefit plan.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If the insured dies while the policy is in force, we will pay
    $500,000 to the owner-beneficiary.

2.2 If the owner has assigned policy rights under a collateral assignment
    for business lending, we will honor that assignment to the extent of
    the assignee's interest.

2.3 Claim requirements include a death certificate, corporate claim
    authorization, proof of beneficiary identity, and assignment records.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is living at the end of the term and this policy is in
    force, we will pay the owner $61,200.

3.2 EARLY TERMINATION VALUES:
    Policy Years 1-6:   0%
    Policy Years 7-10:  35%
    Policy Years 11-15: 65%

3.4 Returned premiums do not bear interest unless required by law.

SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Annual premium is due each April 10.

4.2 Premiums are level and guaranteed not to increase during the term.

4.3 A 31-day grace period applies.

4.4 Any premium paid more than 15 days after the due date reduces the
    final ROP by 1% of the otherwise payable amount for that policy year.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 Conversion to an eligible permanent policy is available before the
    end of policy year 14.

5.2 The owner may convert all or part of the face amount in increments of
    $50,000 without evidence of insurability.

5.3 Any amount converted is excluded from future ROP calculations.

5.4 Conversion does not change ownership unless recorded by us.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7.3 No exclusion applies merely because death occurs while traveling for business.
