SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy with a charitable legacy
    feature.

1.2 If the insured survives the full term and all base premiums are paid,
    we will return 100% of base premiums paid.

1.3 If the insured dies while the policy is in force, we will pay the
    base death benefit to the named beneficiary and the separate
    charitable legacy benefit to the charitable beneficiary.

1.4 The charitable legacy benefit does not reduce the base death benefit.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 Upon death of the insured while this policy is in force, we pay
    $310,000 to the primary beneficiary.

2.2 We also pay $10,000 to the charitable beneficiary named in the policy
    if that organization exists and is legally able to receive funds.

2.3 If the named charitable beneficiary cannot accept payment, the
    charitable legacy benefit is paid to the primary beneficiary unless
    the owner has designated an alternate charity.

2.4 Standard claim proof and beneficiary identification are required.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is alive on April 10, 2046 and this policy is in
    force, we will pay the owner $38,500.

3.2 SURRENDER VALUE:
    Years 1-5:   0%
    Years 6-10:  30%
    Years 11-15: 60%
    Years 16-18: 85%
    Years 19-20: 100%

3.3 The charitable legacy benefit is not refundable and is not considered
    part of the ROP amount.

3.4 Any rider premium or charitable administration charge is excluded from
    the ROP calculation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Annual premium of $1,925 is due each April 10.

4.2 Grace period is 31 days.

4.3 If premium is paid after lapse and the policy is later reinstated,
    the policy year in default earns only 75% ROP credit for that year's
    premium.

4.4 Premiums are level throughout the term.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 This policy may be converted to permanent insurance before the end of
    policy year 15 without evidence of insurability.

5.2 Full conversion terminates the charitable legacy feature unless the
    new policy form includes such feature.

5.3 Full or partial conversion proportionally reduces future ROP.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 The owner may change the charitable beneficiary by written request.

6.2 The charitable beneficiary has no ownership rights while the insured
    is living.

6.3 Any change of charitable beneficiary becomes effective when recorded
    by us, subject to any payment made before recording.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 SUICIDE: If the insured dies by suicide within 24 months from issue,
    liability is limited to return of paid base premiums. No base death
    benefit or charitable legacy benefit is payable.

7.2 CONTESTABILITY: We may contest this policy for material
    misrepresentation within two years after issue.

7.3 No exclusion applies because a beneficiary includes a charity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 8: REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8.1 Reinstatement is available within 5 years after lapse.

8.2 Reinstatement requires evidence of insurability, payment of overdue
    premiums, and 5% annual interest.

8.3 The last recorded charitable beneficiary remains in effect after
    reinstatement unless later changed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

9.2 All changes must be made in writing and recorded by us.
