SECTION 1: POLICY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 This is a Return of Premium Term Life policy with a scheduled increase
    in death benefit beginning at policy year 11.

1.2 The design aligns coverage with expected increases in family or
    financial obligations later in the term.

1.3 If the insured survives the full term and required base premiums are
    paid, we will refund all base premiums paid.

1.4 No new underwriting is required for the scheduled increase.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 2: DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 If death occurs in policy years 1 through 10, we pay $250,000.

2.2 If death occurs in policy years 11 through 20, we pay $400,000.

2.3 Optional rider benefits, if any, are payable in addition to the base
    death benefit.

2.4 Beneficiary claims require standard proof of death and identity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: RETURN OF PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 If the insured is alive at the end of the policy term and the policy
    remains in force, we will pay $40,800 to the owner.

3.2 If the policy is surrendered before the end of term, the owner is
    entitled to the following percentage of paid base premiums:
    Years 1-5:   0%
    Years 6-10:  30%
    Years 11-14: 55%
    Years 15-18: 80%
    Years 19-20: 100%

3.3 The step-up in death benefit does not change the ROP percentage.

3.4 Rider premiums are excluded from the ROP amount.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: PREMIUM PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 Annual premium of $2,040 is due each April 10.

4.2 Premiums remain level for the full 20-year term.

4.3 Grace period is 31 days.

4.4 If a premium is paid after lapse and coverage is later reinstated,
    that policy year earns only 50% ROP credit for that year's premium.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 Conversion to permanent insurance is available before the earlier of
    policy year 15 or age 65.

5.2 Conversion may apply to the face amount then in force.

5.3 Full conversion forfeits future ROP.

5.4 Partial conversion proportionally reduces future ROP.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 Available riders at issue include Waiver of Premium, Accidental

6.2 Accelerated death benefits reduce the death benefit and may reduce any
    future surrender ROP proportionally.

6.3 This policy has no loan feature.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7.3 No exclusion applies for lawful travel, occupation, or sports unless

8.3 Reinstatement does not change the scheduled step-up date.
