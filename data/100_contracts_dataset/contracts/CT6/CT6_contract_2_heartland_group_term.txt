SECTION 1 – ABOUT THIS CERTIFICATE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This certificate is issued under Master Group Policy
    HML-2024-MM-GROUP between Heartland Mutual Life Insurance
    Company ("Heartland") and Midwest Manufacturing Partners.

1.2 The Master Policy is on file at the employer's principal
    office and is available for inspection by any covered
    employee upon request.

1.3 Capitalized terms in this certificate have the meanings
    assigned in Section 12 (Definitions).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 EMPLOYEES: All active, full-time hourly and salaried
    employees regularly working 35 or more hours per week
    are eligible. Coverage begins on the first day of the
    calendar month following 90 days of continuous service.

2.2 DEPENDENTS – SPOUSE: Your legal spouse or domestic partner
    registered under applicable state law is eligible for
    dependent life coverage from the same date as the employee.
    A spouse who is also an employee covered under this plan is
    not eligible for dependent spouse coverage.

2.3 DEPENDENTS – CHILDREN: Your unmarried dependent children
    from live birth (or from 14 days of age, see Section 3.4)
    to age 26 are eligible, including legally adopted children
    and stepchildren living in your household.

2.4 EVIDENCE OF INSURABILITY (EOI): Basic employee life and
    initial dependent coverage elected within 31 days of
    eligibility require no EOI. The following require EOI:
      (a) Voluntary employee supplemental coverage exceeding
          $50,000
      (b) Spouse coverage exceeding $10,000 if enrolled after
          the initial 31-day period
      (c) Any coverage elected by a Late Entrant

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT AMOUNTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 EMPLOYEE BASIC LIFE: $50,000 flat for all eligible
    employees, regardless of salary, position, or tenure.

3.2 EMPLOYEE VOLUNTARY SUPPLEMENTAL: Available in $25,000
    increments up to $300,000. Amounts above $100,000 require
    EOI. Maximum total employee coverage: $350,000.

3.3 SPOUSE DEPENDENT LIFE: $25,000 flat. Cannot exceed 100%
    of the employee's total life coverage in force.

3.4 CHILD DEPENDENT LIFE:
      • Age 14 days through 6 months:   $1,000
      • Age 6 months through age 26:    $10,000
    The child benefit is a per-child amount covering all
    eligible children for a single monthly premium.

3.5 REDUCTION SCHEDULE: Employee coverage reduces as follows:
      Age 65:  Reduces to 65% of in-force benefit
      Age 70:  Reduces to 50% of in-force benefit
    Dependent coverage is not subject to age reduction.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – WHEN COVERAGE ENDS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Employee coverage ends on the earliest of:
    (a) Termination of the Master Policy
    (b) The last day of the month employment terminates
    (c) The date the employee is no longer in an eligible class
    (d) Failure to pay required premiums for voluntary coverage

4.2 Dependent coverage ends on the earliest of:
    (a) The date employee coverage ends
    (b) The date the dependent no longer meets the definition
        of eligible dependent (including divorce or annulment
        for spouse coverage)
    (c) The date dependent coverage is cancelled

4.3 Child coverage terminates at the end of the policy month
    in which the child attains age 26, marries, or otherwise
    ceases to qualify, whichever is earliest.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – CONVERSION AND PORTABILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 EMPLOYEE CONVERSION: Upon termination of all or part of
    group life coverage (other than nonpayment of premium),
    you may convert to an individual whole-life or universal-
    life policy without EOI within 31 days. Maximum convertible
    amount: $250,000.

5.2 DEPENDENT CONVERSION: Upon termination of dependent life
    coverage, the covered dependent may convert to an
    individual policy within 31 days without EOI. The
    application must be signed by the employee (for spouse) or
    the dependent (if age 18 or older).

5.3 PORTABILITY: Not available under this plan. Conversion is
    the sole continuation option upon termination.

5.4 CONTINUATION DURING LEAVE: Coverage may continue during
    approved FMLA leave for up to 12 weeks, provided the
    employee pays the employee-share premiums directly.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – ACCIDENTAL DEATH BENEFIT (OPTIONAL RIDER)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 This plan does not include an AD&D rider by default.
    Employees may elect a separate AD&D rider through
    open enrollment. See the AD&D Rider Addendum if elected.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 CHRONIC ILLNESS RIDER: If certified by two independent
    licensed physicians as chronically ill (unable to perform
    2 of 6 Activities of Daily Living for 90+ days), you may
    accelerate up to 25% of the employee life benefit per
    calendar year, subject to a $10,000 annual minimum.

7.2 TERMINAL ILLNESS: If certified terminally ill with life
    expectancy of 12 months or less, up to 80% of the
    employee benefit may be accelerated in a lump sum, not
    to exceed $200,000. An actuarial discount applies.

7.3 Dependent life benefits are not subject to acceleration.

7.4 Accelerated benefits reduce the death benefit paid to
    beneficiaries and may have tax consequences.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 SUICIDE: No benefit for death by suicide within 12 months
    of the employee's effective date of basic or supplemental
    coverage, or within 12 months of any increase in voluntary
    coverage (limited to the amount of the increase only).

8.2 DEPENDENT SUICIDE: No benefit for dependent death by
    suicide within 12 months of the dependent's effective date.

8.3 WAR EXCLUSION: No benefit is payable for death resulting
    from declared or undeclared war, insurrection, or service
    in the armed forces of any nation, unless the employee is
    in the National Guard or Reserve activated for domestic
    disaster relief.

8.4 PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS: No pre-existing condition
    limitation applies to employee or dependent coverage.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 Employee basic and voluntary life coverage premiums are
    waived upon proof of total disability beginning before
    age 60, following a 6-month elimination period.

9.2 "Total disability" is defined as complete inability to
    engage in the employee's own occupation for the first
    24 months, and any gainful occupation thereafter.

9.3 Dependent coverage premiums are also waived during a
    period of approved employee disability waiver.

9.4 Waiver terminates at age 65 or earlier recovery.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10 – BENEFICIARY AND CLAIMS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10.1 Beneficiaries for employee life are designated by the
     employee. Beneficiary for dependent life is the employee,
     unless the employee predeceases the dependent.

10.2 Claims must be reported within 60 days of death.

10.3 Required: certified death certificate, completed claim
     form, proof of beneficiary identity.

10.4 Submit to:
     Heartland Mutual Life – Claims Division
     500 Meridian Street, Indianapolis, IN 46204
     Claims Line: 1-877-555-4600

10.5 Heartland will pay valid claims within 10 business days
     of complete documentation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 12 – DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Active Employment" means performing regular duties at the
  employer's place of business or an approved remote location.
"Dependent" means an eligible spouse or child as defined in
  Section 2.
"Late Entrant" means an employee or dependent who failed to
  enroll within the initial eligibility period.
"Total Disability" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.2.
