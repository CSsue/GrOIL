───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – NATURE OF THIS PLAN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This plan is maintained pursuant to a collective bargaining
    agreement ("CBA") between Ironworkers Local 77 and the
    participating employers. Coverage is funded entirely by
    employer contributions to the Ironworkers Local 77 Joint
    Welfare Trust ("Trust") as required by the CBA.

1.2 The Trust, acting through its Board of Trustees, holds
    the master policy from Continental Shield Life Company.
    This certificate is issued by Continental Shield and
    represents the Trustees' agreement to provide coverage.

1.3 This plan is an employee welfare benefit plan subject to
    ERISA. Certain ERISA rights are described in Section 11.

1.4 Benefits are subject to amendment or termination by the
    Trustees in accordance with Trust documents and ERISA.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY BASED ON CONTRIBUTION HOURS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 Eligibility and benefit level are based on employer
    contribution hours credited to the participant's account
    in the prior plan year (May 1 – April 30).

2.2 Hours are credited when a participating employer remits
    contributions to the Trust on the participant's behalf
    at the rate prescribed in the CBA.

2.3 Minimum hours required for any coverage: 500 hours in
    the prior plan year.

2.4 Coverage for the current plan year becomes effective on
    May 1 following the qualification year.

2.5 NEW PARTICIPANTS: Members who have not yet completed
    a full plan year qualify for Tier 1 coverage after
    accumulating 500 hours from first contribution date
    (see Section 3).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT SCHEDULE (HOURS-BASED TIERS)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 Life and AD&D benefit is determined by contribution hours
    in the prior plan year:

    Contribution Hours      Life Benefit    AD&D Benefit
    ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    500 – 999               $30,000         $30,000
    1,000 – 1,799           $60,000         $60,000
    1,800 or more           $90,000         $90,000

3.2 AD&D DISMEMBERMENT SCHEDULE: For losses resulting from
    accidental injury, the following percentage of the AD&D
    benefit is payable:
      Both hands, feet, or sight of both eyes:   100%
      One hand and one foot:                     100%
      One hand, foot, or sight of one eye:        50%
      Loss of speech and hearing (both ears):    100%
      Loss of hearing (both ears):                50%
      Loss of thumb and forefinger (same hand):   25%

3.3 SURVIVING SPOUSE WEEKLY INCOME RIDER:
    Upon the participant's death (any cause), the surviving
    legal spouse receives $400 per week for 52 weeks,
    commencing 30 days after death benefit payment.
    This rider is available only when the participant has
    completed a qualifying year at Tier 2 or Tier 3.

3.4 MAXIMUM BENEFIT: $90,000 life + $90,000 AD&D + $20,800
    surviving spouse income benefit (52 × $400).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – HOURS BANKING AND CONTINUATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 HOURS BANK: Participants may accrue a reserve of
    contribution hours up to a maximum of 2,400 banked hours.

4.2 During periods of unemployment or reduced work (layoff,
    strike, approved leave), banked hours may be drawn down
    at the rate of the participant's most recent tier
    threshold per month to maintain coverage.

4.3 Coverage terminates when banked hours are exhausted and
    no new contributions are received.

4.4 Self-pay continuation: When hours are exhausted, a
    participant may self-pay monthly contributions at the
    employer contribution rate for up to 12 months to
    maintain Tier 1 coverage only.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – CONVERSION UPON PERMANENT WITHDRAWAL
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 A participant who permanently leaves the trade or the
    bargaining unit may convert to an individual whole-life
    policy within 31 days of loss of eligibility.

5.2 Maximum convertible amount: lesser of the participant's
    life benefit in force or $150,000.

5.3 No EOI required. Conversion is not available for AD&D
    or the surviving spouse weekly income rider.

5.4 The Trust has no portability program. Conversion is the
    sole continuation option.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 A participant diagnosed as terminally ill (life expectancy
    12 months or less, certified by two physicians) may
    request acceleration of up to 50% of the life benefit.
    Minimum: $5,000. Maximum: $45,000.

6.2 The AD&D benefit and surviving spouse rider are not
    subject to acceleration.

6.3 Actuarial discount applies. Accelerated amount is not
    repayable and reduces the death benefit.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 LIFE BENEFIT EXCLUSIONS:
    (a) Suicide within 12 months of the effective date of
        any coverage or tier increase

7.2 AD&D EXCLUSIONS: The AD&D benefit is not payable for
    loss resulting from:
    (a) Suicide or intentional self-inflicted injury
    (b) Sickness, disease, or infection (except from an
        accidental wound)
    (c) War, declared or undeclared
    (d) Participating in a riot, insurrection, or felony
    (e) Voluntary ingestion of drugs or alcohol to the
        extent of legal intoxication

7.3 SURVIVING SPOUSE RIDER EXCLUSIONS: Payments cease if the
    surviving spouse remarries within the 52-week benefit
    period. Weekly benefits are not payable retroactively
    after remarriage.

7.4 No pre-existing condition limitation on life coverage.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM / DISABILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 A participant disabled due to an occupational injury or
    illness covered under workers' compensation may have
    contribution hours credited at the participant's most
    recent average weekly hours for up to 52 weeks during
    the period of disability.

8.2 Disability hours credit requires: (a) written physician
    certification, and (b) documentation of an active workers'
    compensation claim.

8.3 Non-occupational disability does not trigger hours credit.
    The participant must use banked hours or self-pay
    (see Section 4).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – BENEFICIARY DESIGNATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 Participants designate beneficiaries through the Trust
    office or online at iwlocal77welfare.org.

9.2 Surviving spouse weekly income rider payments are made
    to the legal surviving spouse; this rider cannot be
    redirected to a non-spouse beneficiary.

9.3 If no beneficiary survives the participant, life benefit
    is paid per Trust default: spouse > children equally >
    parents equally > estate.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
