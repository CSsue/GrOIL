───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – GLOBAL COVERAGE STRUCTURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This certificate covers employees of Global Constructors
    International who are working on international assignments
    ("Expatriate Employees") or regularly travel internationally
    for business ("Global Roaming Employees").

1.2 Coverage is designed to follow the employee worldwide.
    There is no restriction on geographic location for coverage
    except as specifically stated in Section 7 (Exclusions)
    and modified by the Buyback Riders in Section 6.

1.3 Premiums and benefits are denominated in U.S. dollars.
    Claims are payable in USD; payment in foreign currency
    may be arranged by request.

1.4 The Master Policy is governed by the laws of Texas.
    International disputes may be resolved by ICC arbitration
    at the employer's election.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY AND CLASSES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 EXPATRIATE EMPLOYEES: U.S. nationals or legal permanent
    residents assigned outside the United States on assignments
    expected to last 6 months or more. This certificate's class.

2.2 GLOBAL ROAMING EMPLOYEES: Employees based in the U.S. who
    spend more than 90 days per year outside the U.S. on
    business travel. Eligible for basic coverage only under
    separate certificate class.

2.3 LOCAL NATIONAL EMPLOYEES: Citizens of host countries
    employed locally are not eligible under this policy.

2.4 Coverage commences on the first day of an approved
    international assignment, provided the employee is
    actively at work and all EOI requirements are satisfied.

2.5 EVIDENCE OF INSURABILITY: Basic coverage up to $500,000
    does not require EOI. Coverage above $500,000 and any
    voluntary supplemental election require EOI.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT AMOUNTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 BASIC LIFE: 3x annual base salary (USD), rounded to the
    next higher $1,000. Includes base salary only; housing
    allowances, COLA, and hardship premiums are excluded.

3.2 VOLUNTARY SUPPLEMENTAL: Available in increments of
    $50,000, up to a maximum of $500,000 or 4x salary,
    whichever is less. EOI required above $200,000.

3.3 BENEFIT RECALCULATION: Annual recalculation on February 1
    based on January 1 salary.

3.4 MAXIMUM: $2,000,000 total (basic + supplemental,
    inclusive of rider benefits).

3.5 HARDSHIP LOCATION BENEFIT: For assignments in designated
    Hardship Level 3 or 4 locations (as listed on the
    current Hardship Schedule maintained by HR), basic life
    coverage is automatically increased by 1x salary while
    on assignment in that location.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – REPATRIATION BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 In the event of the employee's death while on assignment
    outside the U.S., Transoceanic will pay a Repatriation
    Benefit of up to $25,000 in addition to the death benefit
    to cover the cost of:
      • Preparation and transportation of remains to the U.S.
        (or the employee's designated home country)
      • Airfare for one immediate family member to accompany
        remains
      • Local administrative and documentation costs

4.2 The Repatriation Benefit is paid directly to the employer
    or funeral home upon submission of receipts, or as a
    flat $15,000 lump sum if receipts are not provided.

4.3 Repatriation Benefit is not subject to beneficiary
    designation and is not part of the death benefit.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – CONTINUATION UPON REPATRIATION OR ASSIGNMENT END
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 When an expatriate employee is repatriated (returns to
    U.S.-based employment), this certificate terminates and
    coverage transitions to the employer's U.S. domestic
    group life plan, if any, effective the first day of
    U.S. employment. No coverage gap applies.

5.2 If the employee leaves the company, portability is
    available: the employee may continue basic and supplemental
    life coverage as direct-pay for up to 24 months.
    Maximum portable: $500,000. No EOI required.

5.3 After the 24-month portability period, the employee may
    convert to an individual policy without EOI.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – OPTIONAL BUYBACK RIDERS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 WAR AND TERRORISM BUYBACK RIDER (Elected):
    By electing this rider, the employee removes the War and
    Terrorism Exclusion (see Section 7.2) and the death benefit
    is payable for death resulting from:
      (a) Declared or undeclared war
      (b) Acts of terrorism as defined by the U.S. State Dept.
      (c) Civil war, insurrection, or political violence
      (d) Military action by any government
    The war buyback does NOT cover death while serving as
    a combatant in any military force.

6.2 AVIATION EXCLUSION BUYBACK RIDER (Elected):
    By electing this rider, the employee removes the Aviation
    Exclusion for non-scheduled private aircraft (Section 7.3)
    and the death benefit is payable for death resulting from
    operation of or travel in private or charter aircraft
    anywhere in the world.
    This rider does NOT cover death as a pilot or crew member
    of a military aircraft.

6.3 Riders are renewable annually at open enrollment.
    Removal of a rider reinstates the applicable exclusion
    prospectively.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 SUICIDE: No benefit for death by suicide within 24 months
    of the effective date of any coverage or reinstatement.

7.2 WAR AND TERRORISM (ABSENT BUYBACK RIDER): If the
    War/Terror Buyback Rider is NOT in force, no death
    benefit is payable for death resulting from war, acts
    of terrorism, or civil insurrection.

7.3 AVIATION (ABSENT BUYBACK RIDER): If the Aviation Buyback
    Rider is NOT in force, no benefit is payable for death
    occurring while operating or traveling as a passenger in
    a non-scheduled private aircraft (excluding commercial
    scheduled airline service).

7.4 INCONTESTABILITY: After 24 months of continuous coverage,
    Transoceanic may not deny a claim based on application
    misrepresentation, except fraud.

7.5 No pre-existing condition exclusion.

7.6 HAZARDOUS ACTIVITIES: No exclusion for hazardous
    occupational activities, including work at height,
    offshore platforms, confined spaces, or in seismic zones.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 The employee may accelerate up to 75% of total life
    coverage upon certification of terminal illness (life
    expectancy 12 months or less).

8.2 Maximum: $500,000. Minimum: $10,000.

8.3 For employees on assignment, acceleration request must
    include physician certification from a licensed physician
    in the employee's current country of assignment or the U.S.

8.4 Actuarial discount based on current U.S. 6-month T-bill
    rate plus 150 basis points.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 If the employee becomes totally disabled (per the
    employer's long-term disability plan definition) while on
    international assignment, premiums are waived after a
    6-month elimination period.

9.2 For overseas disabilities, the employer's occupational
    physician may fulfill the certification role in lieu of
    an independent U.S. physician.

9.3 Waiver continues through age 65 or recovery.
