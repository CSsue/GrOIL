───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – ABOUT THIS CERTIFICATE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This certificate is issued under a group master policy
    obtained by Grace Ministries United from Providence
    Covenant Life Insurance Society ("Providence").

1.2 This plan is designed for small nonprofits and religious
    organizations. The maximum aggregate group benefit
    payable under the master policy is $5,000,000.

1.3 Providence is a mutual insurance society. As a certificate
    holder, you have no ownership rights in Providence.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 All ordained ministers, full-time staff, and employees of
    Grace Ministries United regularly working 25 or more hours
    per week are eligible.

2.2 Coverage begins on the first day of the month following
    completion of 30 days of employment.

2.3 Ordained clergy who are designated as self-employed for
    Social Security purposes ("Dual Status Ministers") are
    eligible as employees for purposes of this plan.

2.4 Volunteers and independent contractors are not eligible.

2.5 Voluntary supplemental coverage may be elected during the
    initial 30-day window without EOI. Late enrollment requires
    EOI at any benefit amount.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT DESIGN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 BASIC LIFE: $40,000 flat benefit for all eligible
    employees, regardless of salary, position, or tenure.
    The flat benefit design reflects Grace Ministries United's
    commitment to equal provision for all staff.

3.2 VOLUNTARY SUPPLEMENTAL: Available in $10,000 increments
    only. Maximum supplemental benefit: $60,000. Maximum total
    coverage: $100,000 per employee.

3.3 BENEFIT MAXIMUM (PLAN-WIDE): No single employee may have
    total coverage exceeding $100,000 under this master policy.

3.4 CLERGY HOUSING ALLOWANCE: The death benefit is not
    calculated based on compensation and is not affected by
    any portion of compensation designated as clergy housing
    allowance.

3.5 AGE REDUCTION:
      Age 65: reduces to 75% of benefit in force
      Age 70: reduces to 50% of benefit in force
      Age 75: coverage terminates

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – WHEN COVERAGE ENDS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Coverage ends on the earliest of:
    (a) Termination of the master policy
    (b) Last day of employment with Grace Ministries United
    (c) Date the employee is no longer in an eligible class
    (d) Date premium payment is discontinued (voluntary only)
    (e) Date the employee attains age 75

4.2 LEAVE OF ABSENCE: Coverage may be continued for up to
    90 days during approved medical leave. The employer must
    continue premium payments during this period.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – CONVERSION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 Upon termination of employment, any covered employee may
    convert to an individual whole-life policy within 31 days
    without EOI.

5.2 Maximum convertible amount: the lesser of the coverage in
    force at termination or $50,000.

5.3 If the employee dies within the 31-day conversion window
    before converting, the full group benefit is payable.

5.4 PORTABILITY: This plan does not offer portability. The
    conversion right in Section 5.1 is the sole continuation
    option.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 If diagnosed terminally ill with life expectancy of 6
    months or less, the employee may request a one-time
    acceleration of up to 50% of total coverage.

6.2 Minimum acceleration: $5,000. Maximum: $30,000.

6.3 An actuarial discount applies at the rate of 6% per annum
    compounded monthly, applied against the anticipated
    remaining life expectancy.

6.4 Any amount accelerated reduces the death benefit.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 SUICIDE: No benefit is payable if death occurs by suicide,
    sane or insane, within 12 months of the effective date
    of coverage or the effective date of any supplemental
    coverage increase.

7.2 MISREPRESENTATION: Providence may rescind coverage and
    deny claims within 2 years of the effective date of
    coverage if the employee made material misrepresentations
    in any application. After 2 years, the policy is
    incontestable except for fraud.

7.3 No exclusion for pre-existing conditions, hazardous
    occupation, or foreign travel.

7.4 MISSION TRAVEL: Death or injury occurring during employer-
    sanctioned mission travel, including travel to high-risk
    regions, is covered under this policy without exclusion
    or premium surcharge. Documentation of employer
    authorization may be required.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 This plan does not include a waiver of premium benefit.
    Employees who become disabled must arrange for continued
    premium payment or exercise the conversion right under
    Section 5 to maintain life coverage.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – BENEFICIARY DESIGNATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 Designations are submitted through the HR office of
    Grace Ministries United and forwarded to Providence.

9.2 If a minor is named as beneficiary, proceeds will be
    held by Providence in trust until the minor reaches age
    18, unless a guardian has been appointed by a court of
    competent jurisdiction.

9.3 If no surviving beneficiary is designated, proceeds are
    paid per Tennessee intestacy law order: spouse, children,
    parents, siblings, estate.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10 – CLAIMS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10.1 Claims must be filed within 90 days of death. Failure to
     file within 90 days does not void a valid claim if
     filing was not reasonably possible.

10.2 Required documents:
     • Certified death certificate
     • Completed Providence claim form
     • Proof of beneficiary identity
     • For minors: court guardianship order if applicable

10.3 Submit to:
     Providence Covenant Life Insurance Society
     Nonprofit Benefits Division
     1800 Church Street, Nashville, TN 37203
     Phone: 1-800-555-2277

10.4 Claims are paid within 14 business days of receiving
     complete documentation.
