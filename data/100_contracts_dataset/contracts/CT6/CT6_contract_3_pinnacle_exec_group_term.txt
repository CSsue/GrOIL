───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – PURPOSE AND STRUCTURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This certificate evidences participation in FinServ Capital
    Group's Executive Group Term Life Plan, a non-qualified
    carve-out plan covering Class A executives.

1.2 Coverage is maintained under a group term master policy and
    is supplemental to any basic group coverage provided to
    all employees under Plan No. PLAC-2024-FS-BASIC.

1.3 A split-dollar endorsement arrangement governs the division
    of the death benefit between the employer and the
    insured's designated beneficiary. The endorsement is
    attached as Exhibit A and is incorporated herein.

1.4 This plan is not subject to ERISA as a "top-hat" plan
    maintained for a select group of management or highly
    compensated employees.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY AND CLASS DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 Participation is limited to employees designated by the
    employer as Class A Executives (Vice President level
    and above) at the time of policy issuance or upon
    subsequent written designation by the employer.

2.2 The employer retains sole discretion to add or remove
    employees from the eligible class. Addition to the class
    requires EOI only if the employee is classified above
    the Guaranteed Issue Limit (see Section 3.3).

2.3 Eligibility commences on the date of written designation
    by the employer and Pinnacle's written acceptance.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT CALCULATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 BENEFIT FORMULA: Total death benefit equals 5 times total
    annual compensation, defined as base salary plus target
    annual cash bonus as of January 1 of the coverage year.

3.2 BENEFIT REVIEW: Benefit is recalculated annually on March 1
    using compensation as of the preceding January 1. The
    employer certifies compensation figures to Pinnacle
    by February 1 each year.

3.3 GUARANTEED ISSUE LIMIT: Coverage up to $1,500,000 per
    executive is available without EOI at initial enrollment
    and at annual recalculation. Coverage above $1,500,000
    requires full EOI including medical examination.

3.4 MAXIMUM BENEFIT: $5,000,000 per covered executive.

3.5 SPLIT-DOLLAR ENDORSEMENT SPLIT: The employer's collateral
    interest equals the greater of: (a) cumulative premiums
    paid, or (b) 60% of the total death benefit. The remaining
    amount (minimum 40%) is the employee's personal benefit.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – COLLATERAL ASSIGNMENT AND EMPLOYER RIGHTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 The insured has collaterally assigned to FinServ Capital
    Group that portion of the death benefit equal to the
    employer's interest as defined in Section 3.5.

4.2 In the event of the insured's death, Pinnacle will pay
    the employer's portion directly to FinServ Capital Group
    and the employee's personal benefit to the designated
    personal beneficiary.

4.3 The employer may not increase the collateral assignment
    percentage during the insured's lifetime without the
    insured's written consent.

4.4 The employee may not assign, pledge, or encumber the
    personal benefit portion without Pinnacle's written consent.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – GOLDEN HANDCUFF VESTING PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 The employee's personal benefit vests as follows based
    on years of Class A service:

    Years of Class A Service     Personal Benefit Vesting
    ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Less than 2 years            20%
    2 but less than 4 years      40%
    4 but less than 6 years      60%
    6 but less than 8 years      80%
    8 or more years              100%

5.2 Upon termination of employment before full vesting,
    the non-vested portion of the personal benefit reverts
    to the employer's collateral interest.

5.3 Vesting is immediately accelerated to 100% upon:
    (a) Death of the employee while covered
    (b) Total disability (as defined in Section 9)
    (c) Change in control of FinServ Capital Group
    (d) Termination by employer without cause

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – PORTABILITY UPON DEPARTURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 Upon voluntary resignation, the employee may assume the
    policy directly (assume-and-assign) by paying Pinnacle
    directly for premiums. The split-dollar arrangement
    terminates; the employer's collateral interest is released
    upon repayment of cumulative employer premium advances.

6.2 If the employer premium advance is not repaid, the employer
    retains its collateral interest and the policy converts to
    a personally owned individual term policy subject to
    Pinnacle's then-current underwriting.

6.3 Mandatory conversion to an individual policy is required
    within 60 days of termination. Pinnacle will not continue
    group coverage beyond 60 days post-termination.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 Upon certification of terminal illness with life expectancy
    of 12 months or less, the insured may accelerate up to
    100% of the personal benefit portion only.

7.2 The employer's collateral interest is not subject to
    acceleration.

7.3 Maximum acceleration: $500,000 or full personal benefit,
    whichever is less. Actuarial discount applies.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 SUICIDE: No personal benefit or employer collateral
    benefit is payable if death results from suicide within
    24 months of the effective date of coverage.

8.2 MISREPRESENTATION: If the insured made a material
    misrepresentation in any application or EOI, Pinnacle
    may contest the claim within 2 years of the effective
    date of coverage. After 2 years, the policy is
    incontestable except for fraudulent misrepresentation.

8.3 No exclusion applies for pre-existing conditions,
    occupation hazards, or aviation (other than licensed
    commercial scheduled service).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – TOTAL DISABILITY DEFINITION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 "Total disability" means continuous inability to perform
    the material duties of the insured's own occupation as
    an executive for a period exceeding 180 days, as
    certified by a board-certified physician.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10 – CLAIMS PROCEDURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10.1 Upon death of the insured, both the employer and the
     personal beneficiary must be notified within 30 days.

10.2 Pinnacle will issue separate claim payments to the employer
     (collateral interest) and the personal beneficiary
     (personal benefit) within 10 business days of complete
     documentation.

10.3 Required documents:
     • Certified death certificate
     • Completed employer claim certification
     • Personal beneficiary claim form and identity proof
     • For split-dollar release: employer written release

10.4 Submit to:
     Pinnacle Life Assurance – Executive Claims Unit
     One Financial Square, New York, NY 10005
     Direct Line: 1-800-555-9100
