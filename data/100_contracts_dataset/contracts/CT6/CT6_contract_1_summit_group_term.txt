SECTION 1 – ABOUT THIS CERTIFICATE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This certificate summarizes your coverage under the Group
    Master Policy. All rights and obligations are governed by
    the Master Policy.

1.2 In the event of conflict between this certificate and the
    Master Policy, the Master Policy governs.

1.3 This certificate is not a contract of insurance. It is
    evidence of your participation in the group plan.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY AND EFFECTIVE DATE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 All active full-time employees scheduled to work 30 or more
    hours per week are eligible after completing a 60-day
    waiting period from date of hire.

2.2 Part-time employees (20–29 hours/week) are eligible for
    basic coverage only at one-half the standard benefit
    applicable to their salary band.

2.3 Voluntary supplemental coverage requires enrollment within
    45 days of the date of eligibility without evidence of
    insurability. Enrollment after 45 days requires satisfactory
    Evidence of Insurability (EOI) approved by the insurer.

2.4 Coverage becomes effective on the first day of the calendar
    month coinciding with or next following the end of the
    waiting period, provided the employee is actively at work
    on that date.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT AMOUNT: SALARY-BAND SCHEDULE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 Basic life insurance is determined by the following salary
    bands based on annual base earnings:

    Annual Salary              Basic Benefit
    ─────────────────────────────────────────
    Up to $30,000              $30,000
    $30,001 – $40,000          $50,000
    $40,001 – $70,000          $75,000
    $70,001 – $100,000         $100,000
    Over $100,000              $150,000

3.2 Band reclassification occurs on January 1 of each year
    based on prior-year W-2 earnings. Mid-year salary changes
    do not trigger band reclassification.

3.3 VOLUNTARY SUPPLEMENTAL: Available in increments of $10,000
    up to a maximum of $200,000 or 3x annual salary, whichever
    is less. EOI required above $100,000.

3.4 ACCIDENTAL DEATH & DISMEMBERMENT (AD&D): The AD&D benefit
    equals the basic life benefit amount for accidental death.
    Dismemberment schedule:
      Loss of both hands or both feet:      100% of AD&D benefit
      Loss of one hand and one foot:        100% of AD&D benefit
      Loss of sight of both eyes:           100% of AD&D benefit
      Loss of one hand or one foot:          50% of AD&D benefit
      Loss of sight of one eye:              50% of AD&D benefit
      Loss of thumb and index finger
        (same hand):                         25% of AD&D benefit

3.5 AD&D benefits are payable in addition to the life benefit
    for accidental death. Total AD&D payments will not exceed
    100% of the AD&D benefit per occurrence.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – WHEN COVERAGE ENDS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Coverage terminates on the earliest of:
    (a) Termination of the Master Policy
    (b) The last day of the month in which employment ends
    (c) The date the employee is no longer in an eligible class
    (d) The date voluntary coverage is voluntarily cancelled
    (e) The last day premium is paid for voluntary coverage

4.2 A 31-day grace period applies to involuntary termination
    due to layoff, during which basic coverage remains in force
    without premium payment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – PORTABILITY AND CONVERSION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 PORTABILITY: Upon termination of employment, you may port
    your basic and supplemental life coverage (combined) as a
    direct-billed policy, subject to:
      • Application submitted within 31 days of termination
      • Maximum portable amount: $300,000
      • Premiums based on attained-age group rates
      • No evidence of insurability required

5.2 CONVERSION: If portability is not elected, you may convert
    to an individual whole-life policy within 31 days of
    termination without evidence of insurability. Conversion
    is not available for the AD&D rider.

5.3 AD&D coverage cannot be ported or converted and terminates
    with group employment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT (TERMINAL ILLNESS)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 An employee certified by a licensed physician as having
    a terminal illness with a life expectancy of 24 months or
    less may request acceleration of death benefit.

6.2 Maximum acceleration: 50% of the combined life benefit
    (basic + voluntary), not to exceed $150,000.

6.3 Acceleration is subject to an actuarial present-value
    discount at the then-current treasury rate plus 100 bps.

6.4 Acceleration reduces the death benefit payable to
    beneficiaries dollar-for-dollar.

6.5 The AD&D benefit is not subject to acceleration.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 SUICIDE EXCLUSION: No death benefit is payable if death
    results from suicide, whether sane or insane, within
    24 months of the effective date of coverage or the date
    of last increase in voluntary coverage.

7.2 AD&D EXCLUSIONS: The AD&D benefit is not payable if loss
    results from:
    (a) Suicide or intentional self-inflicted injury
    (b) War or act of war (declared or undeclared)
    (c) Active military service
    (d) Participation in a felony
    (e) Operating a vehicle while legally intoxicated
    (f) Illness, disease, bacterial infection, or medical
        treatment thereof (except infections from an
        accidental cut or wound)
    (g) Voluntary use of any narcotic, drug, or hallucinogen
        not prescribed by a licensed physician

7.3 PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS: No pre-existing condition
    limitation applies to basic or supplemental life coverage.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 Upon proof of total disability commencing before age 65,
    premiums for basic and voluntary coverage are waived after
    a 9-month elimination period.

8.2 "Total disability" means complete inability to perform any
    occupation for which the employee is reasonably qualified
    by education, training, or experience.

8.3 Annual proof of continuing disability is required. Summit
    reserves the right to require examination by a physician
    of its choosing at its expense.

8.4 Waiver terminates upon the earliest of: recovery, return
    to work in any capacity, or attainment of age 70.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – BENEFICIARY DESIGNATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 You may designate or change beneficiaries at any time
    through your employer's HR portal. Changes take effect upon
    receipt by the plan administrator.

9.2 If the primary beneficiary predeceases the insured,
    benefits pass to the contingent beneficiary. If no
    beneficiary survives, benefits are paid to the estate.

9.3 For irrevocable beneficiary designations, the consent of
    the irrevocable beneficiary is required for any change.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10 – FILING A CLAIM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10.1 Claimants must notify Summit Life & Casualty within
     90 days of the insured's death or qualifying AD&D event.

10.2 Required documents:
     • Completed claim form (available at summitlifeclaims.com)
     • Certified death certificate (life claims)
     • Attending physician's statement (AD&D claims)
     • Police or accident report, if applicable (AD&D claims)
     • Proof of beneficiary identity and relationship

10.3 Submit to:
     Summit Life & Casualty – Group Claims
     800 Commerce Blvd., Columbus, OH 43215
     Toll-Free: 1-800-555-7742

10.4 Summit will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days
     and render a decision within 15 business days of complete
     documentation.
