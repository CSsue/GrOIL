───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – ABOUT THIS PLAN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This is a group term life plan made available through
    Brightwork's worker benefits platform, powered by Ember
    Insurance Technology. The master policy is issued by
    Verity Life Insurance Company to Brightwork as sponsor.

1.2 Coverage is available to any Brightwork-registered worker
    who has completed at least one platform transaction within
    the preceding 90 days ("Active Worker").

1.3 This plan is NOT an employer-sponsored benefit under IRC
    §79. Premiums are not excludable from gross income as
    employer-provided group term life.

1.4 All coverage elections, changes, and cancellations are
    managed through the Brightwork app or web portal.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY AND ENROLLMENT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 ELIGIBILITY: Any registered Brightwork worker who (a) is
    at least 18 years old, (b) has completed at least one
    platform transaction, and (c) resides in the United States.

2.2 INITIAL ENROLLMENT: Workers may enroll within 60 days of
    first platform transaction without EOI for coverage up to
    $300,000.

2.3 OPEN ENROLLMENT: Brightwork's platform offers continuous
    enrollment. Workers may enroll or increase coverage at any
    time; however, increases above the GI Limit (see 3.3) or
    outside the initial 60-day window require EOI.

2.4 ACTIVE WORKER REQUIREMENT: Coverage requires at least one
    completed platform transaction in any rolling 90-day
    period. Workers inactive for 90 or more consecutive days
    are placed in a "Lapse Hold" pending activity or
    self-pay election (see Section 4.3).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT AMOUNTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 Workers elect coverage in the following amounts:
      $50,000 / $100,000 / $200,000 / $300,000 / $500,000

3.2 PREMIUM SCHEDULE (per $50,000 of coverage per month):

    Age Bracket     Monthly Rate per $50k
    ─────────────────────────────────────
    18–29           $1.80
    30–39           $2.50
    40–49           $4.00
    50–59           $7.50
    60–69           $14.00

3.3 GUARANTEED ISSUE LIMIT: $300,000 within first 60 days
    of enrollment. EOI required above $300,000 or for
    enrollment after initial window.

3.4 MAXIMUM COVERAGE: $500,000.

3.5 Coverage amount may be decreased at any time with no
    underwriting required. Decreases take effect the first
    day of the next calendar month.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – PREMIUM PAYMENT AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Premiums are deducted automatically from Brightwork
    platform earnings on the first business day of each
    calendar month for coverage in that month.

4.2 If platform earnings in a given month are insufficient
    to cover the premium, Verity will attempt to collect
    via the worker's registered payment method on file.

4.3 LAPSE HOLD: If a worker has no platform activity for
    90+ consecutive days and no registered payment method,
    coverage enters Lapse Hold. Lapse Hold continues for
    30 days, during which:
      (a) Coverage remains in force, and
      (b) The worker may restore active status or switch
          to direct-pay to prevent lapse.

4.4 If Lapse Hold expires without resolution, coverage
    terminates. Reinstatement requires re-enrollment and
    a new 2-year contestability period.

4.5 Workers may cancel coverage at any time through the
    platform app. Cancellation takes effect immediately.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – PORTABILITY AND CONVERSION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 Because coverage is directly linked to platform
    participation, portability operates differently from
    employer-sponsored plans.

5.2 CONTINUED DIRECT-PAY: At any time, a worker may switch
    from earnings-deduction to direct monthly billing and
    continue coverage regardless of platform activity.
    Coverage amount and terms remain the same.

5.3 CONVERSION: If the master policy terminates (e.g.,
    Brightwork ceases operations), covered workers may convert
    to an individual policy within 31 days without EOI.
    Maximum convertible amount: the lesser of current coverage
    or $200,000.

5.4 No employer-style portability provision applies.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 Workers with life expectancy of 12 months or less,
    certified by a licensed physician, may accelerate up to
    70% of coverage, subject to a maximum of $250,000.

6.2 An actuarial discount (interest rate-based) applies.
    No additional fees or administrative charges.

6.3 Accelerated benefits reduce the death benefit paid to
    beneficiaries. The worker receives the accelerated amount
    directly.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 SUICIDE: No benefit payable for suicide within 24 months
    of the effective date of any enrollment or reinstatement.
    For coverage in force more than 24 months, the full
    benefit is payable regardless of cause of death.

7.2 INCONTESTABILITY: After 24 months of continuous coverage
    (without lapse), Verity may not contest the validity of
    coverage based on misrepresentation in the application,
    except for fraud.

7.3 No exclusion for occupation, hazardous activities, or
    international travel. Coverage is worldwide.

7.4 No pre-existing condition exclusion.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 This plan does not include a traditional waiver of premium
    for disability. Workers who become disabled should elect
    direct-pay billing (Section 5.2) to maintain coverage
    independently of platform activity.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – BENEFICIARY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.2 Workers may designate any person, trust, or entity as

9.3 If no valid beneficiary designation exists, the benefit
