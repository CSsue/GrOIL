───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – NATURE OF THIS PLAN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This is an association group term life plan. The policyholder
    is NAIFA, not any employer. Coverage is available solely to
    active members in good standing and their eligible spouses.

1.2 Coverage is not contingent upon employment. A member who
    retires, changes employment, or becomes self-employed
    retains coverage so long as NAIFA membership is maintained
    and premiums are paid.

1.3 The master policy is held by NAIFA. This certificate is
    your evidence of coverage under that policy.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – ELIGIBILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 MEMBER ELIGIBILITY: Active NAIFA members in continuous
    good standing are eligible. Coverage ends immediately upon
    lapse or termination of NAIFA membership for any reason.

2.2 SPOUSE ELIGIBILITY: The member's legal spouse or qualified
    domestic partner is eligible. Divorced spouses lose
    eligibility on the date the divorce is finalized.

2.3 NEW MEMBERS: Members within 60 days of joining NAIFA may
    enroll at any coverage tier through Tier 4 without EOI.
    Enrollment after 60 days requires EOI for any tier.

2.4 OPEN ENROLLMENT: NAIFA conducts an annual 30-day open
    enrollment each January. Members may increase coverage
    by one tier without EOI. Increases of two or more tiers
    require EOI.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – COVERAGE TIERS AND AMOUNTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 Member coverage is elected in tiers:

    Tier    Member Benefit    Monthly Rate (Age 55–59)
    ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
    1       $50,000           $10.25
    2       $100,000          $20.50
    3       $250,000          $47.50
    4       $500,000          $92.00
    5       $750,000          $139.50

    Rates vary by 5-year age band and are published annually.
    The current rate schedule is attached as Schedule 1.

3.2 MAXIMUM MEMBER BENEFIT: $750,000 (Tier 5).

3.3 SPOUSE COVERAGE: Spouse coverage is available in a flat
    amount of $25,000 or $50,000. Spouse coverage may not
    exceed 50% of member coverage in force.

3.4 EOI THRESHOLDS:
      • New enrollment or open enrollment increase without EOI:
        Tiers 1–4 (up to $500,000)
      • Tier 5 ($750,000) always requires EOI
      • Any enrollment outside the 60-day new-member window
        or annual open enrollment requires EOI

3.5 RATE ADJUSTMENTS: Rates are age-banded and recalculated
    when the member enters a new 5-year age band at each
    policy anniversary. Cornerstone provides 90 days' notice
    before any rate increase affecting an existing tier.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – CONTINUATION AND TERMINATION OF COVERAGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Coverage remains in force so long as:
    (a) NAIFA membership is maintained in good standing, and
    (b) Monthly premiums are paid when due

4.2 Coverage terminates immediately upon:
    (a) Voluntary cancellation by the member
    (b) Lapse or revocation of NAIFA membership
    (c) Non-payment of premium (subject to grace period)
    (d) Age 80 (coverage is not available past age 80)
    (e) Death of the member

4.3 GRACE PERIOD: A 31-day grace period applies to monthly
    premiums. If premium is received within the grace period,
    coverage is deemed continuous. Death during the grace
    period is covered.

4.4 AGE 75 REDUCTION: Coverage automatically reduces to 50%
    of the elected tier benefit at the policy anniversary
    following the member's 75th birthday.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – CONVERSION RIGHT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 If coverage terminates because NAIFA membership lapses
    or the master policy is cancelled, the member may convert
    to an individual whole-life or term policy within 31 days
    without EOI.

5.2 Conversion is not available if coverage terminates due to
    non-payment of premium or member-requested cancellation.

5.3 Maximum convertible amount: $250,000 or the member's
    coverage in force, whichever is less.

5.4 PORTABILITY: Because this is not employer-sponsored
    coverage, conventional portability provisions do not apply.
    Membership is the sole condition for continued coverage.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 Members diagnosed with a terminal condition by two
    independent licensed physicians may request acceleration
    of up to 60% of the member benefit, subject to a maximum
    of $300,000.

6.2 Life expectancy must be certified as 18 months or less.

6.3 Cornerstone applies an actuarial discount based on the
    member's current age and anticipated life expectancy.

6.4 Spouse coverage is not eligible for acceleration.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 SUICIDE: No death benefit is payable for suicide within
    24 months of the effective date of any new or increased
    coverage. For amounts of coverage in force for more than
    24 months, the full benefit is payable for suicide.

7.2 INCONTESTABILITY: After 2 years from the effective date
    of any coverage amount, Cornerstone may not contest a
    claim on that amount on the basis of misrepresentation,
    except for fraudulent misrepresentation.

7.3 No exclusion for pre-existing conditions, occupation,
    foreign travel, or aviation (other than as a crew member
    of a non-commercial aircraft operating outside the U.S.).

7.4 FOREIGN DEATH: Claims arising from death outside the
    United States require an authenticated death certificate
    and, if applicable, a U.S. consular report of death. A
    90-day supplemental review period applies to foreign
    death claims.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 If the member becomes totally disabled before age 65,
    premiums are waived after a 6-month elimination period
    and remain waived while disability continues.

8.2 "Total disability" means complete inability to engage in
    any gainful occupation for which the member is reasonably
    suited. Own-occupation standard does not apply.

8.3 Waiver of premium is available for member coverage only.
    Spouse coverage premiums are not waived.

8.4 Waiver terminates at age 65 or recovery, whichever is
    earlier.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – BENEFICIARY AND CLAIMS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 Members designate beneficiaries through NAIFA's online
    member portal. Changes are effective upon submission.

9.2 Spouse coverage beneficiary is the member. If the member
    predeceases the covered spouse, the spouse's estate
    receives the spouse benefit.

9.3 Claims must be submitted within 12 months of death.
    Required: certified death certificate, completed claim
    form, proof of beneficiary identity.

9.4 Submit to:
    Cornerstone National Life – Association Claims
    2200 Insurance Row, Minneapolis, MN 55402
    Phone: 1-866-555-0044
    Email: assocclaims@cornerstonell.com

9.5 Payment is issued within 10 business days of complete
    documentation.
