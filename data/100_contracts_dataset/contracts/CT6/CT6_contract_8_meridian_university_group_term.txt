───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – ABOUT THIS CERTIFICATE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 Eastern University maintains this group term life program
    for eligible faculty and staff through Meridian Academic
    Life Insurance Company ("Meridian"). This certificate
    describes your rights and coverage.

1.2 This plan is administered by Eastern University's Office
    of Human Resources in coordination with Meridian. The
    Master Policy is on file in the HR office.

1.3 This plan is separate from any retirement life insurance
    or survivor benefits provided through a pension or
    defined contribution retirement plan.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – EMPLOYEE CLASSES AND ELIGIBILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 Coverage is available in two classes:

    CLASS I: Faculty (tenure-track, tenured, and full-time
    research faculty): eligible for basic and supplemental
    life coverage.

    CLASS II: Staff (full-time professional and administrative
    staff working 30+ hours/week): eligible for basic coverage
    and limited supplemental coverage (up to 1x salary).

2.2 Eligibility begins on the first day of the academic year
    following hire, or on the first day of the month after
    completion of 60 days of service, whichever is earlier.

2.3 Supplemental coverage requires enrollment within 31 days
    of initial eligibility without EOI. Late enrollment or
    increases beyond the current amount require EOI.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT AMOUNTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 BASIC LIFE:
    Class I Faculty:  1x annual base salary (nearest $1,000)
    Class II Staff:   1x annual base salary (nearest $1,000)

3.2 SUPPLEMENTAL LIFE (Class I only): Available in increments
    of 1x salary, up to a maximum of 4x salary. EOI required
    for amounts above 3x salary or $600,000, whichever is less.

3.3 BENEFIT RECALCULATION: Benefits are recalculated on
    October 1 each year based on the prior July 1 salary.

3.4 AGE REDUCTION SCHEDULE:
      Active employees age 65–69: 10% reduction per year
      Active employees age 70+:   50% of age-65 benefit
    Supplemental coverage is subject to the same reduction.

3.5 MAXIMUM TOTAL COVERAGE: $2,000,000 (basic + supplemental).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – SABBATICAL AND LEAVE OF ABSENCE PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 SABBATICAL LEAVE: Faculty on approved sabbatical leave
    (up to 12 months) retain full basic and supplemental
    life coverage. The employer continues premium payments
    during sabbatical as if the faculty member were on active
    duty.

4.2 RESEARCH LEAVE / EXTERNAL GRANT APPOINTMENT: Faculty on
    approved external research appointments (e.g., NSF,
    NIH, or fellowship programs) where salary is paid by
    an external funding source retain coverage. The employer
    continues basic coverage; the faculty member is responsible
    for supplemental premiums.

4.3 UNPAID PERSONAL LEAVE: Coverage may continue for up to
    6 months on unpaid personal leave. The employee is
    responsible for both basic and supplemental premiums
    via self-pay during this period.

4.4 FMLA: Coverage continues through FMLA leave per
    federal law with premium responsibilities as they exist
    during active employment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – RETIREE LIFE INSURANCE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 Faculty and staff who retire from Eastern University with
    at least 15 years of continuous service and at age 60
    or later are eligible for retiree group term life
    insurance ("Retiree Coverage").

5.2 RETIREE BENEFIT AMOUNT:
    Years of Service at Retirement   Retiree Benefit
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
    15–19 years                      $25,000
    20–24 years                      $35,000
    25 or more years                 $50,000

5.3 Retiree coverage is entirely employer-funded. No premium
    is charged to the retiree.

5.4 Active coverage converts automatically to retiree
    coverage upon retirement. No application is required.

5.5 Retiree supplemental coverage terminates at retirement.
    Retirees may convert supplemental coverage to an individual
    policy within 31 days of retirement without EOI.

5.6 Retiree coverage is NOT subject to age reduction after
    commencement and continues for life at the benefit level
    determined at retirement.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – PORTABILITY (PRE-RETIREMENT DEPARTURE)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 Faculty or staff who depart before meeting retiree
    eligibility may port basic and supplemental coverage
    as a direct-pay policy.

6.2 Maximum portable amount: $500,000.

6.3 Application must be submitted within 31 days of
    termination. No EOI required.

6.4 Ported coverage is subject to age-banded group rates
    paid directly to Meridian.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 Available to active employees and retirees upon
    certification of terminal illness with life expectancy
    of 12 months or less.

7.2 Maximum acceleration: 75% of the applicable benefit
    (basic + supplemental for active; retiree benefit for
    retirees), not to exceed $500,000.

7.3 Minimum acceleration: $10,000.

7.4 Actuarial discount applies at a rate not to exceed
    the 90-day Treasury bill rate plus 100 basis points.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 If an employee becomes totally disabled before age 65
    and remains disabled for 6 consecutive months, premiums
    for basic and supplemental life coverage are waived
    for the duration of disability.

8.2 "Total disability" for Class I Faculty means inability
    to perform the duties of an academic faculty member
    (teaching, research, service) as confirmed by the
    Faculty Senate disability review process and a licensed
    physician.

8.3 Disability premium waiver is available through age 65.
    At 65, the normal age reduction schedule applies.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 SUICIDE: No benefit payable for suicide within 12 months
    of the effective date of coverage or any benefit increase.
    After 12 months, suicide is a covered cause of death.

9.2 INCONTESTABILITY: After 2 years, Meridian may not contest
    coverage based on application misrepresentation except
    for fraud. This clause applies independently to basic
    and supplemental coverage from their respective effective
    dates.

9.3 No exclusion for pre-existing conditions, foreign travel,
    or occupational hazards associated with academic research
    (including laboratory work and international field study).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10 – CLAIMS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10.1 Claims should be filed with Eastern University HR, which
     forwards documentation to Meridian.

10.2 Required: certified death certificate, completed claim
     form, proof of beneficiary identity.

10.3 Retiree claims may be filed directly with Meridian:
     Meridian Academic Life – Benefits Center
     700 University Avenue, Providence, RI 02903
     Phone: 1-877-555-3344

10.4 Meridian targets payment within 10 business days of
     complete documentation.
