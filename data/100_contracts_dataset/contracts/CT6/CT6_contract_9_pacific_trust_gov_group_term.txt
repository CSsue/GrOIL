───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – STATUTORY AND REGULATORY AUTHORITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.1 This plan is established pursuant to Cascade State Code
    §44-7200 et seq. (Public Employee Benefits Act) and
    administered by the Department of Administration, Office
    of Employee Benefits ("OEB").

1.2 Coverage is provided under a master group policy issued
    by Pacific Trust Life & Annuity ("Pacific Trust") to the
    State of Cascade.

1.3 In the event of any conflict between this certificate and
    applicable law or the master policy, the master policy
    and Cascade state law govern.

1.4 This plan is exempt from ERISA as a governmental plan
    under ERISA §4(b)(1).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – COVERAGE OPTIONS AND ELIGIBILITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1 The following coverage options are available to eligible
    state employees:

    OPTION A (Basic Life): 1x annual base salary. Available
    to all eligible employees. Employer subsidizes 60% of
    premium. Enrollment is automatic; declining requires
    written waiver.

    OPTION B (2x Basic Life): 2x annual base salary.
    Employer subsidizes 60% of premium. Requires EOI unless
    elected within 31 days of initial eligibility.

    OPTION C (Optional Life – 1x): Additional 1x salary on
    top of Option A. Employee-paid. No EOI if elected within
    31 days; EOI required thereafter.

    OPTION D (Optional Life – 2x): Additional 2x salary on
    top of Option A. Employee-paid. EOI required at all times.

2.2 Eligibility extends to all permanent classified and
    unclassified civil service employees, including full-time
    and part-time employees (at pro-rated benefit if working
    50–99% of full-time equivalent).

2.3 Temporary employees, contract employees, and contractors
    employed through third-party staffing agencies are not
    eligible.

2.4 OPEN ENROLLMENT: Annual open enrollment in November.
    Employees may change options without EOI only during
    the 15-day open enrollment window. Option increases
    require EOI outside this window.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – BENEFIT CALCULATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1 Annual base salary is as recorded on the certified
    payroll as of January 1, rounded to the nearest $1,000.

3.2 Benefits are updated annually on January 1 following
    any salary change from annual cost-of-living adjustments,
    promotions, or reclassifications. Mid-year changes
    (except for reclassification to a higher grade) do not
    affect coverage until the next January 1 renewal.

3.3 AGE REDUCTION:
      Age 65: reduces to 65% of benefit in force at age 65
      Age 70: reduces to 50% of benefit in force at age 65
    Reduction applies equally to Options A, B, C, and D.

3.4 MAXIMUM: No single employee may have total coverage
    exceeding $600,000.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – CONTINUATION COVERAGE (CASCADE PUBLIC ACT §44-7219)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4.1 Under the Cascade Public Employee Benefits Continuation
    Act ("CPEBCA"), employees who terminate employment may
    continue life coverage for up to 18 months by paying
    100% of the group premium directly to OEB.

4.2 Eligible qualifying events for continuation:
    (a) Voluntary resignation
    (b) Termination (other than for gross misconduct)
    (c) Reduction in hours below the eligibility threshold
    (d) Approved leave of absence without pay beyond 12 weeks

4.3 Continuation applies to Options A and C. Option B
    and D coverage may be continued at the employee's
    election during the continuation period.

4.4 CPEBCA continuation is separate from and in addition to
    any conversion right (Section 5).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – CONVERSION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 Upon termination of group coverage for any reason except
    nonpayment of premium, the employee may convert to an
    individual whole-life policy within 31 days without EOI.

5.2 Maximum convertible amount: the lesser of coverage in
    force or $250,000.

5.3 The conversion right may be exercised at the end of any
    CPEBCA continuation period, provided the 31-day window
    is measured from the date continuation coverage ends.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6 – RETIREE COVERAGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6.1 Employees who retire through the State of Cascade
    Retirement System (SCRS) with at least 10 years of
    state service are eligible for retiree group term coverage.

6.2 RETIREE BENEFIT:
      10–19 years service at retirement: $15,000 flat
      20–29 years service at retirement: $25,000 flat
      30 or more years at retirement:    $35,000 flat

6.3 Retiree coverage is employer-funded; no premium charged.

6.4 Optional life (Options C and D) terminates at retirement.
    Retirees may convert optional coverage within 31 days.

6.5 Retiree coverage is not subject to further age reduction
    once retiree status commences.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7 – ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7.1 Employees and retirees with a certified terminal illness
    (life expectancy ≤12 months) may accelerate up to 50%
    of total group life benefit, not to exceed $100,000.

7.2 Minimum acceleration: $5,000. Actuarial discount applies.

7.3 For active employees, acceleration of optional coverage
    requires concurrent acceleration of a proportional amount
    of basic coverage.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8 – EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8.1 SUICIDE: As required by Cascade §44-7228, no suicide
    exclusion may be applied after 12 months of coverage.
    During the first 12 months, no basic or optional benefit
    is payable for death by suicide.

8.2 INCONTESTABILITY: Per Cascade insurance regulations,
    after 2 years Pacific Trust may not deny a claim based
    on misrepresentation except for fraudulent intent to
    obtain coverage.

8.3 No pre-existing condition exclusion. No exclusion for
    occupational hazards related to public safety duties.

8.4 LAW ENFORCEMENT AND FIREFIGHTER PROVISIONS: For employees
    in hazardous duty classifications (firefighter, law
    enforcement, corrections), no additional exclusion
    applies for death in the line of duty.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9 – WAIVER OF PREMIUM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 Upon approval of a long-term disability claim under the
    State's Long-Term Disability plan (Plan 44-LTD), life
    insurance premiums are automatically waived without
    a separate application.

9.2 Waiver continues while LTD benefits are payable.
    Coverage continues at the benefit level in force at
    the onset of disability (not subject to annual salary
    recalculation during waiver period).

9.3 Waiver terminates when: LTD benefits end, the employee
    returns to active service, the employee retires, or
    the employee attains age 70.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
