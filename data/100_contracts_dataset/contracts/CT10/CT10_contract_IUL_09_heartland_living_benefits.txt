ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Chronic Illness Acceleration" - A living benefit allowing
    early access to a portion of the death benefit when the
    insured qualifies as chronically ill under HIPAA §101(f).

1.2 "Chronically Ill" (HIPAA definition) - An individual who:
    (a) Cannot perform at least 2 of 6 Activities of Daily
        Living (ADLs) due to a loss of functional capacity
        expected to last at least 90 days, OR
    (b) Requires substantial supervision to protect from
        threats to health and safety due to severe cognitive
        impairment.

1.3 "Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)" - The 6 standard ADLs:
    Bathing, Dressing, Eating, Continence, Toileting, and
    Transferring (moving in/out of bed or chair).

1.4 "Per Diem Limit" - The IRS §7702B daily limit for tax-free
    chronic illness benefit payments ($400/day in 2024;
    adjusted annually for inflation).

1.5 "Indemnity-Style Benefit" - A chronic illness benefit paid
    as a fixed monthly amount without requiring proof of actual
    LTC expenses incurred, as long as qualifying criteria are met.

1.6 "Reimbursement-Style Benefit" - A benefit paid based on
    actual qualified LTC expenses submitted; this policy uses
    INDEMNITY style (more flexible for the insured).

ARTICLE 3 - HOW INDEX CREDITING WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 ANNUAL POINT-TO-POINT: Standard formula.
    Credit = MAX(0%, MIN(Cap, Index% Change))

3.2 MONTHLY AVERAGE METHOD:
    Sum of 12 monthly index values ÷ 12 = Average
    Monthly Average Return = (Average - Start) ÷ Start
    Credit = MAX(0%, MIN(Cap, Monthly Average Return))

    Example: Index starts at 5,000. Monthly values over
    12 months average to 5,350.
    Return = (5,350 - 5,000) ÷ 5,000 = 7.0% → capped at 7%.
    If market surges late in year (P-to-P would credit higher),
    monthly average dampens the gain. If market surges early
    but pulls back, monthly average protects some gain.

3.3 FIVE-YEAR ACCOUNT: Uses point-to-point over the full
    5-year segment. A policy anniversary every 5 years is
    the crediting event.

ARTICLE 6 - LIVING BENEFITS: CHRONIC ILLNESS RIDER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 ELIGIBILITY: The Chronic Illness Acceleration Rider (CIAR)
    is activated when the insured:
    (a) Is certified by a licensed health care practitioner
        as chronically ill (HIPAA §101(f) definition), AND
    (b) Has been chronically ill for at least 90 consecutive
        days, AND
    (c) The condition is expected to be permanent or last
        at least 12 months.

6.2 BENEFIT AMOUNT: Indemnity-style monthly benefit equal to
    the LESSER of:
    (a) 4% of the death benefit per month ($14,000/month
        at $350,000 face), OR
    (b) The IRS per diem limit × 30 days ($400 × 30 = $12,000
        per month in 2024)
    No receipts or LTC invoices required to receive payment.

6.3 MAXIMUM BENEFIT: Cumulative acceleration limited to 90%
    of death benefit ($315,000). Remaining 10% reserved
    for death benefit at end.

6.4 BENEFIT PERIOD: Benefits paid monthly as long as:
    (a) Insured remains eligible (re-certified annually), AND
    (b) Cumulative maximum has not been reached, AND
    (c) Policy remains in force.

6.5 EFFECT ON POLICY: Each monthly chronic illness payment
    reduces the death benefit and account value proportionally.
    COI charges also reduce proportionally.

6.6 WAITING PERIOD: 90-day elimination period from onset of
    qualifying chronic illness to first benefit payment.

6.7 INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENT: Heartland Security may require
    an independent medical review. Owner may contest any
    unfavorable assessment within 30 days.

6.8 TAX TREATMENT: Indemnity LTC benefits up to the IRS
    per diem limit ($400/day in 2024) are generally income
    tax-free per IRC §7702B. Amounts above the per diem
    limit may be taxable. Consult a tax professional.

ARTICLE 10 - EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE: Return of premium if death by suicide within
     2 years. Chronic illness acceleration payments made
     prior to death are not recoverable.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: 2 years from issue.

10.3 CHRONIC ILLNESS BENEFIT EXCLUSIONS:
     Chronic illness benefits are NOT payable if the
     qualifying condition arises from or is materially
     caused by:
     (a) Alcohol or drug addiction (unless actively treated)
     (b) War or act of war (declared or undeclared)
     (c) Intentional self-inflicted injury
     (d) Any pre-existing condition that was not disclosed
         on the application and that would have affected
         the underwriting decision (within 2-year period)

10.4 PRE-EXISTING CONDITION LOOK-BACK: For chronic illness
     purposes, a pre-existing condition is one for which the
     insured received medical treatment, consultation, or
     diagnosis within 12 months before policy issue.
