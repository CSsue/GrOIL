ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Table Rating" - An underwriting classification for
    substandard risks, expressed as a multiplier to the
    standard COI rate. Table D = 1.50× standard COI.

1.2 "Flat Extra Premium" - An additional flat dollar charge
    per $1,000 of face amount per year applied to certain
    substandard risks. Not applicable to this policy (table
    rating used instead of flat extra).

1.3 "Rating Improvement Rider" - A rider providing that the
    table rating will be reviewed and potentially improved
    at specified intervals based on health evidence submitted.

1.4 "Simplified Underwriting" - A streamlined application
    process using health questionnaire and third-party data
    (MIB, Rx check, motor vehicle report) without requiring
    a physical exam.

1.5 "Impaired Risk" - An insured whose health history results
    in a substandard rating classification.

1.6 "Standard COI" - The cost of insurance rate applicable
    to a Standard risk of the same age and gender.

ARTICLE 3 - HOW INDEX CREDITING WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Standard annual point-to-point crediting:
    Credit = MAX(0%, MIN(Cap, Index% Change))

3.2 CHARGE OFFSET CONTEXT: At Table D rating, approximate
    Year 1 monthly COI is ~$260/month (~$3,120/year).
    At a 6% average annual index credit on a $9,000/year
    funded policy, the account value growth may partially
    but not fully offset rising COI charges in later years.
    Owner should review in-force projections regularly.

3.3 The 0% floor ensures market downturns do not compound
    the pressure from high COI charges.

ARTICLE 5 - MONTHLY POLICY CHARGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 COST OF INSURANCE:
    Monthly COI = Standard COI Rate × Table D Multiplier (1.50)
                × NAR ÷ 1,000

    The 1.50× multiplier reflects the actuarial assessment
    of elevated mortality risk due to the rated conditions.
    Maximum COI rates (guaranteed) are shown in the policy
    schedule and include the table rating.

5.2 ADMINISTRATIVE FEE: $12 per month (guaranteed for life).

5.3 ILLUSTRATIVE COI TRAJECTORY (Table D, Male):
    Age 59: ~$17/month per $1,000 NAR (×1.50 = ~$25.50)
    Age 70: ~$40/month per $1,000 NAR (×1.50 = ~$60.00)
    Age 80: ~$110/month per $1,000 NAR (×1.50 = ~$165.00)
    These are current non-guaranteed rates for illustration;
    guaranteed maximums are approximately 2× these amounts.

5.4 COI DRAG WARNING: At older attained ages, Table D COI
    rates consume account value significantly faster than
    at standard rates. Long-term policy sustainability depends
    on consistent premium funding above the planned amount.

ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6.2 ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT - TERMINAL: Available if life

    months reflects common rated risk disease profile).
    Maximum acceleration: 75% or $187,500.

6.3 ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT - CHRONIC ILLNESS: Available
    for inability to perform 2 of 6 ADLs. Given the medical
    profile of this insured, this benefit may be particularly
    relevant. Benefit: up to 2% of face per month ($5,000/month
    maximum). Cumulative limit: 80% of face amount.

6.4 FACE AMOUNT REDUCTION OPTION: Owner may reduce face amount
    at any policy anniversary to lower COI charges. Minimum
    face after reduction: $100,000. This is a valuable option
    if financial needs change or if lower charges are needed.
    No surrender charge applies to face reductions.

ARTICLE 7 - RATING IMPROVEMENT PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 RATING IMPROVEMENT RIDER (Included at no additional charge):
    Owner may request a rating review at the following intervals:
    • 3 years from policy issue (November 2027)
    • 5 years from policy issue (November 2029)
    • Every 3 years thereafter through age 70

7.2 REVIEW PROCESS: Owner submits:
    (a) Attending physician statement
    (b) Updated lab work (A1C, blood pressure, lipid panel)
    (c) Current body weight and BMI

7.3 IMPROVEMENT OUTCOMES:
    If health metrics improve sufficiently, Inclusive Life
    may reduce the table rating (e.g., Table D → Table B)
    or remove the table rating entirely (Standard).
    Rating improvements are permanent and retroactive to
    the next policy anniversary after approval.

7.4 NO IMPROVEMENT: If health metrics remain unchanged or
    worsen, the table rating remains as issued. There is no
    penalty for requesting a review that does not result in
    improvement.

7.5 REMOVAL OF FLAT EXTRAS: If any flat extra charges were
    assessed (none on this policy), they may be removed
    separately from the table rating.
