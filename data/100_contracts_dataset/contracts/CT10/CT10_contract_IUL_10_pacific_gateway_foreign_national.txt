═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Foreign National" - For purposes of this policy, a
    non-US citizen who has obtained this policy while present
    in the United States, with the policy issued under US
    law and in US dollars.

1.2 "Non-Resident Alien (NRA)" - A foreign national who is
    not a US citizen and does not meet the IRS substantial
    presence test for US residency.

1.3 "US Nexus" - A connection to the United States sufficient
    to support the issuance of a US life insurance policy,
    including: presence for medical exam, insurable interest
    in the US, or documented US financial assets.

1.4 "FATCA" - Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act; US law
    requiring reporting of US financial accounts held by
    foreign nationals.

1.5 "CRS" - Common Reporting Standard; OECD framework
    for automatic exchange of tax information between
    participating jurisdictions, including Singapore.

1.6 "Repatriation Risk" - The potential difficulty of
    remitting death benefit proceeds to foreign beneficiaries
    due to currency controls, sanctions, or other legal
    restrictions in the beneficiary's country of residence.

ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 LEVEL DEATH BENEFIT: $2,000,000 USD payable upon death
    of insured, subject to the conditions herein.

6.2 PAYMENT TO FOREIGN BENEFICIARIES:
    Pacific Gateway will remit proceeds in USD via
    international wire to a bank account designated by
    the beneficiary. It is the beneficiary's responsibility
    to comply with all applicable laws in their country
    of residence regarding receipt of insurance proceeds.

6.3 SANCTIONS COMPLIANCE: No death benefit will be paid
    if the payment would violate US Office of Foreign
    Assets Control (OFAC) regulations, including if the
    beneficiary or their country of residence is subject
    to US sanctions at the time of claim.

6.4 REPORTING: Death benefit payments to foreign beneficiaries
    exceeding $10,000 will be reported per applicable US
    federal reporting requirements.

6.5 COUNTRY RISK REVIEW: If the beneficiary's country of
    residence has been added to OFAC sanctions lists or
    FATF high-risk lists between policy issue and claim,
    Pacific Gateway will hold proceeds in escrow pending
    legal clearance, for up to 5 years. If clearance is not
    obtained within 5 years, proceeds will be interpleaded
    in a US court of competent jurisdiction.

6.6 ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT: Available for terminal illness
    (life expectancy < 12 months). Maximum 75% of face
    ($1,500,000 USD). Medical documentation may be provided
    by a licensed physician in the insured's country of
    residence if acceptable to Pacific Gateway's medical
    director.

ARTICLE 9 - RESIDENCE AND STATUS CHANGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    suspend premium acceptance and place the policy in a
    paid-up reduced death benefit status pending resolution.

9.4 VISA STATUS: Changes in US visa or immigration status
    do not affect the validity of the policy. However, they
    may affect tax reporting obligations. Owner is solely
    responsible for compliance with applicable tax law.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE: Return of premiums if death by suicide within
     2 years of issue. No geographic restriction on this
     exclusion; applies worldwide.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: 2 years from issue. Pacific Gateway
     may contest for misrepresentation in any language or
     jurisdiction; translation costs borne by Pacific Gateway.

10.3 WAR EXCLUSION (ACTIVE COMBAT): No death benefit is
     payable if death results directly from the insured's
     voluntary participation in armed combat as a member
     of any military, paramilitary, or insurgent organization,
     whether war is declared or not. This exclusion does NOT
     apply to civilian casualties of war, terrorism, or
     political violence.

10.4 AVIATION EXCLUSION: Death while operating (as pilot or
     crew) a non-commercial aircraft is EXCLUDED. Commercial
     aviation as a passenger is fully covered worldwide.

10.5 FOREIGN TRAVEL: No exclusion for foreign travel,
     including high-risk regions, EXCEPT as modified by
     the War Exclusion in Section 10.3.

10.6 SANCTION EXCLUSION: No death benefit is payable if
     payment would violate OFAC or other applicable US
     or international sanctions in effect at the time
     of claim.
