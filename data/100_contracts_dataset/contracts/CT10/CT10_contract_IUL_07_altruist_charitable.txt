ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "NOLI" - Nonprofit-Owned Life Insurance; life insurance
    owned by a 501(c)(3) organization on the life of a donor,
    board member, key executive, or other insurable party.

1.2 "Charitable Endowment" - The accumulation of policy
    value intended to fund a permanent charitable purpose
    upon maturity or insured's death.

1.3 "Charitable Bequest Intent Letter" - A non-binding letter
    from the insured expressing charitable intent, maintained
    in the policy file.

1.4 "Insurable Interest (Charitable)" - Under California law,
    a 501(c)(3) charitable organization has an insurable
    interest in the life of a major donor who has made a
    documented pledge or gift to the organization.

1.5 "Philanthropic Dividend" - An annual bonus interest credit
    of 0.25% applied to account value, available under this
    product series, in lieu of standard persistency bonuses.

1.6 "Index Credit" - Non-guaranteed interest based on index
    performance, subject to cap and floor.

ARTICLE 3 - HOW INDEX CREDITING WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 Annual point-to-point crediting:
    Credit = MAX(0%, MIN(Cap, Index% Change))

3.2 ESG ACCOUNT PERFORMANCE CONTEXT: ESG-screened indices
    may perform differently from unscreened broad market
    indices in any given year. Historical performance of
    the MSCI KLD 400 has been broadly comparable to the
    S&P 500 over long periods, though with some deviations
    in specific market cycles.

3.3 PHILANTHROPIC DIVIDEND APPLICATION: The 0.25% annual
    bonus credit is applied to total account value after
    standard index crediting on each policy anniversary.
    This is separate from and additive to the index credit.

3.4 ZERO FLOOR: No account value reduction from negative
    index performance.

ARTICLE 4 - PREMIUM STRUCTURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 PLANNED PREMIUM: $35,000 per year represents the annual
    charitable contribution budget allocated by the Foundation.

4.2 VARIABLE PREMIUM FLEXIBILITY: Foundations often have
    variable annual contribution capacity. Minimum premium
    per year: $1,000 (as long as account value is sufficient
    to cover charges). Maximum per year: $75,000.

4.3 CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTION NATURE: Premiums are paid by
    the Foundation from charitable assets. Per IRS guidance,
    premiums paid by a 501(c)(3) on a NOLI policy are
    generally not deductible as charitable contributions.
    Altruist does not provide tax advice on this matter.

4.4 PREMIUM LOAD: 4.0% years 1-7; 2.0% years 8+.

4.5 PLANNED CESSATION: The policy is designed to continue
    to endowment size without additional premium after year 20
    (age 85 of insured), though this is not guaranteed.

5.4 CHARGE DISCLOSURE: Total estimated monthly charges in

ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 LEVEL DEATH BENEFIT: $2,000,000 payable to The Waverly
    Foundation upon insured's death.

6.2 CHARITABLE PURPOSE OF PROCEEDS: While Altruist has no
    obligation to enforce the intended charitable use of
    proceeds, the insured's Charitable Bequest Intent Letter
    (in policy file) expresses the intent that all proceeds
    be allocated to the Waverly Scholarship Endowment Fund.

6.3 ACCELERATED BENEFIT FOR TERMINAL ILLNESS: Up to 50%
    of face amount ($1,000,000) available if insured has
    terminal illness with life expectancy < 12 months.
    Proceeds retained by Foundation for ongoing operations.
    Minimum acceleration: $100,000.

6.4 NO PERSONAL BENEFIT: Death benefit proceeds may not be
    used to benefit any private individual in a manner
    that violates IRC §4941 (self-dealing rules). Foundation
    board members are advised to consult with legal counsel
    before accessing policy funds for any purpose other
    than the stated endowment.

ARTICLE 9 - OWNERSHIP CONTINUITY PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 SUCCESSOR OWNER: If The Waverly Foundation, Inc. ceases
    to exist or loses its 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status, ownership
    of this policy may be assigned to another qualifying
    501(c)(3) organization designated by the Foundation's
    board or successor in interest.

9.2 IRS EXEMPTION LOSS: If the Foundation loses tax-exempt
    status and no successor 501(c)(3) can be designated within
    90 days, ownership reverts to the insured, and Altruist
    will reissue the policy with appropriate beneficiary
    designations. No surrender charge applies to this event.

9.3 BOARD RESOLUTION REQUIRED: All policy transactions
    (loans, withdrawals, beneficiary changes, surrenders)
    require a copy of the applicable board resolution
    authorizing the action.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

     issue, death benefit equals premiums paid less charges.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: 2 years from issue.

10.3 CONSENT WITHDRAWAL: If insured withdraws consent for
     the NOLI coverage in writing at any time, Altruist will
     notify the Owner (Foundation) and allow 90 days for
     the Owner to surrender, reassign, or convert the policy.
     Altruist is not obligated to continue coverage beyond
     90 days after receipt of written consent withdrawal.

10.4 NO WAR, AVIATION, OR OCCUPATION EXCLUSIONS.
