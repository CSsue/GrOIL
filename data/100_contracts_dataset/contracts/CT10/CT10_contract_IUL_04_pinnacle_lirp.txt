ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "LIRP" - Life Insurance Retirement Plan; an informal term
    for an overfunded permanent life insurance policy used as a
    supplemental retirement income vehicle.

1.2 "MEC" - Modified Endowment Contract; classification under
    IRC §7702A that subjects distributions to income tax and
    possible 10% penalty. This policy is designed to stay
    below MEC threshold.

1.3 "Seven-Pay Test" - The IRS test determining MEC status;
    premiums in any 7-year period must not exceed the net level
    premium for a fully paid-up policy.

1.4 "GPT" - Guideline Premium Test; the §7702 tax qualification
    method elected, which allows the highest annual premiums
    relative to face amount.

1.5 "Policy Loan Income Strategy" - Tax-efficient withdrawals
    using a combination of basis withdrawals and policy loans
    structured to generate tax-free retirement income.

1.6 "Wash Loan" - A loan arrangement where the loaned amount
    earns a crediting rate equal to (or near) the loan interest
    rate, resulting in minimal net cost to the policy.

ARTICLE 3 - HOW INDEX CREDITING WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 STANDARD ANNUAL FORMULA:
    Credit = MAX(Floor, MIN(Cap, Index Change%))

3.2 UNCAPPED ACCOUNT:
    Credit = MAX(0%, Index Change% × 60%)
    This account is appropriate when long-term upside capture
    is prioritized over capped near-term potential.

3.3 VOLATILITY-CONTROLLED INDEX: The underlying index
    automatically adjusts equity exposure (0%-100%) daily to
    target a 5% volatility budget. In turbulent markets,
    equity exposure decreases, reducing both upside and
    downside. This produces more consistent crediting over time.
    Index is provided by a third-party index provider under
    license; index composition and rules are independently
    published.

3.4 BLOOMBERG DYNAMIC BALANCE: Blends equity and bond futures
    in a daily rules-based process. Index includes an embedded
    cost deduction of approximately 0.50%-0.75% annually that
    reduces gross index performance before caps are applied.

3.5 CREDITED INTEREST AND INCOME: Because this is a GPT policy
    under §7702, interest credited enhances account value and
    may be accessed income-tax-free via the loan strategy
    outlined in Article 7, up to the policy's cost basis.

ARTICLE 4 - PREMIUM STRUCTURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 LIRP DESIGN CONCEPT: This policy is designed to be
    aggressively funded to the maximum non-MEC limit annually.
    High front-loaded premiums maximize account value growth
    by minimizing the death benefit relative to premium.

4.2 PLANNED PREMIUM: $50,000 per year for 10 years ($500,000
    total). Projections assume this funding schedule.

4.3 MEC MONITORING: We will track seven-pay test compliance
    on your behalf and notify you if a proposed additional
    premium would cause MEC classification. Owner is ultimately
    responsible for MEC compliance; consult a tax professional.

4.4 FACE AMOUNT MINIMIZATION: Option 2 death benefit and minimum
    face amount ($500,000) are selected to keep the death benefit
    as low as possible relative to premium, minimizing COI drag
    and maximizing accumulation efficiency.

4.5 CORRIDOR AUTO-ADJUSTMENT: If account value growth causes
    the CVAT/GPT corridor to require a higher death benefit,
    we automatically increase the face amount. No evidence of
    insurability required for corridor increases.

4.6 PREMIUM LOAD: 4.0% years 1-5; 2.0% years 6+.

4.7 SHORT PAY OPTION: Owner may elect to stop premiums after
    year 10 and allow the policy to continue from accumulated
    values. Year 11+ policy is designed to be self-sustaining
    under current assumptions. Guarantees do not apply to
    non-guaranteed premium scenarios.

ARTICLE 7 - RETIREMENT INCOME STRATEGY (LOANS & WITHDRAWALS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 RETIREMENT INCOME CONCEPT:
    Phase 1 (Accumulation): Premiums paid for 10+ years.
    Phase 2 (Income): Owner takes tax-free income via:
    Step A: Withdraw cost basis first (tax-free return of basis).
    Step B: After basis exhausted, use policy loans (tax-free
            per IRC §72 as long as policy remains in force
            and is not a MEC).

7.2 STANDARD POLICY LOANS:
    Maximum: 90% of surrender value.
    Loan rate: 5.0% annually (variable; resets annually based
    on Moody's Composite Bond Yield + 1%).
    Collateral treatment: Loaned amounts earn fixed account rate.

7.3 PARTICIPATING (WASH) LOANS:
    Available beginning in policy year 11.
    Loan rate: 5.0% fixed annually.
    Loaned amounts continue to earn FULL index crediting rate
    (as if not loaned). Net cost: 5.0% interest charged minus
    whatever the indexed accounts earn.
    In a year where index credits = 7%: net gain 2%.
    In a year where index credits = 0%: net cost 5%.
    Wash loans create leveraged income potential but also risk.

7.4 POLICY LOAN STRATEGY ILLUSTRATION:
    Age 65 projected account value (current assumptions): $2.1M
    Annual loan income available at 5% of account: ~$105,000
    Tax character: Tax-free (as long as policy not MEC/lapsed)
    Policy remains in force as long as account value exceeds
    outstanding loan balance (managed repayment critical).

7.5 PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS:
    Minimum: $1,000. No fee for first 12 per year.
    Reduces account value and death benefit under Option 2.
    Reduces cost basis; tracked by Pinnacle for tax reporting.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 9 - LAPSE AND GRACE PERIOD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 Lapse Risk Advisory: A key risk of the LIRP strategy is
    policy lapse during the retirement income phase due to
    excessive loans. If a policy lapses with outstanding loans,
    the net loan amount is treated as taxable income in the
    lapse year per IRC §72. Owner should model income
    distributions conservatively.

9.2 AUTOMATED MONITORING: Pinnacle will send an annual
    sustainability projection to Owner. If modeled loan
    income strategy is projected to cause lapse before age 100,
    Pinnacle will notify Owner and suggest remediation options.

9.3 LAPSE GRACE PERIOD: 61 days from notice. Paying additional
    premium cures the lapse risk; loans cannot be "uncured" except
    by repayment.
