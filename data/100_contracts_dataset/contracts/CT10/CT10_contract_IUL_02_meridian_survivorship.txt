ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Survivorship Policy" - A policy insuring two lives where the
    death benefit is payable only upon the death of the second,
    or last surviving, insured.

1.2 "Blended COI Rate" - A composite mortality rate based on the
    ages, genders, and risk classes of both insureds, calculated
    actuarially to price survivorship coverage.

1.3 "Single Life Continuation" - The period after the first
    insured's death during which the policy continues under
    modified COI calculation based solely on the surviving
    insured.

1.4 "Estate Multiplier" - The ratio of face amount to total
    planned premiums paid; designed to exceed 3:1 in this policy.

1.5 "Index Credit" - Interest applied to an account based on
    measured change of a referenced market index.

1.6 "Guaranteed Interest Account (GIA)" - The fixed rate account
    earning a declared, guaranteed minimum rate.

ARTICLE 3 - HOW INDEX CREDITING WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 STANDARD ANNUAL CREDITING:
    Interest Credited = MAX(0%, MIN(Cap, Index% Change))

3.2 HIGH PARTICIPATION ACCOUNT:
    Interest Credited = MAX(0%, Index% Change × 55%)
    Example: S&P 500 gains 20% → Credit = 11.0%
    Example: S&P 500 gains 8%  → Credit = 4.4%

3.3 MULTI-INDEX BLENDED ACCOUNT:
    The weighted average of the three component indices is
    computed annually. The resulting blended percentage is
    applied to the cap and floor formula. Bond component
    provides downside dampening in equity-down years.

3.4 DEATH OF FIRST INSURED - CREDITING IMPACT:
    Upon first insured's death, the policy continues unchanged
    for the surviving insured. All index allocations, segments,
    and pending credits remain in effect. COI charges adjust
    to reflect single-life survivorship mortality only.

ARTICLE 4 - PREMIUM FLEXIBILITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 PLANNED PREMIUM: $42,000 per year, payable by Trustee from

4.2 CRUMMEY NOTICE COORDINATION: Meridian will provide annual

    documentation. Owner retains responsibility for legal
    Crummey procedures.

4.3 FLEXIBLE PAYMENT: Premiums may be paid monthly, quarterly,
    semi-annually, or annually. Monthly modal factor: 0.0875.
    Quarterly modal factor: 0.255. Semi-annual: 0.510.

4.4 PREMIUM LOAD: 4.5% of each premium, years 1-7.
                  2.0% of each premium, years 8+.

4.5 ESTATE PLANNING NOTE: Planned premium is calibrated to
    maximize the estate multiplier effect while remaining
    within annual gift tax exclusion limits ($18,000 per
    donee in 2024 per IRC §2503(b)).

4.6 LUMP SUM FLEXIBILITY: Additional premiums accepted up to
    IRC §7702 corridor limits. No MEC corridor testing bypass.

ARTICLE 5 - MONTHLY POLICY CHARGES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.1 COST OF INSURANCE: Charged monthly using a blended COI
    rate applied to the net amount at risk. After first insured
    dies, the blended rate converts to a single-life rate for
    the surviving insured. COI rates are guaranteed never to
    exceed those in the policy schedule, which are based on
    2017 CSO tables.

5.2 POLICY FEE: $18 per month (guaranteed, no increase).

5.3 ESTATE PLANNING EXPENSE CHARGE: $0.10 per $1,000 face
    amount per month, deducted for years 1-15 to offset
    high face-amount issuance costs at older insured ages.

5.4 CHARGES AFTER FIRST DEATH: Policy fee and expense charges
    remain unchanged. COI reprices to surviving-life basis.
    No additional underwriting or charges for the transition.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 SECOND-TO-DIE BENEFIT: The full $5,000,000 death benefit
    is payable only upon the death of the last surviving insured.
    No benefit is payable upon the first insured's death.

6.2 FIRST DEATH - CONTINUED PREMIUM WAIVER OPTION:
    Upon first insured's death, Owner may elect the Optional
    Premium Waiver (OPW) at an additional charge of $0.05 per
    $1,000 face per month. If elected within 90 days of first
    death, no further premiums are required to maintain coverage.
    (See Rider OPW-S for full terms.)

6.3 ESTATE TAX DESIGN: Proceeds payable to ILIT are intended
    to be outside the taxable estates of both Grantors.
    Tax treatment depends on proper ILIT establishment and
    administration; counsel with estate attorney is required.

6.4 ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT (ADB): If either surviving insured
    is diagnosed with a terminal illness (life expectancy < 12
    months), Owner may accelerate up to 50% of death benefit.
    Minimum acceleration: $100,000. See Rider ADB-T01.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE: If either insured dies by suicide within 2 years
     of policy issue, we pay only premiums paid minus charges
     and withdrawals. The 2-year period resets upon reinstatement.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: Policy is contestable for 2 years from issue.
     Incontestable thereafter except for fraud. Both insureds'
     representations are subject to this provision.

10.3 SIMULTANEOUS DEATH: If both insureds die simultaneously or
     within 24 hours of each other, the younger insured is deemed
     to have survived for policy purposes.

10.4 NO AVIATION, WAR, OR OCCUPATION EXCLUSIONS apply to this
     policy.
