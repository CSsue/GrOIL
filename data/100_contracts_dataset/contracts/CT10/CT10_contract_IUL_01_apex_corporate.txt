ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Index Account" - A sub-account where interest is credited
    based on the measured performance of an external index, without
    direct ownership of any securities.

1.2 "Segment" - The designated allocation unit within an Index
    Account, carrying its own start date, end date, and crediting
    parameters.

1.3 "High-Water Mark" - For applicable accounts: the highest
    index value recorded on any monthly anniversary within the
    crediting period.

1.4 "Spread" - A deduction applied to index gains before crediting,
    used in lieu of or in addition to a cap rate.

1.5 "Buffer" - For Buffer Accounts: the percentage of index loss
    absorbed by the company before losses affect the account value.

1.6 "Crediting Period" - The duration of a Segment, either 1 year
    or 2 years as specified per account.

1.7 "CVAT" - Cash Value Accumulation Test; the IRC §7702 tax
    qualification method elected for this policy.

ARTICLE 3 - HOW INDEX CREDITING WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 ANNUAL CREDITING FORMULA:
    Credit = MAX(Floor, MIN(Cap, Index% × Participation Rate))

    For Spread Account:
    Credit = MAX(0%, Index% - Spread)

    For Buffer Account:
    If Index% ≥ 0%: Credit = MIN(Cap, Index%)
    If -Buffer ≤ Index% < 0%: Credit = 0%
    If Index% < -Buffer: Credit = Index% + Buffer (negative result)

3.2 TWO-YEAR SEGMENT EXAMPLE:
    Year 1 Index: +12% → no crediting yet
    Year 2 Index: +8% → cumulative performance evaluated
    Two-year cumulative gain: 21.0% → capped at 20.0% → 20.0%
    credited to segment at end of year 2.

3.3 CREDITING DATE: Interest credits are applied to each segment
    on its individual segment anniversary, not the policy
    anniversary. This means different segments within the same
    policy may credit on different calendar dates.

3.4 INTRA-YEAR WITHDRAWALS FROM INDEXED SEGMENTS:
    Withdrawals taken during an active crediting period are
    assessed on a pro-rated basis. No partial-year index credit
    is applied; the withdrawal reduces the segment principal only.

ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 OPTION 2 - INCREASING DEATH BENEFIT (CURRENT):
    Death Benefit = $3,000,000 + Account Value at time of claim
    This design maximizes the net amount at risk efficiency for
    tax-efficient corporate-owned life insurance (COLI) purposes
    and ensures the death benefit always exceeds account value.

6.2 IRC §7702 COMPLIANCE: Under CVAT, the death benefit must
    always equal or exceed the account value multiplied by the
    applicable corridor factor from IRS tables. We will
    automatically increase the death benefit if account value
    growth would otherwise cause a CVAT violation.

6.3 AUTOMATIC FACE INCREASE: If CVAT corridor requirements
    demand a higher death benefit, we increase coverage
    automatically. No additional underwriting is required for
    automatic corridor increases.

6.4 SPLIT-DOLLAR DEATH BENEFIT ALLOCATION (per Endorsement SD-01):
    Employer receives: Greater of total premiums paid or cash
    surrender value at date of death.
    Insured's beneficiary receives: Remaining proceeds, if any.

ARTICLE 10 - EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE: If insured dies by suicide within 2 years of policy
     issue or reinstatement, the death benefit equals total
     premiums paid, less withdrawals, less outstanding loans.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: This policy may be contested for material
     misrepresentation within 2 years of issue date. After 2
     years, this policy is incontestable except for non-payment
     of premium or fraud in the procurement of insurance.

10.3 AVIATION EXCLUSION: No exclusion. Full death benefit is
     payable regardless of aviation-related cause of death.

10.4 WAR EXCLUSION: None. Coverage is worldwide.

10.5 BUSINESS PURPOSE NOTICE (IRC §264): This is a corporate-
     owned policy subject to interest deduction limitations.
     Premium payments are generally not tax-deductible. Owner
     should consult tax counsel annually.
