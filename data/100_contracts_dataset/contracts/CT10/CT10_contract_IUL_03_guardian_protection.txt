ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "No-Lapse Guarantee (NLG)" - A contractual guarantee that
    the policy will not lapse as long as the cumulative
    guaranteed premium has been paid on or before each due date,
    regardless of account value performance.

1.2 "NLG Premium" - The level annual premium amount that, if
    paid cumulatively on time, activates the No-Lapse Guarantee.
    Shown on the Policy Specifications page.

1.3 "Cumulative NLG Test" - The test applied monthly: total
    premiums paid to date must equal or exceed total NLG
    premiums due to date. Failure voids NLG.

1.4 "Shadow Account" - A notional ledger used exclusively to
    test NLG compliance, calculated separately from the actual
    account value. Shadow account uses conservative guaranteed
    assumptions.

1.5 "Protection Zone" - Policy years 1-20, during which the
    NLG provides the primary coverage guarantee.

1.6 "Index Credit" - Non-guaranteed interest based on market
    index performance, subject to cap and floor.

ARTICLE 2 - NO-LAPSE GUARANTEE PROVISIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2.1 GUARANTEE COVERAGE PERIOD: The NLG guarantees coverage
    through the insured's age 90, provided NLG premiums are
    paid as required.

2.2 NLG ACTIVATION REQUIREMENT:
    Each month, we compare:
    (A) Total premiums paid to date (less withdrawals)
    (B) Total NLG premiums due to date ($3,200 × months/12)
    If (A) ≥ (B): NLG is active.
    If (A) < (B): NLG is suspended for that month.

2.3 NLG SUSPENSION: If the NLG is suspended in any month
    and account value is also insufficient to cover monthly
    charges, the policy enters the 61-day grace period. The
    NLG resumes immediately if cumulative payments are brought
    current before the grace period expires.

2.4 NLG VOID EVENTS: The NLG is permanently voided by:
    (a) Any outstanding policy loan balance (including accrued
        interest) during the NLG guarantee period.
    (b) Any partial withdrawal during policy years 1-10.
    (c) Cumulative unpaid NLG premiums that are never remedied.
    CRITICAL: Taking a loan permanently voids the No-Lapse
    Guarantee. This policy is primarily a protection product.

2.5 NLG DEATH BENEFIT: During any year the NLG is active, the
    full $1,000,000 death benefit is guaranteed regardless of
    account value, even if account value is zero or negative.

2.6 NLG EXTENSION: Owner may extend NLG coverage past age 90
    to age 100 by paying an additional rider premium of $400
    per year beginning at age 80. See Rider NLG-EXT.

ARTICLE 4 - PREMIUM STRUCTURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4.1 NLG PREMIUM TIER: $3,200 per year maintains the No-Lapse
    Guarantee through age 90. Paying this amount on time each
    year is the primary commitment for this policy.

4.2 ACCUMULATION PREMIUM TIER: Premiums above $3,200 per year
    (up to IRS limits) are treated as accumulation contributions
    and directed per the elected index allocation.

4.3 PREMIUM ALLOCATION SEQUENCING:
    First $3,200/year: Allocated 100% to Fixed Account to
    preserve NLG shadow account compliance.
    Amounts above $3,200: Allocated per elected index allocation.

4.4 PREMIUM LOAD: 3.0% of all premiums, all years.

4.5 MODAL PREMIUMS:
    Monthly:      $276 ($3,312 annually - includes modal fee)
    Quarterly:    $820 ($3,280 annually)
    Semi-Annual: $1,614 ($3,228 annually)
    Annual:      $3,200 (no modal charge)

4.6 IMPORTANT: Monthly payments incur a $9.33/month modal
    charge. Paying annually is recommended to minimize cost.

    cannot perform 2 of 6 activities of daily living (ADLs).
    This benefit is subject to per-diem IRS limits. No charge.
    See Rider ADB-CI for full ADL definitions.

6.5 ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT - CRITICAL ILLNESS: Lump sum
    of 25% of face amount ($250,000) upon first diagnosis of
    specified critical illness. Covered conditions include:
    heart attack, stroke, major organ failure, ALS, and
    end-stage renal disease. See Rider ADB-CRIT.
    Monthly rider charge: $4.50.

ARTICLE 7 - LOANS AND WITHDRAWALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7.1 LOAN AVAILABILITY: Loans are available but will permanently
    void the No-Lapse Guarantee. Owner should not take loans
    while depending on NLG for coverage certainty.

7.2 POLICY LOAN TERMS (if taken, NLG voided):
    Maximum: 90% of surrender value.
    Loan rate: 5.5% annually.
    Loaned amounts earn: Fixed account minimum rate (2.25%).

7.3 PARTIAL WITHDRAWALS: Available after year 1.
    Withdrawals during years 1-10 permanently void NLG.
    Withdrawals after year 10 reduce death benefit but do
    not affect NLG status if cumulative test remains current.
    Minimum withdrawal: $500.

7.4 OWNER ADVISORY: Given the protection-first design of this
    policy, Guardian Shield strongly recommends consulting your
    agent before taking any loan or withdrawal.

ARTICLE 9 - REINSTATEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9.1 A lapsed policy may be reinstated within 5 years of lapse
    by paying all overdue NLG premiums with interest at 6%,
    and providing satisfactory evidence of insurability.

9.2 NLG REINSTATEMENT: Reinstated policies restart the NLG
    cumulative test from the reinstatement date. The
    contestability and suicide periods also restart.

9.3 SIMPLIFIED REINSTATEMENT: Within 60 days of lapse, no
    evidence of insurability is required. Payment of overdue
    premium is sufficient.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARTICLE 10 - EXCLUSIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10.1 SUICIDE: Benefits limited to return of premium (less
     withdrawals) if death occurs by suicide within 2 years of
     issue or reinstatement.

10.2 CONTESTABILITY: 2 years from issue or reinstatement.

10.3 AVIATION: EXCLUDED if insured is pilot or crew member of
     non-commercial aircraft. Passenger travel on commercial
     scheduled airlines is fully covered.

10.4 HAZARDOUS OCCUPATION: EXCLUDED if insured's death arises
     from occupation as: underground miner, offshore oil platform
     worker, explosive demolition worker, or similar Category IV
     high-risk occupation as defined in our underwriting manual.
     If insured changes to a Category IV occupation, owner must
     notify Guardian Shield within 30 days.
