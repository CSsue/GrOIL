ARTICLE 1 - KEY DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1.1 "Jumbo Policy" - A life insurance policy with a face
    amount requiring formal reinsurance due to the insurer's
    retention limits. Jumbo threshold at UPIC: $10,000,000.

1.2 "Super Preferred Plus" - Ultralife's proprietary highest
    underwriting classification, reserved for individuals who
    meet stricter criteria than standard Preferred Plus:
    no family history of cancer or cardiac disease before age
    70, BMI 18-26, cholesterol under 175, no medications
    except preventatives, executive physical within 12 months.

1.3 "Reinsurance" - An arrangement by which a portion of the
    mortality risk is transferred to one or more reinsurers.
    The policy owner's rights are unaffected by reinsurance.

1.4 "Dynasty Trust" - A long-duration irrevocable trust
    designed to hold assets across multiple generations while
    minimizing estate and generation-skipping transfer taxes.

1.5 "Private Placement" - Not applicable; this is a registered
    product. See Article 12 for distinction from PPLI.

1.6 "Indexed Strategy" - The method of crediting interest
    based on external index performance.

ARTICLE 3 - HOW INDEX CREDITING WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3.1 STANDARD ANNUAL FORMULA:
    Credit = MAX(0%, MIN(Cap, Index% Change))

3.2 UNCAPPED ACCOUNT:
    Credit = MAX(0%, 65% × Index% Change)

3.3 TWO-YEAR SUM ACCOUNT:
    Year 1 sub-credit = MAX(0%, MIN(11%, Index% Change Yr1))
    Year 2 sub-credit = MAX(0%, MIN(11%, Index% Change Yr2))
    Total credit applied at Year 2 = Sum of sub-credits
    (maximum 22% cumulative over 2 years)
    Note: Two-year sum differs from two-year point-to-point:
    each year's gain is independently measured and capped,
    then summed, rather than measuring the cumulative gain
    at period end.

3.4 GOLDMAN SACHS MOTIF INDEX: A rules-based index designed
    to track 5 investment themes (Technology Disruption,
    Healthcare Innovation, Clean Energy, Consumer Resilience,
    and Financial Infrastructure) with equal weight and
    quarterly rebalancing. Embedded index fee of ~0.65%/year
    reflected in the higher cap. Index rules are published
    and available from Goldman Sachs Asset Management.

3.5 LARGE ACCOUNT VALUE IMPACT: At the scale of this policy,
    even a 1% index credit represents $120,000+ of credited
    interest on a $12M account value. Premium of $1,200,000/
    year compounding at 6% average credit would produce
    an estimated account value of $22M-$28M after 15 years.

ARTICLE 6 - DEATH BENEFIT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6.1 OPTION 2 INCREASING: Death Benefit = $50,000,000 +
    Account Value. As account value reaches $20M, total
    death benefit = $70,000,000. This exceeds many competing
    UHNW policies by maintaining the full face plus cash value.

6.2 ESTATE TAX PLANNING: For a 2024 federal estate tax
    exemption of $13.61M (per individual), the Ashworth
    estate faces potential estate tax exposure. The Dynasty
    Trust structure with ILIT provisions is intended to
    keep policy proceeds outside the taxable estate.

6.3 GST TAX: Generation-Skipping Transfer (GST) tax
    implications should be reviewed with estate counsel.
    Ultralife provides no tax guidance.

6.4 ACCELERATED BENEFITS: Terminal illness acceleration
    up to 50% of total death benefit. Maximum per benefit:
    $25,000,000. Medical Director review required.

6.5 REINSURANCE DISCLOSURE: Reinsurers share in the death
    benefit payment obligation proportionally to their
    assumed risk. UPIC remains the primary contract obligor;
    all claims are paid by UPIC regardless of reinsurer
    solvency. Reinsurers have no contractual relationship
    with the policy owner.

ARTICLE 11 - PRIVATE PLACEMENT vs. REGISTERED PRODUCT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11.1 This policy is a REGISTERED indexed universal life product.
     It is NOT a Private Placement Life Insurance (PPLI) policy.
     Index accounts are general account products; they are not
     separate account investment products subject to securities
     registration. Policy owners do not choose investment
     managers or separately managed accounts.

11.2 For clients seeking PPLI structures (with separately
     managed investment accounts), please inquire about UPIC's
     PPLI division product suite.
