SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Death Benefit" means $150,000, payable upon the
    insured's death while this policy is in force.

4.2 "Simplified Issue" means underwriting based solely on
    responses to a health questionnaire, without a
    physical examination, blood draw, or urinalysis.

4.3 "Pre-Existing Condition" means any illness, disease,
    disorder, or physical condition for which the insured
    received medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment in the
    24 months preceding the issue date of this policy.

4.4 "Graded Benefit Period" means the first two policy years,
    during which a reduced benefit applies as described
    in Section 6.

4.5 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period following each
    monthly premium due date.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: UNDERWRITING BASIS AND REPRESENTATIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 This policy was issued in reliance upon the accuracy
    of the insured's responses to the health questionnaire
    included in the application dated August 14, 2024.

5.2 No medical examination was required. However, SwiftCover
    may access the MIB Group database and prescription drug
    history databases to verify application responses.

5.3 If any material misrepresentation is discovered within
    the contestability period, this policy may be voided
    and only premiums paid will be refunded.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: GRADED DEATH BENEFIT (YEARS 1–2)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 During the Graded Benefit Period (policy years 1 and 2),
    the death benefit payable is as follows:

    Death in Policy Year 1: 30% of the face amount ($45,000)
                            plus a refund of premiums paid

    Death in Policy Year 2: 70% of the face amount ($105,000)

6.2 After the Graded Benefit Period (from the start of
    policy year 3), the full death benefit of $150,000
    is payable.

6.3 The graded benefit does not apply if death is the result
    of an accidental injury as defined in Section 9.3.
    Accidental death pays the full $150,000 in all years.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: DEATH BENEFIT — FULL BENEFIT PERIOD
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 From policy year 3 through expiry, we will pay $150,000
    to the named beneficiary if:
    (a) The insured dies while this policy is in force,
    (b) Death occurs before August 14, 2034, and
    (c) We receive satisfactory proof of death.

7.2 The death benefit is paid in a lump sum within 30 days
    of claim approval.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 Premiums are due on the 14th of each month.

8.2 A grace period of 31 days applies after each missed
    premium due date.

8.3 Non-payment beyond the grace period causes lapse.
    No reinstatement right is available under this policy.
    The insured must re-apply for a new policy.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide, whether
    sane or insane, within two years of the issue date, our
    liability is limited to a refund of premiums paid.
    After two years, the full death benefit is payable.

9.2 PRE-EXISTING CONDITION EXCLUSION: If the insured dies
    within the first two policy years and the cause of
    death is directly related to a Pre-Existing Condition
    that was not disclosed on the application, no benefit
    is payable beyond the graded benefit in Section 6.

9.3 ACCIDENTAL DEATH EXCEPTION: Notwithstanding Sections
    6 and 9.2, the full face amount of $150,000 is payable
    at any time if the insured's death results solely and
    directly from an accidental bodily injury that:
    (a) Occurs within 90 days of the accident,
    (b) Is independent of illness or disease,
    (c) Is not self-inflicted, and
    (d) Is not the result of alcohol or drug impairment.

9.4 DRUG AND ALCOHOL EXCLUSION: No benefit (including
    accidental) is payable if death results directly from
    voluntary intoxication by alcohol, non-prescribed
    drugs, or illegal substances.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
