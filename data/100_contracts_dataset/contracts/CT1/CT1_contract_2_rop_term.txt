SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Death Benefit" means the face amount payable upon the
    insured's death while this policy is in force.

4.2 "Return-of-Premium Benefit" (ROP) means the total of all
    base premiums paid to us under this policy, returned to
    the owner if the insured survives to the policy maturity
    date.

4.3 "Wellness Credit" means a reduction in annual premium
    earned by satisfying qualifying health benchmarks under
    the BrightPath Vitality Program.

4.4 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period after each premium
    due date during which this policy remains in force.

4.5 "Policy Maturity Date" means February 1, 2049.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 We will pay the death benefit to the named beneficiary upon:
    (a) Receipt of satisfactory proof of the insured's death,
    (b) Death occurring while this policy is in force, and
    (c) Death occurring before the policy maturity date.

5.2 Payment shall be made in a single lump sum within 30 days
    of approval of a complete claim submission.

5.3 If the insured dies during the grace period before the
    overdue premium is paid, we will deduct any unpaid premium
    from the death benefit before payment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: RETURN-OF-PREMIUM BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 If the insured is living on the policy maturity date and
    this policy has not lapsed, we will pay the owner a
    return-of-premium benefit equal to the sum of all base
    premiums actually paid under this policy.

6.2 The ROP benefit does not include:
    (a) Premiums for optional riders
    (b) Reinstatement fees
    (c) Any Wellness Credits received

6.3 Payment of the ROP benefit terminates this policy.

6.4 The ROP benefit is not payable if the policy has been
    assigned as collateral and the assignee has not released
    the assignment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: BRIGHTPATH VITALITY WELLNESS PROGRAM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 The owner may enroll in the BrightPath Vitality Program
    at no additional cost.

7.2 Wellness Credits are calculated annually on each policy
    anniversary based on the insured's participation in
    qualifying activities, which include:
    (a) Annual preventive health examination — up to $120/yr
    (b) Verified fitness activity (150+ min/week) — up to
        $96/yr
    (c) Tobacco-free certification — up to $60/yr

7.3 Maximum annual Wellness Credit shall not exceed $276 or
    20% of the annual base premium, whichever is less.

7.4 Wellness Credits are applied as a premium offset against
    the following policy year's premiums. Credits do not
    accumulate cash value and are forfeited if not earned.

7.5 BrightPath reserves the right to modify qualifying
    activities with 90 days' written notice to the owner.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 Premiums are due on the 1st of each month.

8.2 A grace period of 31 days follows each due date.
    Coverage continues uninterrupted during the grace period.

8.3 Failure to pay by the end of the grace period causes the
    policy to lapse, terminating all benefits including the
    accrued return-of-premium benefit.

8.4 Reinstatement is available within 3 years of lapse upon:
    (a) Evidence of insurability satisfactory to us,
    (b) Payment of all overdue premiums with 6% annual interest,
    (c) Payment of any applicable reinstatement fee.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide, whether
    sane or insane, within two years of the issue date, our
    total liability is limited to a refund of base premiums
    paid, without interest.

9.2 AVIATION EXCLUSION: No death benefit is payable if death
    results from the insured acting as a pilot, co-pilot, or
    crew member of any non-commercial aircraft, unless the
    Aviation Rider is attached to this policy.

9.3 No other exclusions apply.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 The owner may convert all or part of the death benefit
     to any permanent life insurance plan then offered by
     BrightPath, without evidence of insurability, provided:
     (a) Conversion is requested at least 5 years before the
         policy maturity date, and
     (b) The insured has not yet reached age 60.

10.2 Minimum convertible amount is $100,000.

10.3 Upon partial conversion, premiums under this policy will
     be proportionally reduced and the ROP benefit will be
     adjusted to reflect premiums paid on the remaining
     term coverage only.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 11: CLAIMS PROCEDURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

11.1 To file a death claim, the beneficiary must submit:
     (a) BrightPath claim form (Form BP-CLM-01)
     (b) Certified copy of the death certificate
     (c) Original policy document, or a lost-policy affidavit

11.2 To claim the ROP benefit, the owner must submit:
     (a) BrightPath maturity claim form (Form BP-MAT-01)
     (b) Photo identification

11.3 Submit all claims to:
     BrightPath Life Insurance Corporation
     Policyholder Services — Claims
     88 Commerce Drive, Suite 400
     Columbus, OH 43215

11.4 We will pay valid claims within 30 days of receiving all
     required documentation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 12: GENERAL PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

12.1 INCONTESTABILITY: After this policy has been in force
     during the insured's lifetime for two years from the
     issue date, we may not contest it except for non-payment
     of premium.

12.2 MISSTATEMENT OF AGE OR SEX: If age or sex is misstated,
     the death benefit will be adjusted to the amount the
     premiums paid would have purchased at the correct age
     and sex.

12.3 ENTIRE CONTRACT: This policy, together with the
     application (attached hereto), constitutes the entire
     contract. No agent may modify this policy orally.

12.4 ASSIGNMENT: Absolute or collateral assignments are
     permitted. Written notice to our home office is required
     before an assignment is binding on us.
