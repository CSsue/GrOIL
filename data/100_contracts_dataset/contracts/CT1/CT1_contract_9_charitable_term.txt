───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Personal Tranche" means the $800,000 death benefit
    payable to the personal beneficiary as designated above.

4.2 "Charitable Tranche" means the $400,000 death benefit
    payable to the qualifying charitable organization
    designated above.

4.3 "Qualifying Charity" means an organization exempt from
    federal income tax under IRC § 501(c)(3) at the time
    a claim is filed.

4.4 "Charitable Substitution Right" means the owner's right
    to change the charitable tranche beneficiary to any
    Qualifying Charity.

4.5 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period following each
    missed monthly due date.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: DEATH BENEFIT — SPLIT PAYMENT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 Upon the insured's death while this policy is in force
    before September 2, 2044, we will pay:
    (a) The Personal Tranche to the designated personal
        beneficiary, and
    (b) The Charitable Tranche to the designated charitable
        beneficiary.

5.2 Both tranches are paid simultaneously within 30 days of
    claim approval.

5.3 If the designated Qualifying Charity has lost its
    IRC § 501(c)(3) status at the time of claim, the
    Charitable Tranche will be paid to the contingent
    charitable beneficiary. If the contingent beneficiary
    also does not qualify, we will hold the Charitable Tranche
    pending the owner's (or estate's) direction for up to
    180 days, then distribute to the personal tranche
    contingent beneficiary.

5.4 We make no representations regarding the tax deductibility
    of premiums or the charitable nature of the benefit for
    any tax purpose.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: CHARITABLE SUBSTITUTION RIGHT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 The owner may change the charitable tranche beneficiary
    to any Qualifying Charity at any time without the consent
    of the current charitable beneficiary.

6.2 A charitable substitution request must include:
    (a) Written request signed by the owner
    (b) EIN and evidence of IRC § 501(c)(3) status of the
        proposed charitable beneficiary

6.3 Changes are effective upon receipt at our home office
    and are not retroactive.

6.4 The personal tranche beneficiary has no right to
    direct, restrict, or otherwise affect the charitable
    tranche designation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: PREMIUM CREDIT FOR CHARITABLE DESIGNATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 Philanthropy Life recognizes the altruistic nature of
    the Charitable Legacy Plan structure. Accordingly, the
    monthly premium reflects a 5% Charitable Designation
    Credit applied to the total premium. The base premium
    without this credit would be $562.50/month.

7.2 The Charitable Designation Credit is maintained for
    as long as this policy remains in force with a
    Qualifying Charity as charitable tranche beneficiary.

7.3 If the owner removes the charitable tranche beneficiary
    and designates a non-charitable beneficiary, the premium
    will be recalculated at the standard rate of $562.50/month
    effective the following policy anniversary.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 Monthly premiums are due on the 2nd of each month.

8.2 A 31-day grace period follows each missed due date.

8.3 Non-payment after the grace period causes lapse of the
    entire policy, including both tranches.

8.4 Reinstatement is available within 3 years of lapse upon
    evidence of insurability and payment of all overdue
    premiums with 6% annual interest.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide within
    two years of the issue date, our liability is limited
    to a refund of premiums paid, allocated proportionally
    between the personal and charitable tranches.

9.2 No other exclusions apply.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 The owner may convert all or a portion of this policy
     to a permanent life insurance plan offered by
     Philanthropy Life, without evidence of insurability,
     provided conversion occurs before the earlier of:
     (a) The 18th policy anniversary, or
     (b) The insured's attained age 70.

10.2 Upon conversion, the split-beneficiary structure may
     be maintained or restructured at the owner's election.

10.3 The Charitable Designation Credit will apply to the
     converted policy's premium if the charitable tranche
     designation is maintained.
