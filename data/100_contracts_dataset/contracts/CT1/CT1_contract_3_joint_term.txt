───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "First-to-Die Event" means the death of whichever insured
    person dies first while this policy is in force.

4.2 "Surviving Insured" means the insured person who remains
    living after the first-to-die event.

4.3 "Continuation Option" means the right of the surviving
    insured to obtain a new individual policy as described
    in Section 8.

4.4 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period following a missed
    premium due date during which coverage remains active.

4.5 "Joint Age" means the blended age used by Meridian Mutual
    to price joint policies, calculated at policy issuance.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: DEATH BENEFIT — FIRST-TO-DIE TRIGGER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 We will pay the death benefit upon the first-to-die event,
    provided:
    (a) This policy is in force on the date of death, and
    (b) The death occurs before the policy expiry date, and
    (c) We receive satisfactory proof of death.

5.2 After payment of the death benefit, this policy terminates
    with respect to both insureds, subject to Section 8
    (Continuation Option).

5.3 If both insureds die simultaneously or within 24 hours of
    each other, we will treat the older insured as having died
    first, and the death benefit will be paid to the
    contingent beneficiary.

5.4 The death benefit will be paid in a lump sum within
    30 days of claim approval.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 A single joint premium is due monthly on the 10th.

6.2 A grace period of 31 days follows each missed due date.

6.3 If premiums remain unpaid after the grace period, the
    policy lapses and all coverage terminates.

6.4 Either owner may pay the premium. Payment by one joint
    owner satisfies the obligation of both.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If either insured commits suicide, whether
    sane or insane, within two years of the issue date, our
    liability is limited to a refund of premiums paid,
    and no death benefit shall be payable.

7.2 WAR EXCLUSION: No death benefit is payable if the
    first-to-die event results directly or indirectly from
    declared or undeclared war, armed conflict, or the
    insured's participation in a military operation, unless
    the insured was serving as a civilian non-combatant.

7.3 No other exclusions apply.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: CONTINUATION OPTION (SURVIVING INSURED)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 Within 90 days of the first-to-die event, the surviving
    insured may elect to purchase a new individual level term
    life insurance policy from Meridian Mutual without
    providing evidence of insurability, subject to:
    (a) The new policy face amount may not exceed $1,000,000,
    (b) The new policy term may not exceed the remaining years
        on this policy at the time of the first-to-die event,
    (c) The new premium will be based on the surviving
        insured's attained age and then-current rate tables.

8.2 The Continuation Option expires if not exercised within
    the 90-day window.

8.3 This option is not available if the surviving insured
    is age 65 or older at the time of the first-to-die event.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: OWNERSHIP AND DIVORCE PROVISION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 Either joint owner may exercise ownership rights, including
    beneficiary changes, independently of the other.

9.2 Upon legal dissolution of the marriage or domestic
    partnership of the two insureds, either insured may
    request severance of ownership. We will, upon receipt of
    a court decree or written mutual consent:
    (a) Split this policy into two individual policies of
        $500,000 each, or
    (b) Assign sole ownership and continued coverage on
        the full amount to one insured, with the other's
        interest released.

9.3 Severance requests must be made before the earlier of
    the policy expiry or the first-to-die event.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 Prior to the first-to-die event, the owners may convert
     this joint policy to two individual permanent life
     insurance policies without evidence of insurability,
     provided conversion occurs before the earlier of:
     (a) The 15th policy anniversary, or
     (b) The younger insured's 60th birthday.

10.2 Each converted policy may cover up to 50% of the current
     death benefit.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 11: CLAIMS PROCEDURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

11.1 To file a claim, submit to Meridian Mutual:
     (a) Completed claim form (Form MM-CLM-10)
     (b) Certified death certificate of the deceased insured
     (c) Original policy document or lost-policy affidavit

11.2 Send to:
     Meridian Mutual Life — Claims Administration
     One Meridian Plaza, P.O. Box 8800
     Minneapolis, MN 55402

11.3 Valid claims are paid within 30 days of complete submission.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 12: GENERAL PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

12.1 INCONTESTABILITY: We may not contest this policy after
     it has been in force for two years from the issue date
     during the lifetime of either insured, except for
     non-payment of premium.

12.2 MISSTATEMENT OF AGE OR SEX: If age or sex of either
     insured is misstated, the benefit will be adjusted to
     what the premium would have purchased at the correct
     age and sex, applied to the blended joint age.

12.3 ENTIRE CONTRACT: This policy and application form
     constitute the entire agreement.

12.4 GOVERNING LAW: This policy is governed by the laws of
     the State of Minnesota.
