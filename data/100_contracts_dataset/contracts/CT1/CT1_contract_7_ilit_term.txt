SECTION 3: BENEFICIARY DESIGNATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Sole Beneficiary: The Harrington 2024 Irrevocable Life Insurance
                  Trust — 100%
                  (Proceeds distributed per trust instrument
                   to trust beneficiaries)

Note: As the trust is both owner and beneficiary, proceeds
pass outside the insured's taxable estate subject to IRS
rules and the terms of the trust instrument. Cornerstone
Heritage makes no representations regarding tax treatment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Death Benefit" means the $5,000,000 payable to the
    trust beneficiary upon the insured's death in force.

4.2 "Trustee" means the person named as trustee under the
    Harrington 2024 ILIT, and any duly appointed successor.

4.3 "Crummey Contribution" means an annual gift made by the
    grantor to the trust for the purpose of funding premium
    payments, consistent with IRC § 2503(c) withdrawal rights.

4.4 "Grace Period" means the 60-day period following a
    missed annual premium due date, during which coverage
    remains in force. (Extended grace reflecting annual
    payment mode and trust funding cycle.)

4.5 "Successor Trustee" means any individual or institution
    who assumes trusteeship upon the resignation, incapacity,
    or death of the current trustee.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 We will pay the death benefit to the named beneficiary
    (the trust) if:
    (a) The insured dies while this policy is in force,
    (b) Death occurs before July 22, 2054, and
    (c) We receive satisfactory proof of death.

5.2 We will accept instructions from the trustee (or
    successor trustee) regarding payment of the death
    benefit, including election of a settlement option.

5.3 We are not responsible for ensuring that proceeds are
    distributed by the trustee in accordance with the
    trust instrument. Our only obligation is to pay the
    named beneficiary of record.

5.4 Payment is made within 30 days of claim approval.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: TRUSTEE AUTHORITY AND SUCCESSION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 We will deal exclusively with the trustee of record
    as the policy owner, subject to receipt of written
    evidence of trustee appointment.

6.2 Upon a change of trustee, the successor trustee must
    provide us with:
    (a) A certified copy of the trust instrument confirming
        the succession mechanism, and
    (b) A written letter of acceptance from the successor.

6.3 We will not honor policy transactions (beneficiary
    changes, conversions, assignments) from any party
    other than the trustee of record.

6.4 We assume no liability for acts taken in good faith
    in reliance on trustee instructions.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND EXTENDED GRACE PERIOD
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 The annual premium is due July 22 of each policy year.

7.2 An extended grace period of 60 days applies, reflecting
    the annual payment mode and ILIT funding timelines.
    Coverage remains fully in force during the grace period.

7.3 If the premium is not received within the grace period,
    the policy lapses with no cash or surrender value.

7.4 Reinstatement is available within 5 years of lapse upon:
    (a) Evidence of insurability satisfactory to us,
    (b) Payment of all overdue premiums with 6% interest,
    (c) Written authorization from the trustee.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide, sane or
    insane, within two years of the issue date, our liability
    is limited to a refund of premiums paid to the trust.

8.2 No other exclusions apply to this policy.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: IRREVOCABILITY AND ASSIGNMENT RESTRICTIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 This policy is owned by an irrevocable trust. The grantor
    (the insured) has no ownership rights in this policy
    and may not:
    (a) Surrender or cancel this policy,
    (b) Change the beneficiary designation,
    (c) Borrow against or assign this policy, or
    (d) Exercise any other ownership rights.

9.2 The trustee may assign this policy solely:
    (a) To a successor trustee of the same trust, or
    (b) As directed by a court of competent jurisdiction.

9.3 No collateral assignment of this policy is permitted
    without a court order.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 The trustee may convert this policy to a permanent
     life insurance plan offered by Cornerstone Heritage
     without evidence of insurability, provided:
     (a) The insured's attained age does not exceed 75,
     (b) Conversion is requested before the 25th anniversary.

10.2 The converted policy will be owned by the trust.

10.3 The trustee must provide evidence of continued trust
     existence and authority prior to conversion.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 11: CLAIMS PROCEDURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

11.1 The trustee must submit the following:
     (a) Completed claim form (Form CH-CLM-TR)
     (b) Certified copy of the death certificate
     (c) Certified copy of trust instrument (relevant pages)
     (d) Evidence of trustee authority (letters of trust)
     (e) Original policy or lost-policy affidavit

11.2 Submit to:
     Cornerstone Heritage Life Insurance Company
     Trust & Estate Claims Division
     One Heritage Square, Hartford, CT 06103

11.3 We will pay valid claims within 30 days of receiving
     all required documentation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 12: GENERAL PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

12.1 INCONTESTABILITY: After two years from the issue date,
     we may not contest this policy based on statements in
     the application, except for non-payment of premium.

12.2 MISSTATEMENT OF AGE OR SEX: Benefits will be adjusted
     to amounts purchasable at the correct age and sex.

12.3 ENTIRE CONTRACT: This policy and the application
     constitute the entire contract. The trust instrument
     is not part of this contract.

12.4 GOVERNING LAW: This policy is governed by the laws
     of the State of Connecticut.
