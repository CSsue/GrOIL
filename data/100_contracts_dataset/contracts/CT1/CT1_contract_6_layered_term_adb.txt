SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Base Policy Death Benefit" means the $300,000 payable
    under Layer 1 of this policy upon the insured's death.

4.2 "Layer 2 Rider" means the additional $300,000 term
    coverage that expires on the 10th policy anniversary.

4.3 "Qualifying Terminal Condition" means a medical condition,
    certified by a licensed physician, that is expected to
    result in the insured's death within 24 months.

4.4 "Qualifying Chronic Condition" means the permanent
    inability to perform at least 2 of 6 Activities of Daily
    Living (ADLs) as certified by a licensed health care
    practitioner, expected to be permanent.

4.5 "Accelerated Benefit" means the advance of a portion of
    the death benefit during the insured's lifetime upon a
    qualifying terminal or chronic condition claim.

4.6 "Reduced Death Benefit" means the remaining death benefit
    after payment of an accelerated benefit, equal to the
    original benefit less any accelerated amount plus a
    discount adjustment.

4.7 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period after each
    monthly premium due date, during which coverage continues.

5.1 We will pay the total death benefit to the named

SECTION 6: ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT RIDER (LIVING BENEFITS)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 TERMINAL CONDITION ACCELERATION:
    If the insured is diagnosed with a Qualifying Terminal
    Condition, the owner may request an acceleration of up
    to 80% of the then-current total death benefit, subject
    to a maximum of $400,000 per claim.

    (a) Only one terminal condition acceleration is permitted
        per policy lifetime.
    (b) A discount factor reflecting the time value of money
        will be applied; the insured will receive less than
        the face amount requested.
    (c) The remaining death benefit payable upon death will
        equal the original benefit less the accelerated
        amount, less the discount adjustment.

6.2 CHRONIC CONDITION ACCELERATION:
    If the insured satisfies the definition of a Qualifying
    Chronic Condition, the owner may request annual
    accelerations of up to 24% of the then-current death
    benefit per calendar year, subject to a $100,000
    per-year maximum.

    (a) Chronic condition accelerations may be made annually
        and renewed each year the condition persists.
    (b) Each acceleration reduces the remaining death benefit
        by the accelerated amount plus the applicable
        discount adjustment.
    (c) Accelerations cease when the remaining death benefit
        falls below $50,000.

6.3 GENERAL ACCELERATION PROVISIONS:
    (a) Acceleration requires written physician certification
        of the qualifying condition submitted on Form
        AP-ABR-CERT.
    (b) We reserve the right to require an independent
        medical examination at our expense.
    (c) Accelerated benefits may affect Medicaid eligibility
        and other government benefit programs. We recommend
        the owner consult a financial advisor before electing
        acceleration.
    (d) This rider is provided at no additional premium and
        is automatically included with this policy.

SECTION 8: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 Total monthly premium of $61.00 is due on the 3rd.

8.2 A grace period of 31 days applies to each missed payment.

8.3 Non-payment beyond the grace period causes lapse and
    terminates all benefits, including the Accelerated
    Benefit Rider.

8.4 Reinstatement is available within 3 years of lapse upon
    evidence of insurability and payment of all overdue
    premiums with 6% annual interest.

SECTION 9: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide within
    two years of the issue date, liability is limited to
    a refund of premiums paid, including rider premiums.

9.2 SELF-INFLICTED INJURY (ACCELERATED BENEFIT): The
    accelerated benefit will not be paid if the qualifying
    condition is the result of intentional self-inflicted
    injury within two years of the issue date.

9.3 No other exclusions apply.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 The base policy (Layer 1) may be converted to any
     permanent plan offered by Atlas Premier without
     evidence of insurability, provided conversion occurs
     before the earlier of:
     (a) The 13th policy anniversary, or
     (b) The insured's attained age 65.

10.2 Prior accelerated benefit payments do not affect the
     conversion amount; the full original face amount of
     $300,000 may be converted regardless of prior
     acceleration.
