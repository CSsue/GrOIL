───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Death Benefit" means the face amount payable upon the
    death of the insured while this policy is in force.

4.2 "Key Person" means the insured individual whose skills,
    relationships, or knowledge are essential to the business
    operations of the policy owner.

4.3 "Annual Premium Due Date" means April 5 of each policy
    year.

4.4 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period following the
    premium due date, during which coverage remains in force.

4.5 "Change in Insurable Interest" means any event that
    causes the owner to no longer have an insurable interest
    in the insured's life, including termination of the
    insured's employment or ownership relationship.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 We will pay the death benefit to the named beneficiary if:
    (a) The insured dies while this policy is in force,
    (b) Death occurs before the policy expiry date, and
    (c) We receive proof of death satisfactory to us.

5.2 The death benefit is paid in a single lump sum within
    30 days of approval of all required claim documents.

5.3 Payment of the death benefit terminates this policy.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: INSURABLE INTEREST AND CHANGE OF OWNERSHIP
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 The owner represents that, at policy issuance, a valid
    business insurable interest exists in the life of the
    insured by reason of the insured's role as a key officer
    or employee of the owner.

6.2 If the insured's employment or key-person relationship
    with the owner terminates for any reason, the owner must
    notify us within 60 days.

6.3 Upon such notification, the owner may:
    (a) Assign this policy to the insured as new owner
        (transfer of ownership), subject to our written
        acknowledgment, or
    (b) Surrender this policy for no cash value.

6.4 We will not pay the death benefit if, at the time of
    death, the owner no longer employs or has an active
    business relationship with the insured and has not
    transferred ownership per Section 6.3(a).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 The annual premium is due on April 5 of each policy year.

7.2 A 31-day grace period follows each annual due date.
    Coverage continues during the grace period.

7.3 Failure to pay by the end of the grace period causes
    the policy to lapse with no cash or return value.

7.4 Reinstatement may be requested within 2 years of lapse
    upon satisfactory evidence of insurability and payment
    of all overdue premiums with 7% annual interest.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide, whether
    sane or insane, within two years of the issue date, our
    liability is limited to a refund of premiums paid, and
    no death benefit shall be due.

8.2 FELONY EXCLUSION: No death benefit is payable if the
    insured's death results directly from the insured's
    commission of or attempt to commit a felony, as finally
    determined by a court of competent jurisdiction.

8.3 FRAUD EXCLUSION: No benefit is payable if the policy
    was obtained through material misrepresentation by
    the policy owner regarding the nature of the insurable
    interest.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: SPLIT-DOLLAR ENDORSEMENT OPTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 The owner may elect, at policy issuance or any policy
    anniversary, to add a Split-Dollar Endorsement to
    this policy. Under the endorsement:
    (a) Premiums may be shared between the owner and the
        insured under a written split-dollar agreement,
    (b) The death benefit may be split between the owner
        (loan repayment tranche) and a personal beneficiary
        designated by the insured.

9.2 The Split-Dollar Endorsement must be requested in writing
    and is subject to our then-current endorsement forms.

9.3 Tax treatment of split-dollar arrangements is governed
    by applicable IRS regulations; we make no representations
    regarding the tax consequences of such arrangements.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 The owner may convert this policy to a permanent life
     insurance plan available from Vanguard Sentinel without
     evidence of insurability, provided:
     (a) The conversion is requested before the policy's
         8th anniversary, and
     (b) The insured's attained age does not exceed 65.

10.2 Upon conversion, the new policy owner may be either
     the business entity or the insured personally, subject
     to execution of the appropriate ownership documents.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 11: CLAIMS PROCEDURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

11.1 The policy owner (or its authorized representative)
     must submit the following to file a claim:
     (a) Completed claim form (Form VS-BIZ-CLM)
     (b) Certified copy of the death certificate
     (c) Evidence of continued insurable interest at time
         of death (e.g., employment records, LLC records)
     (d) Original policy or lost-policy affidavit

11.2 Submit claims to:
     Vanguard Sentinel Life Insurance Company
     Corporate Claims Division
     500 Sentinel Tower, Suite 200
     Cincinnati, OH 45202

11.3 We will pay valid claims within 30 days of receiving
     all required documentation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 12: GENERAL PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

12.1 INCONTESTABILITY: After two years from the issue date,
     we will not contest this policy on the basis of
     statements in the application, except for non-payment
     of premium or lack of insurable interest.

12.2 MISSTATEMENT OF AGE OR SEX: If misstated, the death
     benefit will be adjusted to the amount purchasable at
     the correct age and sex for the premium paid.

12.3 ENTIRE CONTRACT: This policy and the attached application
     constitute the entire agreement between the parties.

12.4 GOVERNING LAW: This policy is governed by the laws of
     the State of Ohio.
