SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Death Benefit" means the $400,000 payable to the named
    beneficiary upon the insured's qualifying death.

4.2 "Portability Conversion" means the issuance of this
    individual policy without evidence of insurability
    following the termination of the insured's participation
    in a ContinuaCare group life insurance plan.

4.3 "Portability Eligibility Period" means the 31-day window
    following a qualifying separation event during which
    the insured was eligible to elect portability coverage.
    This policy was issued within that window.

4.4 "Qualifying Separation Event" means termination of
    employment, reduction in hours below eligibility
    threshold, or retirement — events that caused loss
    of group coverage.

4.5 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period following each
    monthly premium due date.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: PORTABILITY ORIGIN AND UNDERWRITING WAIVER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 This policy was issued under the ContinuaCare Group
    Life Portability Program. Because the insured was
    previously covered under group policy GRP-MC-44200
    and elected portability within the eligible window,
    no physical examination or new health questionnaire
    was required.

5.2 ContinuaCare relied on the insured's prior evidence of
    insurability established at group enrollment. This
    policy is subject to the contestability and
    misrepresentation provisions of Section 12 with
    respect to representations made at the time of
    original group enrollment.

5.3 The face amount of $400,000 represents the maximum
    portability benefit: the lesser of:
    (a) $400,000, or
    (b) 3 times the insured's former group coverage amount.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 We will pay the death benefit to the named beneficiary
    if:
    (a) The insured dies while this policy is in force,
    (b) Death occurs before October 1, 2039, and
    (c) We receive satisfactory proof of death.

6.2 The death benefit is paid within 30 days of claim
    approval in a single lump sum.

6.3 Multiple beneficiaries will be paid in the proportions
    designated above. If a primary beneficiary predeceases
    the insured, their share is paid equally to the
    surviving primary beneficiaries. If no primary
    beneficiaries survive, the contingent beneficiary
    receives the full benefit.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 The monthly premium is due on the 1st of each month.

7.2 A 31-day grace period follows each missed due date.

7.3 Non-payment after the grace period causes lapse.

7.4 Reinstatement is available within 2 years of lapse
    upon:
    (a) Evidence of insurability satisfactory to us,
    (b) Payment of all overdue premiums with 6% interest.

7.5 Premiums for this policy will not increase during
    the policy term, regardless of changes in the insured's
    health or occupation.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: PREMIUM RATE PROTECTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 ContinuaCare guarantees that the premium rate for this
    policy will not increase for the first 5 policy years.

8.2 After the 5th policy anniversary, ContinuaCare reserves
    the right to adjust the premium rate on a class-wide
    basis (not individual basis), with 90 days' written
    notice, subject to regulatory approval.

8.3 Any class-wide rate increase may not exceed 20% of the
    then-current premium in any single adjustment.

8.4 Individual premium adjustments based on the insured's
    personal health changes are not permitted under this
    policy.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide within
    two years of the issue date of this portable policy,
    our liability is limited to a refund of premiums paid
    under this policy. Death by suicide after two years
    is fully covered.

9.2 No other exclusions apply, except that exclusions
    applicable under the former group policy GRP-MC-44200
    that were applicable to the insured at the time of
    portability election are carried forward to this policy.
    A list of any such exclusions is attached as Exhibit B.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 The owner may convert this policy to a permanent
     life insurance plan offered by ContinuaCare without
     evidence of insurability, provided conversion occurs
     before the earlier of:
     (a) The 12th policy anniversary, or
     (b) The insured's attained age 65.

10.2 The convertible amount may not exceed the then-current
     death benefit.

10.3 A conversion credit equal to 50% of the first year's
     premium on the converted policy will be applied to
     offset initial conversion costs.

SECTION 12: GENERAL PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

12.1 INCONTESTABILITY: After two years from the issue date,
     we may not contest this policy, except for non-payment
     of premium. Contestability relates to representations
     made at original group enrollment for portability
     conversions.

12.2 MISSTATEMENT OF AGE OR SEX: Benefits will be adjusted
     to the amount purchasable at the correct age and sex
     for the premium paid.

12.3 ENTIRE CONTRACT: This policy and the portability
     election form constitute the entire agreement.

12.4 GOVERNING LAW: This policy is governed by the laws
