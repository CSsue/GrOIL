SECTION 5: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 "Death Benefit" means the decreasing face amount payable
    upon the insured's death as specified in Section 3.

5.2 "Collateral Assignment" means the assignment of policy
    proceeds to a lending institution as security for a loan,
    up to but not exceeding the outstanding loan balance.

5.3 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period following a missed
    premium due date, during which coverage continues.

5.4 "Policy Anniversary" means each May 20 following the
    issue date.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: DEATH BENEFIT PAYMENT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 We will pay the death benefit if:
    (a) The insured dies while this policy is in force,
    (b) Death occurs before the policy expiry date, and
    (c) Satisfactory proof of death is received by us.

6.2 If a collateral assignment is in effect, we will pay the
    outstanding balance of the assigned loan directly to the
    assignee. Any remaining death benefit will be paid to
    the named beneficiary.

6.3 We will not pay an amount exceeding the death benefit
    in force on the date of death, regardless of the original
    loan amount.

6.4 The death benefit is paid in a lump sum within 30 days
    of claim approval.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 The level monthly premium of $38.00 is due on the 20th
    of each month throughout the policy term.

7.2 A grace period of 31 days follows each missed due date.

7.3 If the premium remains unpaid after the grace period,
    the policy lapses and all coverage ceases.

7.4 Reinstatement is available within 3 years of lapse,
    subject to evidence of insurability and payment of
    all overdue premiums with 6% interest per annum.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: COLLATERAL ASSIGNMENT PROVISIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 A collateral assignment attached at issue or thereafter
    grants the assignee the right to receive proceeds up to
    the outstanding balance of the secured loan.

8.2 The collateral assignee has no right to:
    (a) Change the beneficiary designation,
    (b) Surrender or cancel this policy, or
    (c) Receive more than the outstanding loan balance.

8.3 The owner may request release of the collateral
    assignment at any time by submitting a release form
    signed by the assignee.

8.4 We will not be liable for any act taken in reliance
    on a collateral assignment notice until such notice
    is received at our home office in writing.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide, whether
    sane or insane, within two years of the issue date,
    our liability is limited to a refund of premiums paid.
    Any collateral assignee will receive only the lesser of
    their outstanding loan balance or the refunded premiums.

9.2 No other exclusions apply to this policy.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 The owner may convert the then-current death benefit
     to a permanent life insurance policy offered by
     HomeGuard Life, without evidence of insurability, if:
     (a) Conversion is requested before the 20th anniversary,
     (b) The insured has not yet reached age 65, and
     (c) Any collateral assignee provides written consent.

10.2 The converted policy face amount will equal the
     decreasing benefit amount in force at conversion.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 11: CLAIMS PROCEDURE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

11.1 To file a death claim:
     (a) Complete Form HG-CLM-01
     (b) Submit certified copy of death certificate
     (c) Provide original policy or lost-policy affidavit
     (d) If collateral assigned: provide lender's payoff
         statement dated within 30 days of death

11.2 Send all documents to:
     HomeGuard Life Assurance Company
     Claims Processing Center
     P.O. Box 7700, Milwaukee, WI 53201

11.3 We will pay valid claims within 30 days of receiving
     all required documentation.
