SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 "Death Benefit" means the $300,000 payable upon the
    insured's death while this policy is in force.

4.2 "Table Rating" means the mortality surcharge applied to
    reflect the insured's elevated risk profile, expressed
    as a percentage addition to standard rates.

    Table A = Standard; each Table adds approximately 25%
    to the standard premium. Table D (4) = 100% above
    standard rates.

4.3 "Flat Extra Premium" means an additional per-thousand
    charge applied for a defined period to account for a
    specific identified risk (occupational hazard). The
    flat extra is temporary and expires as stated above.

4.4 "Grace Period" means the 31-day period following each
    missed monthly premium due date.

4.5 "Occupational Hazard Exclusion" means the exclusion
    described in Section 8.2, applicable during the flat
    extra period.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5: DEATH BENEFIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.1 We will pay the death benefit to the named beneficiary
    if:
    (a) The insured dies while this policy is in force,
    (b) Death occurs before November 18, 2034, and
    (c) We receive satisfactory proof of death, and
    (d) The cause of death is not excluded under Section 8.

5.2 The death benefit is paid in a lump sum within 30 days
    of claim approval.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 6: PREMIUM STEP-DOWN UPON FLAT EXTRA EXPIRATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1 The occupational flat extra of $200.00/month will
    automatically expire at the end of policy year 5
    (November 18, 2029).

6.2 No notification or action by the owner is required.
    The monthly premium will automatically reduce to
    $268.13/month beginning with the premium due on
    December 18, 2029.

6.3 The owner may, at any time during the flat extra period,
    request a re-evaluation of the occupational hazard
    surcharge if the insured changes occupations. Written
    evidence of a change in occupation must be submitted.
    Re-evaluation is at our sole discretion.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 7: PREMIUM PAYMENTS AND LAPSE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1 The monthly premium (as specified in Section 2) is due
    on the 18th of each month.

7.2 A grace period of 31 days follows each missed due date.

7.3 Non-payment after the grace period causes lapse.

7.4 Reinstatement is available within 2 years of lapse upon:
    (a) Evidence of insurability satisfactory to us,
    (b) Payment of all overdue premiums with 7% interest,
    (c) Acceptance of any revised rating terms.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8.1 SUICIDE CLAUSE: If the insured dies by suicide within
    two years of the issue date, our liability is limited
    to a refund of premiums paid.

8.2 OCCUPATIONAL HAZARD EXCLUSION (Years 1–5 Only):
    During policy years 1 through 5, no death benefit
    is payable if death results directly from the insured's
    performance of commercial diving operations, including:
    (a) Saturation, mixed gas, or surface-supplied diving,
    (b) Underwater construction, inspection, or salvage,
    (c) Any diving activity in an occupational capacity.
    In lieu of the death benefit, premiums paid will be
    refunded with 4% annual interest.

    After policy year 5, this occupational exclusion
    terminates and death from any commercial diving
    activity is fully covered.

8.3 DIABETES COMPLICATION EXCLUSION: No death benefit is
    payable if the insured fails to maintain insulin or
    medication therapy as prescribed and death results
    directly from uncontrolled diabetic ketoacidosis or
    hyperosmolar hyperglycemic crisis within the first
    two policy years.

8.4 No other exclusions apply.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 9: RATING REVIEW PROVISION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

9.1 The owner may request a formal review of the Table D
    rating on or after the 3rd policy anniversary.

9.2 A rating review requires:
    (a) Written request from the policy owner
    (b) Current attending physician's statement
    (c) HbA1c laboratory results dated within 90 days
    (d) Payment of a $150.00 review fee

9.3 If the review supports a reduction in mortality risk,
    we may reduce the table rating and adjust the premium
    accordingly on a prospective basis.

9.4 No premium refund is available for periods prior to
    the effective date of any rating improvement.

9.5 We may decline to conduct a rating review more than
    once per policy year.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 10: NO CONVERSION PRIVILEGE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

10.1 This substandard-rated policy does not include a
     conversion privilege to a permanent policy on a
     guaranteed basis.

10.2 The insured may apply for a new individual policy at
     the expiry of this term, subject to full underwriting
     at the time of application.
