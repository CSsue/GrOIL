POLICY PROVISIONS – PART I  
Entire Contract  
This policy, together with the application and any attached riders or endorsements, constitutes 
the entire contract between the Policy Owner and the Company. No agent or representative 
may alter or waive any provision of this policy unless expressly authorized in writing by an 
officer of the Company.  
Consideration  
This policy is issued in consideration of the statements made in the application and the 
payment of the first premium. Continued coverage is subject to the timely payment of all 
subsequent premiums due.  
Effective Date  
The Effective Date of this policy is shown on the Schedule of Benefits. Coverage begins at 
12:01 A.M. Standard Time at the Owner’s address on that date.  
Ownership  
The Policy Owner possesses all contractual rights, including the right to change 
beneficiaries, assign the policy, and request cancellation. If no Owner is named, the 
Insured shall be deemed the Owner.  
Right to Examine Policy  
The Owner has the right to examine this policy for a period of 20 days from receipt. If not 
satisfied, the policy may be returned to the Company for a full refund of any premium paid. 
Upon return, the policy shall be void from inception and treated as if never issued.  
Contract Modification  
No change or waiver of any provision in this policy shall be valid unless made in writing and 
signed by an authorized officer of the Company. No agent or broker is authorized to modify

POLICY PROVISIONS – PART II  
Incontestability  
Except for nonpayment of premium, this policy shall be incontestable after it has been in 
force during the lifetime of the Insured for two years from the Effective Date. Any 
misrepresentations or omissions that materially affect the acceptance of risk may render the 
policy void if discovered within this period.  
Suicide Exclusion  
If the Insured dies by suicide, while sane or insane, within two years from the Effective 
Date, the Company’s liability shall be limited to a refund of premiums paid, without 
interest, less any indebtedness.  
Misstatement of Age or Gender  
If the age or gender of the Insured has been misstated, the amount of insurance shall be 
adjusted to the amount that the premium paid would have purchased at the correct age or 
gender.  
Grace Period  
A grace period of 31 days will be granted for the payment of each premium after the first. 
During the grace period, the policy remains in force. If the Insured dies during the grace 
period, any unpaid premium due will be deducted from the Death Benefit.  
Lapse and Termination  
If a premium is not paid before the end of the grace period, this policy shall lapse as of the 
due date of the unpaid premium, and coverage shall terminate with no further benefits 
payable, except as provided under the Reinstatement provision.

Reinstatement  
A lapsed policy may be reinstated within five (5) years of lapse by providing evidence of 
insurability satisfactory to the Company and payment of all past due premiums with interest, 
as determined by the Company at the time of reinstatement. 
 
 
PREMIUMS AND PAYMENT TERMS  
Premium Amount and Mode  
The premium amount and payment mode are shown in the Schedule of Benefits. Premiums 
are payable in advance and may be paid on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual 
basis, as elected by the Policy Owner and approved by the Company.  
Premium Due Dates  
The first premium is due on the Effective Date of the policy. Subsequent premiums are 
due on each premium due date according to the payment mode selected.  
Change in Premium Mode  
The Policy Owner may request a change in the frequency or method of premium payment 
by written notice to the Company. Such change shall become effective upon approval by 
the Company.  
Non-Payment of Premium  
Failure to pay the premium by the end of the grace period will cause the policy to lapse, and 
coverage will terminate without value except as provided under the Reinstatement provision.  
Automatic Premium Loan (APL)  
This policy does not include an Automatic Premium Loan provision unless endorsed or 
added by rider. If APL is in force, unpaid premiums due at the end of the grace period will be 
paid automatically by a policy loan, provided there is sufficient loan value available.

DEATH BENEFIT PROVISIONS  
Death Benefit Payment  
Upon receipt of due proof of the Insured’s death while this policy is in force, the Company will 
pay the Death Benefit to the designated Beneficiary, subject to the terms of this policy. 
Payment will be made in a lump sum unless an alternative settlement option is elected and 
approved by the Company.  
Proof of Death  
Proof of the Insured’s death must be provided in a form satisfactory to the Company. 
Acceptable proof includes a certified copy of the death certificate and any other 
documentation the Company may reasonably require.  
Time of Payment  
The Company will pay the Death Benefit promptly upon receipt of satisfactory proof of death. 
Interest, as required by applicable law, will accrue from the date of death until the date of 
payment.  
Beneficiary Designation  
The Beneficiary is the person or entity named in the application or most recent written 
designation on file with the Company. If no Beneficiary survives the Insured, the Death 
Benefit will be paid to the Policy Owner, if living, or to the Owner’s estate.

Change of Beneficiary 
 The Policy Owner may change the Beneficiary designation at any time by submitting a 
written request to the Company. The change shall take effect as of the date the request is 
signed, regardless of whether the Insured is living at the time of the Company’s receipt. 
 
 
 
POLICYOWNER RIGHTS  
Right to Change Beneficiary  
The Policyowner may change the Beneficiary at any time during the Insured’s lifetime by 
submitting a written request to the Company. The change will take effect as of the date the 
request is signed, regardless of whether the Insured is living at the time of the Company’s 
receipt of the request.  
Right to Assign Policy  
The Policyowner may assign this policy or any interest in it by submitting a written 
assignment acceptable to the Company. The Company shall not be responsible for the 
validity or tax consequences of any assignment. The rights of the Owner and Beneficiary will 
be subject to the terms of the assignment.  
Right to Cancel Policy 
 The Policyowner may cancel this policy at any time by submitting a written request. Upon 
cancellation, insurance coverage will terminate and no further benefits will be payable. If the 
policy has any unearned premiums, they will be refunded as specified under applicable law.

OPTIONAL RIDERS  
Accelerated Death Benefit Rider  
This rider provides for the early payment of a portion of the Death Benefit if the Insured is 
diagnosed with a terminal illness with a life expectancy of 12 months or less, as certified by a 
licensed physician. The amount payable shall not exceed 50% of the policy’s Face Amount 
and is subject to a maximum limit determined by the Company.  
Conditions for Acceleration 
 To receive an accelerated benefit, the Policyowner must submit a written request and 
provide medical documentation acceptable to the Company. The Company reserves the right 
to obtain a second medical opinion at its own expense.  
Effect on Policy Values 
 Upon payment of an accelerated benefit, the Death Benefit will be reduced by the amount 
paid, including any applicable administrative charges and interest. Any outstanding loans or 
liens on the policy will also be deducted.

Exclusions  
No accelerated benefit will be paid if the terminal illness is the result of intentional 
self-inflicted injury, the participation in a felony, or war-related activities.  
Right to Revoke  
The Policyowner may revoke the request for accelerated benefits at any time before the 
benefit is paid, by providing written notice to the Company.
